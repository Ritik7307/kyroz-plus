KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN RARA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (60%)
G-204 ROASTED RUST (40%)

MEAT:
2 Pieces Chicken (Pre-cooked) + 60 g Keema (Pre-cooked)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame High at the beginning.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Ghee

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Water or Stock to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1:
Heat the oil and ghee in the pan.
Add a small amount of Cumin and Ginger Juliennes.
Allow them to sizzle.

Step 2:
Add 60 g cooked Keema and roast for approximately 30 seconds.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 1.5 ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1 ladle G-204 ROASTED RUST

Step 4:
Add 2 pieces of Chicken and mix thoroughly with the gravy.

Step 5 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli Powder for colour
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A pinch of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
Rara gravy should be thick and coating.
If the gravy becomes too dry, add a little water.

The Secret:
Add 1 tsp Desi Ghee and some Kasoori Methi, crushed between your hands before adding.

Final Flame:
Before switching off the gas, cook on high flame for approximately 15 seconds until oil starts appearing from the sides.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Handi or Earthen Handi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of Fresh Coriander
- Long-cut Green Chilli / Slit Green Chilli
- A small amount of Grated Boiled Egg (Optional but authentic)
  OR
- Ginger Juliennes


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy has become too oily.

Solution:
While transferring the dish into the serving bowl, avoid transferring excess oil from the pan.
Alternatively, mix in only 1 tsp White Gravy to balance it.

Problem: Keema and Gravy look separate.

Solution:
Cook the gravy for an additional 1–2 minutes and lightly mash the mixture so that the keema binds properly with the gravy.


HELPER TIP

"Remember, the real character of Rara comes from its roasted texture.
The gravy should not run or flow on the plate.
The keema should cling to the gravy and coat the chicken pieces."
