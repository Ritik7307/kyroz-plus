# KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

## PRODUCT: BARRAH MASALA

### Portion Size: 300 g

**A4 Kitchen Display Format**

### GRAVY MIX

* **G-205 ROYAL ROGAN — 60%**
* **G-204 ROASTED RUST — 40%**

### MAIN ITEM

* 3–4 pieces of Tandoori Mutton Barrah (Chops)
  **OR**
* Chicken Barrah (Large Tikka/Legs)
* Meat should be approximately **80% pre-cooked in the tandoor**.

---

# 1. INITIAL SETUP

### Flame

Keep the flame on **high** for roasting and smoke release.

### Base

Add:

* **1 tbsp Desi Ghee**
* **1/2 tsp Mustard Oil**

Mustard oil gives the dish a raw Punjabi-style flavour.

### Reheating

Add **40 ml Meat Stock or Water** to the pan.

---

# 2. COOKING PROCESS

### Step 1 — Spice Base

In the hot ghee and oil, add:

* **1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste**
* Finely chopped green chilli
* **1 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE**

Allow the spices to sizzle for approximately **10 seconds**.

### Step 2 — Roasting — CRUCIAL

Add the Tandoori Barrah pieces.

Toss on **high flame for 1 minute** so that the smoky tandoor flavour is released into the oil.

### Step 3 — Gravy Mixing

Add:

* **2.5 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN**
* **1.5 ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST**

G-204 ROASTED RUST provides the required coarse, roasted-spice texture.

### Step 4 — Spices

Add:

* **1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli**
* **1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH**
* **1/2 tsp Black Pepper Powder**

Barrah should have a slightly pepper-forward flavour.

### Step 5 — Bhunaai

Cook on **medium flame** until the gravy completely coats the meat.

The final dish should have a **semi-dry consistency**.

---

# 3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

### Consistency

* The gravy should be **extra thick and oily**.
* There should be no free-flowing water in the gravy.

### The Secret

Add:

* **1 tbsp Whisked Curd**
* **1 tsp Lemon Juice**

The curd helps balance the spice level.

### Final Smoke

If available:

1. Place a burning charcoal piece in a small bowl.
2. Place the bowl in the centre of the gravy.
3. Cover with a lid for **1 minute** to create a smoky aroma.

### Final Flame

Finish on **high flame for the last 10 seconds** until the ghee begins to float on the surface.

---

# 4. GARNISH & PLATING

### Plating

Serve on:

* A flat ceramic plate, or
* A sizzler plate

The plate should allow the Barrah pieces to remain clearly visible.

### Garnish

Add:

* Plenty of finely chopped fresh coriander
* Ginger juliennes
* Onion rings / Lachha Pyaaz on the side
* One lemon wedge on top

---

# 5. TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: Gravy Is Too Thin / Curry-Like

**Solution:**
Cook the dish further on high flame until only the thick, coating masala remains.

### Problem: Meat Is Too Spicy

**Solution:**
Add a small amount of butter or **1/2 spoon White Gravy** to balance the spice level.

---

# HELPER TIP

The real character of Barrah Masala comes from serving the meat **with the bones**.

The **charred edges of the meat should remain visible in the gravy**.

This dish is not meant to look delicate or overly refined. It should have a **bold, rough and spicy character**.
