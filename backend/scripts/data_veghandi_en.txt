KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: VEG HANDI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-204 ROASTED RUST (40%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (30%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
Mix Veg — Beans, Carrot, Cauliflower, Peas, Paneer Cubes
70% Pre-steamed


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Water to the pan.
This helps bind all three gravies together.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:

Heat the Butter in the pan.

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE

Step 2 - Vegetable Sauté:

Add the pre-steamed Mixed Vegetables and Paneer.

Toss on High Flame for 30 seconds so that the spices properly coat the vegetables.

Step 3 - The Multi-Layer Mix:

Add:
- 1.5 Ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST
  For texture and crunch.

- 1 Ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE
  For tanginess and colour.

- 1 Ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
  For spice and depth.

Step 4 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

Step 5 - Curd Touch:

Add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd.

This gives the Veg Handi its authentic sour-spicy taste.


3. FINISHING — THE LAYERED AROMA

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick.
It should be neither dry nor watery.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Kasoori Methi

Final Twist:
Add a pinch of Cardamom Powder, if available,
OR
very finely chopped Green Chilli.

Final Flame:
Boil on High Flame for only 10 seconds until the butter starts appearing around the sides.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Clay Handi or Deep Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Butter placed in the centre
- Ginger Juliennes
- Finely Chopped Fresh Coriander
- A light Fresh Cream circle around the mouth of the Handi


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The three gravies have mixed together and the dish looks "Messy".

Solution:
Add a little more K-802 WOK SPICE so that the texture becomes more defined and the vegetables remain visually separate.

Problem:
The dish colour has become too dark or black.

Solution:
Add a little G-201 SUNSET BASE and Fresh Cream to bring the colour back to a Bright Orange-Brown shade.


HELPER TIP

"Veg Handi is best when the customer gets a little taste of every gravy.

Do not over-mix or mash the vegetables.
Keep the vegetables whole."
