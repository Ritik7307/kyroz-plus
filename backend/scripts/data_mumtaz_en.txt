KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MURG MUMTAZ
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-202 IVORY BASE (50%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (25%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (25%)

MAIN ITEM:
5–6 Pieces Tandoori Chicken (70% Pre-cooked)
OR
Boneless Tikka


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.
Because G-202 IVORY BASE is the main base, it can burn easily if exposed to excessive heat.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Desi Ghee

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Milk to the pan.
Milk is used instead of water in Murg Mumtaz to achieve a rich, royal texture.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the butter and ghee in the pan.
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste and finely chopped Green Chilli.

Step 2 - The Rich Base:
First add 2 large ladles G-202 IVORY BASE.
Cook with the milk for approximately 30 seconds to create a smooth base.

Step 3 - The Color Mix:
Add:
- 1 ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE for tanginess
- 1 ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN for depth

This should give the dish a Deep Golden/Peach colour.

Step 4 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken pieces.
The smoky flavour of Tandoori Chicken pairs well with the creamy Mumtaz gravy.

Step 5 - Spices:
Keep the spice level low.
Add:
- 1/4 tsp White Pepper Powder, if available
OR
- A small amount of Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Luscious and Velvety — smooth, rich and thick.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- Crushed Kasoori Methi
- A pinch of Cardamom Powder

Cardamom is an essential part of the signature aroma of this dish.

Final Flame:
Cover the pan and cook on Low Flame for approximately 1 minute.
This allows the chicken to become soft and helps the aroma infuse into the dish.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium White Ceramic Bowl or Royal Copper Handi.

Garnish:
- Egg Net: Egg-white foam or thin omelette strips (optional)
- Finely chopped Cashews and Almonds
- Ginger Juliennes
- One spiral of Fresh Cream


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy colour has become too dark or red.

Solution:
Add a little G-202 IVORY BASE and Fresh Cream.
Adjust until the gravy returns to a Golden tone.

Problem: Gravy has split or looks grainy.

Solution:
Add a little warm Milk and whisk thoroughly until the smooth texture returns.


HELPER TIP

"Murg Mumtaz is a Royal dish.

Its flavour should not be spicy like a Mutton Curry.
It should be Mild and Aromatic.

The aroma of Cardamom and Kasoori Methi is the signature character of this dish."
