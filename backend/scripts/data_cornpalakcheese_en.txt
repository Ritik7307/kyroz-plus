KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CORN PALAK CHEESE
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-203 EMERALD MIX (70%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
Boiled Sweet Corn + Small Paneer Cubes + Processed Cheese


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.
Do not cook the spinach on very high heat, otherwise its colour may turn dark.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter.
Butter works especially well with cheese.

Reheating:
Add 30 ml Water or Milk to the pan.
Milk helps maintain the green colour of the spinach.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the butter in the pan.
Add:
- 1/4 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Step 2 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-203 EMERALD MIX
- 1 ladle G-202 IVORY BASE

G-202 IVORY BASE helps reduce the strong spinach/“iron” taste and makes the gravy silky.

Step 3 - Corn & Paneer:
Add:
- 2 tbsp Boiled Sweet Corn
- 50 g Paneer Cubes

Step 4 - Spices:
Keep the spice level low.

Add:
- A pinch of K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli, as it will affect the green colour.

Step 5 - Cheese Factor:
Add 1 tbsp Grated Cheese to the gravy.
Mix until the cheese completely melts into the gravy.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Smooth and Thick, with a velvety texture.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- A very small pinch of Sugar

This helps reduce the bitterness of the spinach.

Final Flame:
Boil on Low Flame for only 30 seconds.
Then switch off the flame.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a White Ceramic Bowl so that the green colour stands out.

Garnish:
- Plenty of Grated Cheese on top
  The cheese should melt over the piping-hot dish.

- A few Sweet Corn kernels in the centre.

- One Fresh Cream spiral.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The spinach colour has become dark or brown.

Solution:
Do not cook the dish for too long on high flame.
Add a little G-202 IVORY BASE to lighten the colour.

Problem:
The gravy is too thin or watery.

Solution:
Add a little Cheese and Grated Paneer.
This will quickly thicken the gravy.


HELPER TIP

"Corn Palak Cheese is a Mild and Creamy dish.

Do not add spicy masalas.

Make sure the customer gets corn and cheese in every bite."
