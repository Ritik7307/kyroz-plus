KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PALAK PANEER

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-203 EMERALD MIX

MAIN ITEM:
Fresh Paneer Cubes (Approx. 150 g – 180 g)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

IMPORTANT:
The Palak base must be cooked open.
Never cover the pan with a lid, otherwise the colour may turn dark.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.

The combination of ghee with Palak gives an authentic taste.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Hot Water or Vegetable Stock.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Lasooni Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

First add:
- 2 pieces Dry Red Chilli
- 1/2 tsp Whole Cumin Seeds
- 1/2 tsp Finely Chopped Garlic

Sauté the garlic until it becomes lightly golden-brown.

This Lasooni Tadka gives the Palak its authentic restaurant-style flavour.

Then add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Stir for another 10 seconds.

Step 2 - Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-203 EMERALD MIX
- 40 ml Hot Water

Allow the gravy to simmer uncovered on Medium Flame.

Step 3 - Paneer Fusion:

Add the Fresh Paneer Cubes.

Gently coat the paneer with the gravy so that the paneer remains soft and the cubes do not break.

Step 4 - Spices & Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH — for aromatic balance
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING — for a mild tangy punch against the earthy Palak flavour

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli or Turmeric.

The natural forest-green colour of the Palak must be maintained.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick and coating, so that it properly coats the paneer.

The Brightness Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- A pinch of Sugar

The sugar helps lock the green colour of the Palak and prevents it from becoming dull.

Final Flame:
Lightly toss on Medium Flame for 20–30 seconds until the ghee starts to lightly shine around the corners.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium White Ceramic Bowl or Traditional Handi.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream in a neat round swirl or zig-zag pattern
- Thin and long Ginger Juliennes
- A little Grated Paneer sprinkled on top

The white paneer against the green Palak gives the dish a premium appearance.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The Palak colour looks dark or black/oxidized.

Solution:
Never cover the pan with a lid while cooking the Palak.

To help recover the colour:
Add a pinch of Sugar and 1 tbsp Fresh Cream and mix properly.


Problem:
The Palak has a slight bitterness.

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp Butter or slightly increase the Fresh Cream.

Do NOT use Curd or acidic ingredients, as they may make the green colour pale.


HELPER TIP

"Palak Paneer is identified by its bright Forest Green colour and roasted garlic flavour.

Keep the quantity of G-203 EMERALD MIX and K-801 ROYAL PUNCH perfectly measured.

Before sending the dish to the counter, always give it a premium finish with Fresh Cream and Grated Paneer."
