KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: SAAGWALA MEAT
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-203 EMERALD MIX (70%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
3–4 Pieces Mutton or Chicken (80% Pre-cooked)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High for proper meat and spinach bhunai.

Base:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Meat Stock (Mutton cooking water) or plain water to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, finely chopped Green Chilli and 1 tsp Chopped Garlic to the hot ghee.
Cook the garlic until lightly golden.

Step 2 - Meat Sauté:
Add the pre-cooked Mutton or Chicken pieces.
Sauté quickly in ghee for approximately 1 minute to remove the raw smell from the meat.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-203 EMERALD MIX
  Spinach base.

- 1 large ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
  Gives the spinach additional spice and depth.

Step 4 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli for a light red colour
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- Salt according to taste

Step 5 - Simmering / Bhunai:
Reduce the flame to Medium.
Cook until the water from the spinach dries up and the gravy completely coats the meat pieces.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Thick and Coating.
It should not be watery on the serving plate.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd or Fresh Cream.
This helps reduce the bitterness of the spinach.

Final Touch:
Add 1/2 tsp Kasoori Methi and 1 tsp Butter.
Toss on High Flame for approximately 10 seconds.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Black Ceramic Bowl or Copper Handi.

Garnish:
- One spiral of Fresh Cream
- Ginger Juliennes
- One Fried Whole Dry Red Chilli placed on top


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Spinach colour has become too dark.

Solution:
Do not cook it for too long on high flame.
Add a little White Gravy or Cream to balance the colour.

Problem:
Meat remains hard or chewy.

Solution:
Add a little Meat Stock, cover the pan and cook on Low Flame for 2–3 minutes on Dum.


HELPER TIP

"The authentic taste of Saagwala Meat comes from the Desi Ghee and Garlic tempering.

Make sure the spinach does not remain separate from the meat.
Both should blend together into one."
