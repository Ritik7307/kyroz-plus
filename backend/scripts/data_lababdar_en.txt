KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN LABABDAR
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (60%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (20%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
5–6 Pieces Chicken Tikka (70% Pre-cooked)
OR
Boneless Chicken Cubes


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Water or Chicken Stock to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the butter in the pan.
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, finely chopped Green Chilli and Ginger Juliennes.

Step 2 - The Veggie Crunch:
Add 1 tbsp finely chopped Onion and finely chopped Capsicum.
The signature of Lababdar is its chunky yet soft gravy.
Sauté for approximately 30 seconds.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 1/2 ladle White Gravy
- 1/2 ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

Step 4 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Chicken Tikka pieces.

Step 5 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of Kitchen King Masala


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick and Granular due to the chopped onions.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp grated Paneer into the gravy.
This is the signature element of Chicken Lababdar.

Final Touch:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Butter
- Crushed Kasoori Methi

Flame:
Mix rapidly on high flame for approximately 20 seconds until the gravy becomes glossy.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Handi or Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- Plenty of grated Paneer on top
- One spiral of Fresh Cream
- Finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- A small bunch of Ginger Juliennes in the centre


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy looks exactly like Butter Chicken.

Solution:
Increase the grated Paneer and slightly increase the chopped Onion-Capsicum mixture.
Lababdar should always have a slightly Rough texture and should not be completely smooth.

Problem: Gravy is too sour.

Solution:
Add a little White Gravy and a pinch of Sugar to balance the flavour.


HELPER TIP

"The secret of Lababdar lies in its texture.
When the customer takes a spoonful, they should get the soft strands of Paneer and the slight crunch of finely chopped Onion in the gravy.
It should be both Rich and Masaledar."
