# KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

## PRODUCT: TANGY RED SAUCE PASTA (ARRABBIATA)

---

## 1. MEASUREMENT

For consistent taste and quality, follow the quantities below for one portion.

| Item                         |                       Quantity — 1 Portion |
| ---------------------------- | -----------------------------------------: |
| Boiled Pasta (Penne/Fusilli) |                                  180–200 g |
| Water / Tomato Stock         |                           200 ml (1 glass) |
| C-506 MARINARA CORE          |              50 g (approximately 3.5 tbsp) |
| Refined Oil / Olive Oil      |                             10 ml (1 tbsp) |
| Vegetables                   | Garlic chunks, Capsicum, Onion, Sweet Corn |

---

## 2. COOKING STEPS

### Step 1 — Sauté the Basics

Heat the oil in a pan. If fresh garlic and onion are available, sauté them until lightly browned.

### Step 2 — Add Vegetables

Add the remaining vegetables and cook on high heat for approximately **1 minute**.

### Step 3 — Add Water & Premix

Add **200 ml water or tomato stock** to the pan.

Immediately add **50 g C-506 MARINARA CORE** and mix thoroughly.

### Step 4 — Boil the Sauce

Keep stirring the sauce until it begins to boil.

After boiling, the sauce should develop a rich, deep red tomato colour.

### Step 5 — Add Pasta

Once the sauce starts to thicken slightly, add the **boiled pasta**.

### Step 6 — Final Cooking

Cook the pasta with the sauce for **1–2 minutes**, allowing the pasta to absorb the flavour.

Serve hot.

---

## 3. IMPORTANT HELPER ADVICE

### No Extra Salt

* The red sauce premix already contains a balanced level of salt and spice.
* Do not add additional salt or seasoning unless specifically required by the standard recipe.

### Correct Consistency

* Red sauce pasta should be slightly more **saucy and fluid** than white sauce pasta.
* Do not reduce the sauce excessively or allow it to become too dry.

### Pink Sauce Option

* If the customer requests **Mix Sauce Pasta**, combine red sauce and white sauce in a **1:1 ratio**.
* Mix thoroughly before serving.

### Fresh Touch

* If fresh basil leaves are available, tear a few leaves and add them on top just before serving.
* This enhances the aroma and presentation.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem                         | Possible Reason                                   | Solution                                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sauce is too sour               | Too much premix was added.                        | Add a small amount of water and a little sugar to balance the flavour.      |
| Sauce has lumps                 | The powder was not mixed properly with the water. | Add the powder immediately after adding the water and stir continuously.    |
| Pasta tastes bland              | The pasta was washed excessively after boiling.   | Cook the pasta in the sauce for a little longer so it absorbs more flavour. |
| Sauce colour is not deep enough | The sauce was not boiled sufficiently.            | Allow the sauce to boil properly for at least **1–2 minutes**.              |

---

## 5. PRESENTATION TIP

Before serving, garnish with a light amount of:

* Grated cheese
* Oregano
* Chilli flakes

This improves the visual appeal and gives the customer a more premium, value-for-money experience.
