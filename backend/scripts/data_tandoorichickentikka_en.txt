KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: TANDOORI CHICKEN & TIKKA
PROCESS: 1st & 2nd MARINATION

PURPOSE:
To keep Tandoori items soft, juicy and flavorful.


1. SPECIFICATIONS

Tandoori Chicken:
Use a full chicken cut into 8 pieces or half chicken.
Make 2–3 deep cuts on the breast and leg pieces.

Chicken Tikka:
Use boneless chicken cubes weighing 35–40 g each.


2. 1st MARINATION — MUST STEP

Ingredients:
- Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Lemon Juice
- Salt

Method:
Apply the first marinade evenly to the chicken.

Resting Time:
Keep the chicken marinated for at least 1 hour.

Purpose:
This helps remove the raw smell from the chicken and makes the chicken tender from inside.


3. 2nd MARINATION — THE RED MASALA

Base:
Hung Curd — strained curd with no excess water.

Spices:
- Degi Chilli — for colour
- Garam Masala
- Roasted Cumin Powder
- Kasoori Methi
- Mustard Oil

SECRET:
Heat the Mustard Oil and Degi Chilli and mix them into the curd.
This helps produce a natural and bright colour.

Resting Time:
Keep the chicken in the second marinade for at least 4–6 hours in the chiller.


4. PRE-COOKING — THE 70% RULE

Before orders begin, prepare the chicken in batches.

Cook the chicken in the Tandoor up to 70%.

After cooking, remove the chicken from the skewer and place it on a wire rack so that excess water can drain out.


5. STORAGE & SHELF LIFE

Marinated Raw Chicken:
Storage Temperature: 0°C to 4°C — Chiller
Shelf Life: 24 Hours
Container: SS Deep Pan with Lid

70% Cooked Tandoori / Tikka:
Storage Temperature: 0°C to 4°C — Chiller
Shelf Life: 12–18 Hours
Container: Perforated Tray / Wire Rack


6. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Chicken Tikka feels dry or rubbery in the gravy.

Reason:
The first marination was skipped or the chicken was cooked 100% in the Tandoor before refrigeration.

Solution:
Always cook the chicken only up to 70%.
During reheating, apply Butter/Cream and finish it in the Tandoor.

Problem:
Marination is coming off the chicken / watery marination.

Reason:
The curd contained excess water or the chicken was not dried properly after washing.

Solution:
Always use Hung Curd.
Dry the chicken properly using a cloth or strainer before marination.

IMPORTANT:
The marinade must be pushed properly into the cuts of the chicken.
Otherwise, the inside of the piece may taste bland when served with gravy.


7. IMPORTANT REHEATING STEPS — FOR SERVICE

When an order comes in, for example Chicken Changezi:

1. Heat the pre-cooked chicken piece in the Tandoor for 2 minutes.
2. Apply a light coating of Butter and Chaat Masala.
3. Add the chicken to the prepared gravy.
