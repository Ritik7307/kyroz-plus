## 1. STANDARD SHAKE PREPARATION METHOD

| Step | Action       | Description                                                           |
| ---- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01   | Chilled Milk | Add **200 ml ice-cold full-cream milk** to the mixer jar.             |
| 02   | Kyroz Base   | Add **40–45 g SHAKE BASE** according to the recipe.                   |
| 03   | Flavoring    | Add syrup, biscuits such as Oreo, or coffee as required.              |
| 04   | Ice Cubes    | Add only **2–3 ice cubes**. Too much ice can make the shake watery.   |
| 05   | Blending     | Blend for **45–60 seconds** until the shake becomes thick and smooth. |

# 3. IMPORTANT HELPER ADVICE

### 1. Milk Temperature

* Milk must always be **ice cold**.
* If the milk is warm, the shake may not achieve the required thick consistency.

### 2. Preparing Coffee

* For cold coffee, dissolve the coffee powder first in **1 teaspoon of warm water** to make a smooth paste.
* Add the coffee paste to the mixer.
* This helps distribute the coffee flavour evenly.

### 3. Garnishing

* Before serving, apply a small amount of chocolate or strawberry syrup along the inside walls of the glass.
* This gives the drink a more premium appearance.

### 4. Cleaning

* Wash the mixer jar immediately after preparing each shake.
* This prevents the flavour of the previous drink from transferring to the next drink.

---

# 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem                   | Possible Reason                                     | Solution                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shake is too thin         | Milk was not cold enough or too much ice was added. | Use properly chilled milk next time and add approximately 5 g extra base powder if required. |
| Shake is not sweet enough | Customer wants a sweeter drink.                     | Add 1 teaspoon powdered sugar or a small amount of extra syrup.                              |
| Lumps are present         | The mixture was not blended for enough time.        | Blend for at least **45 seconds** until smooth.                                              |
| Coffee tastes bitter      | Too much coffee powder was added.                   | Add a small amount of Vanilla Base powder or a little more milk to balance the flavour.      |

---

# 5. STORAGE RULES FOR OWNER

### Moisture Alert

* Keep the powder container tightly closed at all times.
* Moisture can cause the powder to form lumps and affect its quality.

### Spoon Safety

* Always use a completely **dry spoon** when removing powder from the container.
* Never use a wet or damp spoon.
