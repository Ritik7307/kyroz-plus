KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PRE-COOKED MUTTON (80% DONE)

USE:
For Mutton Curry, Handi, Rogan Josh and Bhuna Gosht

TARGET:
Mutton should be tender but should not fall off the bone by itself.
The piece should hold its shape.


1. PREPARATION (MISE-EN-PLACE)

Mutton Cut:
Use Mix Curry Cut (a mix of Nalli, Put and Chaap).
Each piece should weigh approximately 70–80 g.

Cleaning:
Wash the mutton in cold water with Salt and a little Lemon Juice.
This helps remove the raw smell from the mutton.

Draining:
After washing, keep the mutton in a strainer for 10–15 minutes so that all dirty water drains out.


2. COOKING PROCESS

HELPER INSTRUCTION:
Do not put the mutton directly into the pressure cooker.
Always roast/bhunao the mutton first.

Step 1 - Whole Spices & Ghee:
Heat 2 tbsp Desi Ghee and 1 tbsp Oil in a large Pressure Cooker or Lagan.

Add:
- 4 Black Cardamoms
- 5–6 Cloves
- 2 pieces of Cinnamon
- 4–5 Bay Leaves

Step 2 - Aroma Base:
Add 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste.
Roast until it becomes lightly brown.

Step 3 - The Bhunai (Sealing):
Add the mutton pieces.
Roast on High Flame for 8–10 minutes.

WHY?
When the mutton becomes brown, its juices get locked inside, helping the pieces remain tender.

Step 4 - Liquid & Salt:
Add Hot Water until the water level is approximately 2 inches above the mutton.

Also add:
- 1.5 tsp Salt
- 1/2 tsp Turmeric
- 2–3 Whole Onions, cut from the centre
  This helps make the gravy richer.

Step 5 - COOKING TIME (PRESSURE):

Whistles:
Give 1 whistle on High Flame.
Then reduce the flame and cook for 12–15 minutes.
Cooking time depends on the quality of the mutton.

CHECK:
Mutton should be approximately 80% cooked.
When pressed by hand, it should feel tender but should not separate from the bone.


3. COOLING & STORAGE

Crucial Step:
Immediately remove the mutton from the Yakhni (Stock) and spread the pieces on a tray.

If the mutton remains in the hot stock, it will continue cooking due to carry-over cooking and may become 100% cooked.

THE YAKHNI:
Strain the remaining mutton stock and store it in the refrigerator.
This stock contains the main flavour and should be used in small quantities in different mutton gravies.

Shelf Life:
2 days in the Chiller.

Do not freeze.
Freezing may change the taste.


4. IMPORTANT HELPER POINTS

HOT WATER:
Always add Hot Water to the mutton.
Adding cold water can cause the mutton to shrink and become tough.

SIZE MATTERS:
Nalli pieces take longer to cook.
Therefore, keep the pieces approximately equal in size.

NO RED CHILLI:
Do not add Red Chilli during pre-cooking.
Otherwise, the authentic colour of dishes such as Rogan Josh may be affected.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Mutton remains very hard or chewy.

Solution:
This may mean the mutton was from an older animal.
Next time, add 1 tsp Raw Papaya Paste while cooking.

Problem:
Mutton has been overcooked.

Solution:
Use it in Mutton Keema or Mutton Saagwala, where very soft meat can work well.
Do not use it for Curry or Handi.
