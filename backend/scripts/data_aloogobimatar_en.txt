KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: ALOO GOBI MATAR (SEMI-GRAVY)

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
(1.5 Standard Ladles for Semi-Gravy)

MAIN ITEM:
Pre-fried Aloo & Gobi Florets + Boiled Green Peas (Matar)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
30 ml Hot Water.
This is used to achieve the desired Semi-Gravy texture between dry and gravy.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Halwai-Style Tadka:

Heat the oil and ghee.

First add:
- 1/2 tsp Whole Cumin Seeds
- 1 pinch Asafoetida (Heeng)

As soon as the cumin starts crackling, add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Roast for 10–15 seconds.

Step 2 - Vegetable & Base Integration:

Add 1.5 Standard Ladles of G-205 ROYAL ROGAN base to the pan.

Add 30 ml Hot Water and allow the gravy to come to a light boil.

Now add:
- Pre-fried Aloo
- Pre-fried Gobi
- Boiled Green Peas

Stir gently so that the Gobi florets do not break.

Step 3 - Spices & Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/4 tsp Kashmiri Chilli — for vibrant colour
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH — for aromatic depth
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING — for a mild tangy/spicy taste


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Dry and Coating/Lipti hui in texture.

The masala should properly coat the Aloo and Gobi, with no excess water floating in the pan.

Aroma Lock:
Add:
- Hand-crushed Kasoori Methi
- A small amount of Crushed Black Pepper

Final Flame:
Toss quickly on High Flame for 20–30 seconds until the ghee separates from the sides and gives the vegetables a beautiful shine.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on a Flat Ceramic Plate or Traditional Oval Veg Dish.

Garnish:
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Long, thin Slit Green Chilli
- Ginger Juliennes


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The Gobi florets have become completely soft and the vegetables have turned mushy.

Solution:
Pre-fry the Aloo and Gobi up to approximately 80% before service.

After adding them to the pan, do not stir aggressively.
Only toss gently.


Problem:
The vegetable feels too dry and the customer wants some gravy.

Solution:
Add:
- 1–2 tbsp Hot Water
- 1/2 Ladle extra G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

Simmer for 10 seconds to achieve the perfect coating Semi-Gravy texture.


HELPER TIP

"Aloo Gobi Matar tastes best when the Gobi florets remain intact and the G-205 ROYAL ROGAN masala properly coats the vegetables.

Measure the K-806 ZESTFUL ZING accurately to achieve the authentic tangy wedding-party style flavour."
