# KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

## PRODUCT: INSTANT PIZZA DOUGH

---

## 1. PREPARATION

### Dough Preparation — For 1 kg Premix

| Step | Action         | Quantity / Instruction                                                                                                    |
| ---- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01   | Weight Check   | Take 1 kg C-501 DOUGH MASTER into a clean bowl or mixer bowl.                                                             |
| 02   | Water Addition | Slowly add 600–700 ml lukewarm water.                                                                                     |
| 03   | Oil Addition   | Add 50 ml refined oil or olive oil along with the water.                                                                  |
| 04   | Kneading       | Knead the dough properly for 10–15 minutes until it becomes completely smooth.                                            |
| 05   | Window Test    | Stretch a small portion of the dough. If it stretches thin without tearing and forms a thin membrane, the dough is ready. |

---

## 2. PROOFING & STORAGE

### Resting

* Apply a light layer of oil over the dough.
* Cover the dough with a damp cloth or plastic wrap.
* Make sure the dough is completely covered to prevent it from drying out.

### Proofing Time

* Keep the dough in a warm place at normal room temperature for **45–60 minutes**.
* The dough should approximately double in size.

### Punching

* Once the dough has doubled in size, gently press or punch it down.
* This removes excess air from the dough.

### Portioning

* Divide the dough according to the required pizza size.
* Example: **200 g dough for a 10-inch pizza**.
* Shape each portion into a smooth ball.
* Refrigerate the dough balls or use them immediately as required.

---

## 3. IMPORTANT ADVICE FOR HELPERS

### 1. Water Temperature

* Water should be neither too cold nor too hot.
* The water should feel mildly warm when checked with a finger.
* **Do not use very hot water**, as excessive heat can damage the yeast.

### 2. Protect the Dough from Air

* Do not leave the dough exposed to air.
* Exposure to air can create a dry skin or crust on the surface.
* Always keep the dough covered.

### 3. Cleanliness

* Wash and clean your hands before preparing the pizza.
* Clean the working counter properly.
* Use a light dusting of flour when required during handling.

### 4. Avoid Over-Kneading

* Once the dough becomes smooth and passes the window test, stop kneading.
* Over-kneading can affect the texture and may result in a harder pizza base.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem                | Possible Reason                                    | Solution                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dough is not rising    | Water was too cold or the premix was left open.    | Use slightly warmer water next time and keep the premix packet tightly sealed.        |
| Dough is too sticky    | Too much water was added.                          | Lightly dust with dry flour and knead again until the dough becomes manageable.       |
| Pizza base is hard     | Oven temperature was too low or the dough was old. | Preheat the oven to **220°C–250°C** before baking. Use fresh dough whenever possible. |
| Dough has a sour smell | Dough was left outside for too long.               | Transfer the dough to the refrigerator after approximately **1 hour**.                |

---

## 5. STORAGE RULES FOR OWNER

### Packet Seal

* After opening the C-501 DOUGH MASTER packet, transfer the remaining premix into an airtight container.
* Keep the container tightly closed.
* Protect the premix from moisture, as moisture can affect the yeast and product quality.

### FIFO Rule

* Follow the **First-In, First-Out (FIFO)** system.
* Use the oldest stock first.
* Always check existing stock before opening a new packet.
