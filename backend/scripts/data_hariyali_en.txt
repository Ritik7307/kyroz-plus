KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MURG HARIYALI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-203 EMERALD MIX (60%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
5–6 Pieces Tandoori Hariyali Tikka (70% Pre-cooked)
OR
Roasted Chicken Pieces


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to Low.
Both spinach and G-202 IVORY BASE can change colour when exposed to high heat.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 30–40 ml Milk or Water to the pan.
Milk helps maintain a bright green colour.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the butter and oil in the pan.
Add 1/4 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste and finely chopped Green Chilli.
Sauté for approximately 10 seconds.

Step 2 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-203 EMERALD MIX
- 1.5 ladles G-202 IVORY BASE

G-202 IVORY BASE reduces the sharpness of the spinach and gives the gravy a richer, creamier character.

Step 3 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Hariyali Tikka, if available.
Otherwise, add regular Tandoori Chicken.

Hariyali Tikka gives the gravy an enhanced green-herb flavour.

Step 4 - Spices:
DO NOT ADD KASHMIRI CHILLI.

Add only:
- 1/4 tsp White Pepper
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH

Step 5 - The Secret Punch:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Whisked Curd
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

This gives the dish a pleasant tangy flavour.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely Smooth and Creamy, with a malai-like texture.

Aroma:
Add 1 tbsp Fresh Cream and crushed Kasoori Methi.

Freshness:
Add a handful of finely chopped Fresh Coriander and Mint, if available.

Flame:
Simmer on low flame for 1–2 minutes until the chicken becomes soft.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a White Ceramic Bowl so that the green colour stands out clearly.

Garnish:
- One spiral of Fresh Cream
- Ginger Juliennes
- A light sprinkle of Grated Paneer or Cheese


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy looks Brown or dull.

Solution:
Add 1–2 tbsp G-202 IVORY BASE and a little Milk.
This will help make the colour lighter and brighter.

Problem: Gravy is too thin or watery.

Solution:
Add 1 tbsp Grated Paneer or increase the Cashew Paste (G-202 IVORY BASE) slightly.


HELPER TIP

"Murg Hariyali is a Cool & Creamy dish.

DO NOT USE RED CHILLI in this dish.

Every bite should deliver the fresh flavour of Coriander, Mint and Cream."
