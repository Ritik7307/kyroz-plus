KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: TANDOORI CHICKEN
PROCESS: MARINATION & PRE-COOK

USE:
For Tandoori Chicken Full/Half, Chicken Changezi and Tandoori Platters.

TARGET:
Chicken should remain juicy from inside and the masala should properly coat the outside.


1. PREPARATION (MISE-EN-PLACE)

Chicken Cut:
Use a full chicken weighing approximately 800 g–1 kg.
Cut it into 4 or 8 pieces.

The Cut:
Make 2–3 deep cuts on the thick portions of the Breast and Leg.

WHY?
So that the masala reaches up to the bone and the chicken does not taste bland from inside.

Cleaning:
Wash the chicken with Salt and Lemon water.
Keep it in a strainer for 15 minutes.

IMPORTANT:
The chicken must be completely Dry before marination.


2. FIRST MARINATION — 1 HOUR

HELPER INSTRUCTION:
This step is important for making the chicken tender and removing the raw smell.

Ingredients:
- Ginger-Garlic Paste — 2 tbsp
- Lemon Juice — 2 tbsp
- Salt — according to taste

Method:
Rub the mixture properly into the cuts of the chicken so that the first marinade reaches inside.

Resting:
Keep the chicken in the Chiller for at least 1 hour.


3. SECOND MARINATION — FINAL MASALA

HELPER INSTRUCTION:
This step provides the outer coating and the main flavour.

Ingredients:
1. Hung Curd — 200 g
   The curd must contain no water.

2. Tandoori Masala — 3–4 tbsp
   Use your prepared/pre-made Tandoori Masala.

3. Salt
   The prepared masala does not contain salt, so add Extra Salt and a little Black Salt.

4. Mustard Oil — 2 tbsp
   This gives better smoke and flavour in the Tandoor.

5. Degi Chilli
   For a light red colour.

Method:
Mix all the ingredients together to make a thick paste.
Apply the paste properly over the chicken that has already undergone the first marination.

Resting:
Keep the chicken in the Chiller for 4–6 hours.
For the best result, keep it overnight.


4. PRE-COOKING — TANDOOR SETTING

Temperature:
The Tandoor should be Medium Hot.

The 70% Rule:
Place the chicken on skewers and put it in the Tandoor.
Cook for only 10–12 minutes.

Result:
The chicken should develop colour on the outside but remain slightly undercooked inside.

Storage:
Remove the chicken from the skewers and place it on a wire rack so that excess water can drain out.
Cool it and store it in the Chiller.


5. IMPORTANT HELPER POINTS

Salt:
The Tandoori Masala does not contain salt.
Always check and add salt properly.
Bland chicken is not acceptable.

No Water:
If the curd is thin or the chicken is wet, the masala will slide off in the Tandoor and the chicken will look bare.

Butter Finish:
When an order comes in, heat the chicken in the Tandoor for 2–3 minutes.
Apply plenty of Butter and sprinkle Chaat Masala.


6. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Masala is not sticking to the chicken.

Solution:
This means the curd was too watery.
Add 1 tsp Roasted Besan to the masala.
It will help the masala form a proper coating.

Problem:
Chicken is getting burnt on the outside.

Solution:
The Tandoor is too hot.
Move the chicken to a slightly cooler part of the Tandoor and keep applying Oil/Butter.
