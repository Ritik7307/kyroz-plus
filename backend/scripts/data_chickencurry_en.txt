KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN CURRY

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

MAIN ITEM:
70–80% Pre-cooked Chicken Pieces (Curry Cut)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
50 ml Chicken Stock.
Use the water from pre-cooking the chicken to retain its natural flavour.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Tariwala Tadka:
Heat the oil and ghee.

Add:
- 1 piece Crushed Black Cardamom
- 2 Cloves
- 1/2 tsp Shahi Jeera

As soon as the spices begin to crackle, add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely chopped Green Chilli

Roast for approximately 15 seconds to remove the raw smell.

Step 2 - Base & Meat Integration:
Add 2 Standard Ladles of G-205 ROYAL ROGAN base to the pan.

Add:
- 50 ml Chicken Stock
- Pre-cooked Chicken Pieces

Reduce the flame slightly and allow the curry to simmer for 1–2 minutes so that the chicken absorbs the gravy properly.

Step 3 - Spices & Flavor Punch:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli — for the red colour of the tari
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH — for deep aroma and authentic commercial flavour


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should NOT be completely smooth.
It should be slightly grainy, Medium-Thin and Oily, with a beautiful red Rogan floating on top.

Aroma Lock:
Add hand-crushed Kasoori Methi.

Final Flame:
Cook on High Flame for 20–30 seconds until the ghee and Rogan separate and shine on the surface.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Traditional Ceramic Bowl or Deep Round Handi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Thin Ginger Juliennes
- Pour a little of the remaining hot Rogan/Ghee from the pan over the top before serving.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The curry colour looks pale and the Rogan is not floating on top.

Solution:
Simmer the G-205 base slightly longer.
During finishing, touch 1/4 tsp Kashmiri Chilli mixed with hot ghee separately onto the side of the pan.
The colour will develop properly.

Problem:
Chicken pieces feel dry or hard while cutting.

Solution:
Check whether the pre-cooked chicken was properly kept submerged in the stock.
During reheating, always use low flame and give the chicken enough time to absorb the gravy.
