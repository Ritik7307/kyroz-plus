KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MUTTON ROGAN JOSH
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (80%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (20% - only for tanginess)

MAIN ITEM:
3–4 Pieces Mutton (Shoulder/Raan)
80% Pre-cooked (Slow Boiled with Whole Spices)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.
Slow cooking is necessary for the "Rogan" to rise to the top.

Base:
1.5 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1 tsp Mustard Oil.
Mustard oil provides a pungent flavour.

Reheating:
Add 50 ml Mutton Stock (Yakhni - water from boiling the mutton) to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, 2 Green Cardamoms, 1 Black Cardamom and a piece of Cinnamon to the hot ghee and oil.

Step 2 - Meat Sauté:
Add the pre-cooked Mutton pieces.
Roast on high flame for approximately 2 minutes until the meat develops a light brown colour.

Step 3 - The Rogan Mix:
Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1/2 ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE

G-205 ROYAL ROGAN provides thickness.
G-201 SUNSET BASE provides deep colour.

Step 4 - The Curd Factor (CRUCIAL):
In a bowl, mix:
- 2 tbsp Whisked Curd
- 1 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp Dry Ginger Powder

Add this mixture to the gravy.
Reduce the flame before adding it so that the curd does not split.

Step 5 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Fennel Powder
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- Salt according to taste


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Thin to Medium, similar to a Rassa.
It should not be too thick.

The Secret:
Add 1/2 tsp Kewra Water, if available.
If available in the kitchen, add a little Ratan Jot-infused oil on top to enhance the "Rogan" shine.

The Slow Dum:
Reduce the flame to very low.
Cover the pan and cook for approximately 5 minutes until the Ghee/Rogan completely floats on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Deep Ceramic Bowl or Copper Handi.

Garnish:
- Red Oil (Rogan): Take the red oil from the pan using a ladle and pour it over the top.
- Finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Ginger Juliennes
- Highlight one Mutton Bone (Nalli) in the centre.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Gravy has become too Sour.

Solution:
Add a pinch of Sugar or a little Mutton Stock to balance the flavour.

Problem:
"Rogan" (Red Oil) is not appearing on top.

Solution:
The flame may be too high.
Reduce the flame and add a little Desi Ghee from the side.
The oil should naturally rise to the surface.


HELPER TIP

"Rogan Josh means 'cooked in hot oil'.

Fennel and Dry Ginger Powder are essential.
Without them, it will simply become Mutton Curry.

The colour should be Deep Red, not Orange."
