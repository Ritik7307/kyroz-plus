KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: KADHAI CHICKEN

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-204 ROASTED RUST

MAIN ITEM:
70–80% Pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken Pieces
OR
Roasted Chicken


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
This helps provide a smoky punch and better bhunai.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Chicken Stock.
Use the remaining water from pre-cooking the chicken so that the natural meat flavour is retained.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Kadhai Tadka:

Heat the oil and ghee.

Add:
- 2 Dry Whole Red Chillies
- 1/2 tsp Cumin

As soon as the cumin starts crackling, add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- 1 tsp Whisked Curd

Stir quickly for 10–15 seconds.

Step 2 - Veggie Crunch Sauté:

Add:
- Capsicum, cut into squares/cubes
- Onion, cut into squares/cubes

Sauté on high flame for 30–40 seconds.

IMPORTANT:
The vegetables should retain their natural crunch and bright colour.

Step 3 - Base & Meat Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST
- 40 ml Chicken Stock
- Pre-cooked Chicken Pieces

Mix gently.

Step 4 - Spices & Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE

K-802 WOK SPICE provides the characteristic Kadhai aroma.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Dry with a Bhuna/Grainy texture.
It should properly coat the chicken pieces.

Aroma Lock:
Add:
- Hand-crushed Kasoori Methi
- A pinch of Crushed Black Pepper

Final Flame:
Cook on High Flame until the ghee/oil starts separating from the sides and the chicken develops a beautiful shine.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Traditional Copper Kadhai or Round Handi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Ginger Juliennes
- Long-cut Green Chilli
- Lightly float Desi Ghee on top before serving


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The masala has not absorbed properly into the chicken and the pieces taste bland.

Solution:
After adding the chicken in Step 2, allow it to simmer with the gravy on low flame for at least 1–2 minutes so that the meat properly absorbs the gravy.

Problem:
The gravy has become too dry and is sticking to the bottom.

Solution:
Immediately add 2 tbsp Hot Chicken Stock or Hot Water from the side.
Do NOT add cold water.


HELPER TIP

"Kadhai Chicken tastes best when the onion and capsicum retain their crunch and the G-204 ROASTED RUST base looks properly coated and bhuna.

The aroma of K-802 WOK SPICE is its real identity, so always measure it accurately."
