KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: SIGNATURE PANCH-RATAN CURRY
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (40%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (30%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (30%)

THE FIVE GEMS (PANCH-RATAN):
1. Paneer Cubes
2. Mushroom Slices
3. Sweet Corn
4. Green Peas (Matar)
5. Roasted Makhana (Lotus Seeds)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
This helps properly coat the vegetables and Makhana.

Base:
1 tbsp Ghee + 1 tsp Butter.

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Milk or Water to the pan.
Milk gives the gravy a rich royal shine.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Roasting the Five Gems:

Heat the Ghee in the pan.

First, add the Makhana and fry for 30 seconds so that it remains crunchy.

Then add:
- Paneer
- Mushroom
- Sweet Corn
- Green Peas

Sauté everything on High Flame for 1 minute.

Step 2 - Aroma Base:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- A small amount of K-802 WOK SPICE

Toss everything properly.

Step 3 - The Triple Gravy Blend:

Add:
- 1.5 Ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
  For deep colour and tanginess.

- 1 Ladle G-202 IVORY BASE
  For creamy richness.

- 1 Ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
  For spiced texture.

Step 4 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of Kitchen King Masala, if available.

Step 5 - Simmering:

Reduce the flame to Low.

Cook for 2 minutes so that all five "Gems" properly absorb the gravy.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Extra Rich and Semi-Thick.
It should properly coat all the vegetables and ingredients.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- A pinch of Saffron Milk, if available.

Final Touch:
Add:
- 1 tsp Honey
OR
- A pinch of Sugar

The dish should have a balanced Mild-Sweet and Spicy flavour.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium Round Ceramic Bowl or Glass Bowl.

Garnish:
- Pomegranate Seeds — sprinkle on top for contrast.
- Fried Cashews — 4–5 pieces.
- Fresh Cream Zigzag Design.
- Finely Chopped Fresh Coriander.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Makhana has become too soft or soggy.

Solution:
Always roast the Makhana in Ghee first and add it to the gravy only at the end.

Problem:
The colour of the dish looks dull.

Solution:
Increase the G-201 SUNSET BASE slightly and add some Butter so that a Bright Orange-Red glaze develops.
