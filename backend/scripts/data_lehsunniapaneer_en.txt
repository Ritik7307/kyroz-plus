KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: LEHSUNIA PANEER
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-203 EMERALD MIX (80%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
Paneer Cubes (150 g – 180 g)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
This is required to properly brown the garlic.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.
The ghee helps bring out the garlic flavour.

Reheating:
Add 30–40 ml Water to the pan.
This helps prevent the G-203 EMERALD MIX from burning.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Garlic Punch (Hero Step):

Add 1.5 tbsp finely chopped Garlic to the hot oil and ghee.

Sauté until the garlic becomes Dark Golden/Light Brown.

IMPORTANT:
Do not burn the garlic.
Burnt garlic will make the dish bitter.

Step 2 - Aroma Base:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Sauté for 10 seconds.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-203 EMERALD MIX
- 1/2 ladle G-202 IVORY BASE

G-202 IVORY BASE helps balance the spinach/“iron” taste and brings out the garlic flavour.

Step 4 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING
- Salt according to taste

IMPORTANT:
Do not use Red Chilli so that the green colour remains intact.

Step 5 - Paneer:

Add the Paneer Cubes.

Stir gently for 1 minute so that the garlic flavour properly enters the paneer.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick and silky.

The Secret:
Before switching off the flame, add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- Kasoori Methi

Final Touch:
If the customer wants a stronger flavour, add a spoonful of Garlic Tadka prepared by roasting garlic separately in ghee.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Black or White Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- Fried Golden Garlic Bits
- One Fresh Cream Spiral
- One Fried Whole Dry Red Chilli placed vertically in the centre for visual contrast


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The garlic tastes bitter.

Solution:
The garlic should be Brown, not Burnt.

If the garlic has turned black, the gravy will be spoiled.
Prepare a new batch.

Problem:
The spinach flavour feels dull or suppressed.

Solution:
Add a pinch of Lemon Juice.
This will immediately bring out the natural spinach flavour.


HELPER TIP

"Lehsunia Paneer means a powerful garlic flavour!

The kitchen should be filled with the aroma of garlic while this dish is cooking.

Chop the garlic finely. Do not chop it into large pieces."
