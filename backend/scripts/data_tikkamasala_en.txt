KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN TIKKA MASALA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (60%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
5–6 Pieces Tandoori Chicken/Tikka (70% Pre-cooked)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High for roasting the chicken.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Water or Chicken Stock to the pan so the chicken cooks through properly.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the butter and oil in the pan.
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste and finely chopped Green Chilli.
Allow them to sizzle for approximately 10 seconds.

Step 2 - Tikka Roasting:
Add the Tandoori Chicken/Tikka pieces.
Sprinkle 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE.
Toss on high flame for approximately 1 minute.
This allows the smoky chicken flavour to blend into the gravy.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 1.5 ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

G-201 SUNSET BASE provides smoothness, while G-205 ROYAL ROGAN provides a spicy and masaledar texture.

Step 4 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A pinch of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

Step 5 - Simmering:
Reduce the flame to low.
Cover the pan and cook for 2–3 minutes so that the chicken becomes completely soft and cooked through.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Thick and Luscious, with a smooth and glossy appearance.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Fresh Cream and Kasoori Methi crushed between your hands.

Final Touch:
Add 1/2 tsp Butter and finely chopped Fresh Coriander.
Mix rapidly on high flame for approximately 10 seconds so that the oil releases.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Ceramic Bowl or Copper Handi.

Garnish:
- One spiral of Fresh Cream
- Ginger Juliennes
- One fresh Coriander Leaf in the centre


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Chicken pieces are hard or chewy.

Solution:
Add a little extra water to the gravy and cook the chicken on low flame until it becomes soft.

Problem: Gravy is too tangy.

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp White Gravy or a small amount of Cream and Sugar to balance the flavour.


HELPER TIP

"Chicken Tikka Masala means Smoky & Creamy.
Make sure the charred marks from the Tandoori Chicken remain visible in the gravy.
That smoky char is an essential characteristic of the dish."
