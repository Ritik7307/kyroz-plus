KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PANEER PASANDA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-202 IVORY BASE (80%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
Paneer Sandwiches (Stuffed Paneer)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.
G-202 IVORY BASE burns quickly, so take care.

Base:
1/2 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating:
Add 30–40 ml Water or Milk to the pan.
Milk gives extra richness.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Paneer Preparation:

Make a slit in the middle of the Paneer Triangle pieces.

Fill them with:
- A small amount of G-201 SUNSET BASE
- A little K-806 ZESTFUL ZING
- Finely Chopped Fresh Coriander

Lightly pan-fry the stuffed paneer pieces if possible.

Step 2 - Aroma Base:

Heat the Butter and Oil in the pan.

Add:
- 1/4 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Roast until the raw smell disappears.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 2 large ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 1/2 ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE

This creates a light "Peach/Pink" coloured gravy, which is the identity of the dish.

Step 4 - Balancing:

If the gravy tastes too sweet, add:
- A pinch of Salt
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

Use very little Red Chilli so that the colour is not affected.

Step 5 - Paneer Addition:

Gently place the prepared Paneer Sandwiches into the gravy so that they do not break.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely Velvety and Smooth.

The Secret:
Mix in:
- 1 tbsp Whisked Curd or Fresh Cream

Top with:
- A little Crushed Kasoori Methi
- K-801 ROYAL PUNCH

Flame:
Keep on very Low Flame for 30 seconds, then switch off the gas.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on an Oval or Round White Plate/Bowl.

Garnish:
- A little Grated Paneer
- One small drop/design of G-201 SUNSET BASE in the centre
- Ginger Juliennes
- One Fresh Coriander Leaf


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy has become too sweet.

Solution:
Add a little Chaat Masala and 1 tbsp Whisked Curd.

Problem:
The gravy is curdling.

Solution:
Immediately reduce the flame to Low.
Add a little Hot Water and whisk/stir quickly until the gravy becomes smooth again.


HELPER TIP

"Paneer Pasanda is a Royal dish.

Keep the spicy masalas low and the creaminess high.

Handle the Paneer pieces very gently so that the stuffing does not come out."
