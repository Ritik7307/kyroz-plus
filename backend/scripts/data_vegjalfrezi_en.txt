KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: VEG JALFREZI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-204 ROASTED RUST (60%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
Mixed Vegetables — Beans, Carrot, Cauliflower, Peas, Paneer Sticks
70% Steamed/Blanched


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at High.
High heat is required to properly toss the vegetables.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil.

Reheating:
Add 20 ml Water to the pan.
Use only to loosen the gravies.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Vegetable Sauté:

Heat the Oil in the pan.

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- A small amount of K-802 WOK SPICE

Step 2 - Sauté the Vegetables:

Add the blanched Mixed Vegetables and long Paneer Sticks.

Sauté quickly on High Flame for 1 minute.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 1.5 Ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST
- 1 Ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

Step 4 - Tangy Twist:

Add:
- 1 tbsp Curd
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

The Jalfrezi should have a slightly sweet-sour and spicy taste.

Step 5 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli — for a good red colour
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The dish should be Semi-Dry and the gravy should properly coat the vegetables.

There should be no watery gravy running at the bottom of the bowl.

The Secret:
Add:
- A pinch of Sugar
- 1 tsp Vinegar OR Lemon Juice

This gives the dish its authentic Indo-English Jalfrezi taste.

Final Flame:
Toss on High Flame for 20 seconds so that the gravy develops a shiny coating over the vegetables.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on an Oval Platter or in a Glass Bowl.

Garnish:
- Long-cut Ginger Juliennes
- Finely Chopped Fresh Coriander
- 2 Long Slit Green Chillies placed on top


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The vegetables have become too soft or mushy.

Solution:
Always steam the vegetables only up to 70%.
After adding them to the gravy, do not over-stir or overcook them.

Problem:
The dish feels too dry.

Solution:
Add 1 tbsp G-205 ROYAL ROGAN and a little Butter.
Mix quickly.


HELPER TIP

"Jalfrezi means Crunchy Veggies.

The gravy should only coat the vegetables.
It should not run to the bottom of the bowl.

Capsicum and Onion chunks should be clearly visible in the dish."
