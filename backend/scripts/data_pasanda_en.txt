KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN PASANDA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-202 IVORY BASE (70%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
Chicken Breast Fillets (Flattened/Hammered)
OR
Boneless Tikka (180 g)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.
G-202 IVORY BASE can stick to the bottom quickly.

Base:
1.5 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1 tsp Butter

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Milk to the pan.
Do not use water.
Milk helps maintain the richness of the dish.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Marination:
Lightly hammer and flatten the chicken breast fillets.
Marinate them with Ginger-Garlic Paste and Curd.
If the chicken is already cooked, use it directly.

Step 2 - Aroma Base:
Heat the Ghee and Butter in the pan.
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- 2 Green Cardamom
- 1 small piece of Cinnamon

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 1 large ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE

The mixture should develop a Pale Orange/Cream colour.

Step 4 - The Nutty Touch:
Add 1 tsp Almond Paste or finely crushed Almonds.

Step 5 - Spices:
Keep the spice level very mild.
Add:
- 1/4 tsp White Pepper Powder
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A pinch of Cardamom Powder


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely Smooth and Thick, with a glossy, varnish-like appearance.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Whisked Curd
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream

The curd provides a slight sweet-and-sour balance.

Sweet Balance:
Add a pinch of Sugar or 1/2 tsp Honey.
Chicken Pasanda should have a mildly sweet flavour profile.

Flame:
Cook on Low Flame for approximately 2 minutes.
This allows the chicken fillets to become very soft and tender.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on a Flat White Ceramic Plate or in a Royal Bowl.

Garnish:
- Silver Foil (Edible Silver Leaf) for premium serving
- Slivered Almonds
- Slivered Pistachios
- Fresh Cream Zigzag Design
- 1–2 strands of Saffron on top


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy is too thick.

Solution:
Add a small amount of hot milk and whisk until the required consistency is achieved.

Problem: Dish tastes too bland.

Solution:
Add a pinch of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING and a little extra Butter.


HELPER TIP

"Chicken Pasanda is a delicate and refined dish.
There should be no large visible whole spices and the dish should not be overly spicy.
The chicken pieces should be flat and extremely tender, almost melting in the mouth."
