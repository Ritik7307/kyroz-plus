KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MUTTON CURRY

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

MAIN ITEM:
80% Pre-cooked Mutton Pieces
(Boiled with Mutton Stock)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.
Mutton tastes best with the flavour of Desi Ghee.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
60 ml Rich Mutton Stock.
Use the remaining water from pre-cooking the mutton to provide authentic mutton flavour and depth.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Royal Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

Add:
- 1 piece Whole Cinnamon Stick
- 2 Green Cardamoms
- 1/2 tsp Cumin

As soon as the spices begin to crackle, add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Roast quickly for approximately 15 seconds so that the raw smell disappears.

Step 2 - Base & Meat Integration:

Add 2 Standard Ladles of G-205 ROYAL ROGAN to the pan.

Add:
- 60 ml Hot Mutton Stock
- Pre-cooked Mutton Pieces

Reduce the flame to Slow-Medium and allow the curry to simmer for approximately 2 minutes.

This allows the strong gravy flavour to penetrate the mutton fibres properly.

Step 3 - Spices & Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
  For the red tari and colour.

- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
  For rich Mughlai aromatic depth.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick and slightly Grainy.
It should lightly coat the mutton pieces, with a beautiful Deep Red Rogan floating on top.

Aroma Lock:
Add hand-crushed Kasoori Methi.

Final Flame:
Toss quickly on High Flame for 20–30 seconds until the ghee and meat fat begin to float on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium Flat Ceramic Plate, Round Handi, or Traditional Clay Pot.

Garnish:
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Thin and long Ginger Juliennes
- Pour the hot Rogan released from the sides of the pan over the top before serving.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Mutton pieces feel slightly tight or hard while chewing.

Solution:
During pre-cooking, give the mutton a few more whistles.

During service, cover the mutton lightly and cook it with the gravy and stock on Slow Flame for at least 2–3 minutes.

This will make the meat soft and melt-in-the-mouth.

Problem:
The gravy has become too thin or watery.

Solution:
Increase the flame to High and bhunao without the lid for approximately 30 seconds.

The stock will reduce and the original thick appearance of G-205 ROYAL ROGAN will return.


HELPER TIP

"The real taste of Mutton Curry comes from the tari of G-205 ROYAL ROGAN and the roasted aroma of K-801 ROYAL PUNCH.

Before sending the mutton to the counter, always check that the Rogan is shining on the meat and that the ghee is floating on top."
