KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN KALI MIRCH

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-202 IVORY BASE

MAIN ITEM:
70–80% Pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken Pieces
OR
Chicken Tikka

The smoky flavour of Tandoori Chicken/Tikka pairs best with this dish.


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium-Low.
The cashew-based gravy can burn if cooked on high flame.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Milk or White Chicken Stock.
This helps maintain richness and a smooth texture.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Aromatic Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

Add:
- 2 Green Cardamoms
- 1 Slit Green Chilli
- 1/2 tsp Whole Black Pepper

Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste.
Stir lightly for only 10–15 seconds.

IMPORTANT:
The colour of the paste should not change.
Only the raw smell should disappear.

Step 2 - Base & Meat Integration:

Add 2 Standard Ladles of G-202 IVORY BASE to the pan.

Add:
- 40 ml Milk or White Chicken Stock

Allow the gravy to simmer on low flame.

Add the pre-cooked Chicken/Tikka pieces.
Coat them gently with the gravy and cook for approximately 1 minute so that the chicken heats properly from inside.

Step 3 - Spices & Black Pepper Punch:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Freshly Coarse-Crushed Black Pepper
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli or Turmeric.
The premium off-white colour of the dish must be maintained.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be silky, smooth, velvety and thick enough to coat the chicken pieces properly.

Finishing Secret:
Add:
- 1.5 tbsp Fresh Cream
- A pinch of hand-crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Cook on Low Flame for approximately 20 seconds until a small amount of ghee starts floating on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium Deep White Ceramic Bowl or Royal Handi.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream swirl
- A pinch of Freshly Crushed Black Pepper
- Thin and long Ginger Juliennes


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The black pepper has become too sharp or spicy.

Solution:
Immediately add 1 tbsp extra Fresh Cream or 2 tbsp Hot Milk.
Cook for another 10 seconds.
This will smooth out the sharpness.

Problem:
The gravy is sticking to the bottom of the pan.

Solution:
Immediately reduce the flame to Low.
Add 2 tbsp Cold Milk and stir quickly.
The texture should return to normal.


HELPER TIP

"Chicken Kali Mirch tastes best when the premium creamy colour of G-202 IVORY BASE is maintained and every bite has the sharp punch of freshly crushed black pepper.

Measure K-801 ROYAL PUNCH accurately and always finish the plate with cream and black pepper before sending it to the counter."
