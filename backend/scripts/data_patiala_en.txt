KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MURG PATIALA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-202 IVORY BASE (60%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
70% Pre-cooked Chicken (Boneless or Curry Cut)
+
Egg Omelette (Optional for Stuffing/Topping)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.
G-202 IVORY BASE can stick to the bottom quickly, so take care.

Base:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Water or Chicken Stock to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, finely chopped Green Chilli and Ginger Juliennes to the hot ghee.
Roast for approximately 10 seconds.

Step 2 - Chicken Sauté:
Add the pre-cooked Chicken pieces.
Mix quickly on high flame for approximately 1 minute so that the ghee flavour gets absorbed into the chicken.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 1.5 ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

G-202 IVORY BASE provides a Rich and Nutty taste.
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN provides Spice and Colour.

Step 4 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-802 WOK SPICE for an extra punch.

Step 5 - Simmering:
Reduce the flame to low.
Cover the pan and cook for approximately 2 minutes until the chicken becomes completely soft.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Smooth, Thick and Creamy.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Whisked Curd
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream

Sprinkle crushed Kasoori Methi over the top.

The Patiala Touch:
Patiala Chicken often has the texture of a chopped onion-tomato masala.
This texture will be provided by G-205 ROYAL ROGAN.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Deep Ceramic Bowl or Handi.

Garnish:

Traditional Style:
Prepare a thin omelette and wrap it over the dish.
Alternatively, cut the omelette into thin strips and place them on top.

Standard Style:
Add plenty of Fresh Cream, Ginger Juliennes and Fresh Coriander.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Gravy looks too White.

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp G-205 ROYAL ROGAN and a pinch of Kashmiri Chilli.
Adjust the colour until it becomes Golden-Yellow.

Problem:
Gravy is sticking to the pan.

Solution:
Immediately add a little Water or Milk and reduce the flame to very low.


HELPER TIP

"Murg Patiala is a Heavy and Royal dish.

Its texture should be as creamy as Malai, while the flavour of the spices should still be noticeable.

Do not be stingy with the use of Ghee."
