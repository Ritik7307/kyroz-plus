KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MALAI KOFTA RED
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (60%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
2–3 Paneer & Potato Koftas (Stuffed with Dry Fruits) – Deep Fried


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.
This prevents the gravy from burning.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Water to the pan.
The koftas will absorb some gravy, so slightly extra water is required.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:

Heat the butter in the pan.

Add:
- 1/4 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Roast for 10 seconds.

Step 2 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 2 large ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 1.5 ladles G-202 IVORY BASE

This creates a rich Deep Orange/Reddish colour.

Step 3 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli — for colour
- A pinch of K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING — for tanginess

Step 4 - Balancing:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Sugar or Honey

Malai Kofta should have a mildly sweet and creamy taste.

Step 5 - IMPORTANT RULE:

Never cook the koftas inside the pan.

Keep the koftas separately on a plate and place them over the gravy only while serving.

The kofta may break apart if cooked directly in the gravy.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely smooth, thick and velvety.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- Crushed Kasoori Methi

Flame:
When bubbles start appearing in the gravy and butter begins to separate from the sides, switch off the flame.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
First pour the hot Red Gravy into a deep bowl.

Carefully place the koftas on top of the gravy.

Garnish:
- Make a zigzag design with Fresh Cream over the koftas.
- Add a little Grated Paneer or finely chopped Fresh Coriander.
- Add one Cherry or a piece of Cashew on top (Optional).


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy tastes too tangy or sour.

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp G-202 IVORY BASE and slightly increase the Cream/Sugar.


Problem:
The kofta breaks as soon as it is placed on the plate.

Solution:
Always place the kofta over the gravy only at the time of serving.
Do not put the kofta inside the gravy and boil it.


HELPER TIP

"Malai Kofta Red gets its authentic taste from its velvety, smooth gravy.

If the gravy does not look completely smooth, strain it through a strainer before serving."
