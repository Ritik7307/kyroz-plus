KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MUTTON BHUNA GOSHT
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (70%)
G-204 ROASTED RUST (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
4–5 Pieces Mutton (Preferably Shoulder/Raan)
80% Pre-cooked (Slow cooked with whole spices)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame High.
High heat is essential for proper Bhunai.

Base:
1.5 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 30–40 ml Mutton Stock (Yakhni) to the pan.
Use less water because stock provides better flavour.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, 2 Slit Green Chillies and a small amount of Whole Spices to the hot ghee.

Whole Spices:
- 1 Black Cardamom
- 2-inch Cinnamon
- 2 Cloves

Step 2 - The Sauté:
Add the pre-cooked Mutton pieces.
Roast on high flame in ghee for 2–3 minutes until a light brown layer develops on the meat.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1.5 ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST

G-204 ROASTED RUST provides the chopped Onion-Tomato texture that works well for Bhuna Gosht.

Spices:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE for extra punch

Step 4 - THE BHUNAI (CRUCIAL):
Reduce the flame to Medium.
Keep stirring continuously until the gravy becomes completely dry and starts coating the meat.

The gravy should develop a Dark Brown colour.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Extra Thick / Semi-Dry.
It should not run on the serving plate.

The Secret:
When the gravy starts drying, add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd.
This gives the masala a shine and a slight tang that balances the flavour of the meat.

Final Touch:
Add 1 tsp Desi Ghee on top and sprinkle Kasoori Methi.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on a Flat Ceramic Plate or in an Iron Karahi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of Ginger Juliennes
- Fresh Coriander
- Lemon Wedge on the side
- Onion Rings / Lachha Pyaaz


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Mutton pieces feel dry.

Solution:
While bhunaoing, add a little Mutton Stock or Ghee.
The meat should remain soft and tender, not hard.

Problem:
Gravy colour is too light or pale.

Solution:
Increase the flame and continue the bhunai until the masala becomes Burnished Brown.


HELPER TIP

"Bhuna Gosht means Patience.

The longer you roast it in ghee, the deeper and more earthy the flavour will become.

The masala should be properly absorbed into the meat."
