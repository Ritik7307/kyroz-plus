# KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

## PRODUCT: PREMIUM PINK SAUCE PASTA

---

## 1. MEASUREMENT

For the perfect Pink Sauce, use a **60:40 ratio of White Sauce to Red Sauce** to maintain the right balance of creaminess and spice.

| Item                         |        Quantity — 1 Portion |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------: |
| Boiled Pasta (Penne/Fusilli) |                   180–200 g |
| Full-Cream Milk              |                      150 ml |
| Water                        |                       50 ml |
| C-505 ALFREDO CORE           |               30 g (2 tbsp) |
| C-506 MARINARA CORE          |             20 g (1.5 tbsp) |
| Butter/Oil                   |                        10 g |
| Vegetables                   | Sweet Corn, Capsicum, Onion |

---

## 2. COOKING STEPS

### Step 1 — Sauté Vegetables

Heat butter or oil in a pan. Add the vegetables and sauté on high heat for approximately **1 minute**.

### Step 2 — Prepare the Liquid Base

Add **150 ml full-cream milk** and **50 ml water** to the pan.

### Step 3 — Add the Pink Sauce Mix

Add **30 g C-505 ALFREDO CORE** and **20 g C-506 MARINARA CORE** together.

### Step 4 — Blend the Sauce

Whisk the sauce over medium heat until both powders are completely blended. The sauce should develop a smooth **orange-pink colour** and become slightly thick.

### Step 5 — Add the Pasta

Once the sauce begins to simmer, add the **boiled pasta**.

### Step 6 — Final Cooking

Cook for **1–2 minutes**, stirring gently until the pasta is evenly coated with the sauce.

Serve hot.

---

## 3. IMPORTANT HELPER ADVICE

### Colour Rule

* If the sauce looks too white, increase the red premix by approximately **5 g**.
* If the sauce looks too red, add a small amount of milk or white premix to balance the colour.

### No Extra Salt

* Both premixes already contain salt.
* **Do not add additional salt** unless specifically required by the standard recipe.

### Creamy Texture

* If the customer requests an **extra-rich pasta**, add approximately **1 tablespoon of fresh cream or one cheese slice** at the finishing stage.

### Cold Mixing

* To prevent lumps, mix the powders thoroughly with the milk and water before applying high heat.
* Ensure the powders are properly dispersed before the sauce becomes hot.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem           | Possible Reason                                                                           | Solution                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sauce is curdling | The acidity of the red sauce can cause the milk to curdle when exposed to excessive heat. | Mix the powders into the milk and water first. Then place on heat and stir continuously.        |
| Sauce is too dark | Too much red premix was added.                                                            | Add a small amount of milk or white premix to balance the colour.                               |
| Pasta is too dry  | The pasta has absorbed too much sauce.                                                    | Add approximately 2 tablespoons of hot milk while serving and mix gently to restore creaminess. |

---

## 5. PRESENTATION TIP

For a premium café-style presentation:

* Garnish the Pink Sauce Pasta with **black olives**.
* Add a light sprinkle of **C-509 FIRE DUST** on top.
* Serve immediately while hot.
