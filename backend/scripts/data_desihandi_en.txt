KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: AUTHENTIC DESI HANDI
CHICKEN / MUTTON

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT
NO-MAKHNI VERSION

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (70%)
G-204 ROASTED RUST (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
300 g Chicken or Mutton (Pre-cooked)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High for proper bhunai.

Base:
1.5 tbsp Desi Ghee.
Skip butter if a 100% Desi-style preparation is required.

Reheating:
Add 50 ml Meat/Chicken Stock or Water.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Tadka (The Soul):
Heat the desi ghee and add:
- 1 Bay Leaf
- 2 Black Cardamoms
- 3–4 Cloves
- 1 tsp Shahi Jeera

When the whole spices begin to crackle, add 1 tsp finely chopped Garlic.

Step 2 - Base Bhunai:
Add 1 tbsp finely chopped Onion.
Cook until the onion becomes golden brown.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 3 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1 ladle G-204 ROASTED RUST

G-204 ROASTED RUST provides the required grainy texture.

Step 4 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Chicken or Mutton.

Step 5 - Desi-Style Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli Powder for colour
- 1/2 tsp Crushed Black Pepper
- 1/2 tsp Coriander Powder
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH


3. FINISHING - THE AUTHENTIC WAY

The Tang:
Add 2 tbsp whisked Curd and stir quickly so that the curd does not split.

Curd provides richness and the characteristic tang associated with Handi.

Consistency:
The gravy should have a Thick Coating consistency.
Ghee should start separating from the sides.

Aroma:
Add a handful of Fresh Coriander and Ginger Juliennes.
Mix well.

Final Flame:
Cover the pan with a lid and cook on Dum for the final 1 minute.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in an Earthen Handi.
This helps provide the characteristic earthy aroma.

Garnish:
- Fresh Coriander
- Long-cut Green Chilli
- 1 tbsp Desi Ghee on top

IMPORTANT:
Do not add Cream or Grated Paneer.


5. CHEF'S TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy has become too dry.

Solution:
Add a small amount of hot stock or water.
Make sure the gravy does not become too thin or turn into a curry.

Problem: Flavour is bland.

Solution:
Add a small amount of Roasted Cumin Powder and check and adjust the Salt.


6. WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT?

Removing the Makhni element gives the dish a Deep Brown/Red colour instead of Orange.

The flavour profile shifts from the sweetness of tomatoes to the sharper, roasted flavour of Onion and Curd.

This is the signature character of an authentic North Indian / Dhaba-style Handi.
