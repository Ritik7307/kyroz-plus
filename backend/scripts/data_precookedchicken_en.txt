KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PRE-COOKED CHICKEN (CURRY CUT)

USE:
For Handi, Lababdar, Curry and Gravy Dishes

TARGET:
Chicken should remain soft and juicy and should not break apart when added to gravy.


1. PREPARATION (MISE-EN-PLACE)

Chicken Cut:
Use Curry Cut chicken (one whole chicken divided into 8 or 12 pieces).
Each piece should weigh approximately 80–100 g.

Washing:
Wash the chicken in cold water with a small amount of Salt and White Vinegar.
Leave it for 5 minutes, then wash with clean water.
This helps remove the unpleasant smell from the chicken.

Draining:
After washing, keep the chicken in a strainer for 10 minutes so that all excess water drains out.


2. COOKING PROCESS

Helper Instruction:
Follow Steps 1 to 5 carefully.

Step 1 - Whole Spices & Ghee:
In a large vessel (Lagan or Handi), heat:
- 2 tbsp Oil
- 1 tbsp Ghee

Add:
- 4–5 Bay Leaves
- 5 Black Cardamoms
- 2 pieces of Cinnamon

Step 2 - Ginger-Garlic Paste:
Add 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste.
Cook until the paste becomes lightly golden and aromatic.
Do not burn the paste.

Step 3 - Chicken Sauté (Bhunai):
Add the cleaned chicken pieces.
Cook on High Flame for 5–7 minutes until the chicken changes from Pink to Dull White.

WHY?
This helps seal the chicken and keeps it Juicy inside the gravy instead of becoming dry.

Step 4 - Poaching:
Add enough hot water to completely submerge the chicken.

Also add:
- 1 tsp Salt
  So the chicken does not remain bland inside.
- 1/2 tsp Turmeric
  For a light colour.

Step 5 - THE 80% RULE:

Once the water starts boiling, cover the vessel and cook for only 12–15 minutes.

CHECK:
Use a knife to check the leg piece.
The inside should remain slightly Pinkish.
It should NOT be completely cooked or falling apart.


3. COOLING & STORAGE

Crucial Step:
Immediately remove the chicken from the hot stock and spread the pieces on a clean Steel Tray.

Fan Cooling:
Keep the tray under a fan so that the steam escapes and the cooking process stops.

Stock:
Strain the remaining cooking liquid and store it separately.
This is the Chicken Stock.
Use it when reheating the gravy.

Fridge:
After the chicken has cooled, cover the tray and store it in the Chiller at 0°C to 4°C.


4. IMPORTANT HELPER POINTS

DO NOT OVERCOOK:
Never cook the chicken to 100%.
If the helper completely cooks the chicken, it may break apart and turn into shredded meat as soon as it is added to the gravy.

NO RED CHILLI:
Do not add Red Chilli during pre-cooking.
Otherwise, it can affect the colour of every dish.

HYGIENE:
Do not use dirty hands or a dirty spoon while handling the chicken.
This can cause the chicken to spoil.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Chicken has cooked too quickly.

Solution:
Immediately pour cold water over it and place it in the chiller.
When an order comes in, add this chicken to the gravy at the very end.

Problem:
Chicken has an unpleasant smell.

Solution:
This means there was an error during washing.
Next time, follow the White Vinegar washing step correctly.
