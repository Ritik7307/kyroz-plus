KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: BUTTER CHICKEN

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-201 SUNSET BASE

MAIN ITEM:
70–80% Pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken Pieces
OR
Chicken Tikka


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Amul Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.
The oil helps prevent the butter from burning.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
30 ml Milk or Chicken Stock.
This provides richness and smooth binding.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste and finely chopped Green Chilli to the hot butter-oil.
Sauté for 10–15 seconds to remove the raw smell and release fresh aroma.

Step 2 - Base Integration:
Add 2 Standard Ladles of G-201 SUNSET BASE to the pan.

Add 30 ml Milk or Chicken Stock and bring the gravy to a gentle simmer.

Step 3 - Meat Fusion:
Add the pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken or Chicken Tikka pieces.
Allow the smoky Tandoor flavour to blend with the gravy base.

Step 4 - Spices & Flavor Punch:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli — for bright colour
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH — for flavour depth
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING — for a light tangy punch and balance


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be silky, smooth and velvety.
It should coat the chicken properly.

The Velvet Secret:
Add 1.5 tbsp Fresh Cream and hand-crushed Kasoori Methi.

Sweet Balance:
Add a pinch of Sugar or 1/4 tsp Honey to balance the natural acidity of the tomato base.

Final Flame:
Cook on High Flame for only 20–30 seconds.
Roast quickly until the butter starts shining around the edges of the gravy.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium Round Handi or Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream swirl
- 1 small Butter Cube placed in the centre
- Finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Thin Ginger Juliennes


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Gravy tastes too sour.

Solution:
Add 1 tbsp extra Fresh Cream or slightly increase the Honey.
Cook for another 10 seconds.

Problem:
Gravy has become completely dry or is starting to set.

Solution:
Do NOT add chilled water.
Add 2 tbsp Hot Milk and stir gently.
The smooth texture will return.


HELPER TIP

"Butter Chicken is a Rich and Royal dish.

The smoothness of G-201 SUNSET BASE and the aroma of K-801 ROYAL PUNCH must remain perfectly balanced.

Before sending the dish to the counter, check that the cream and butter shine is clearly visible during plating."
