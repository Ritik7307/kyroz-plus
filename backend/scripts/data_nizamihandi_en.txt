KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: NIZAMI HANDI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-202 IVORY BASE (50%)
G-203 EMERALD MIX (30%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
Mix Veg (Beans, Carrot, Cauliflower)
OR
Chicken Tikka (Pieces) + Diced Capsicum & Onion


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.
Nizami dishes are best prepared with slow cooking.

Base:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 40 ml Milk to the pan.
For an authentic Nizami taste, use less water.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Heat the ghee and add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- 2–3 Slit Green Chillies
- A small amount of Shahi Jeera

Step 2 - Veg/Meat Sauté:
Add the blanched Mixed Vegetables or Chicken pieces.
Add diced Onion and Capsicum.
Lightly sauté for 1 minute.
Do not overcook the vegetables; they should retain their crunch.

Step 3 - The Royal Mix:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
  For richness and the main base.

- 1 ladle G-203 EMERALD MIX
  For a light spinach touch and green colour.

- 1/2 ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE
  For mild sweetness and colour balance.

Step 4 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/4 tsp White Pepper
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A pinch of Cardamom Powder

Do not use Red Chilli so that the dish maintains its Pastel Green/Yellow colour.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Thick and Creamy, similar to Malai.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- Crushed Kasoori Methi

The Final Touch:
Take 1 tbsp Fried Onions (Birista), crush them by hand and mix them into the gravy.
This gives the dish its characteristic Nizami depth.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in an Earthen Handi or Ceramic Deep Bowl.

Garnish:
- Sprinkle Brown Fried Onions (Birista) on top.
- Finely chopped Fresh Coriander.
- One spiral of Fresh Cream.
- A few Fried Cashews.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy has become too dark green.

Solution:
Add a little G-202 IVORY BASE and Fresh Cream.
Adjust the colour until it reaches a Pista/Creamy Green tone.

Problem:
The dish tastes too bland.

Solution:
Add a pinch of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING and a little Butter.
Toss on high flame for approximately 10 seconds.


HELPER TIP

"The real secret of Nizami Handi is Shahi Jeera and Birista (Fried Onion).

This dish should not be spicy.
It should be Rich and Flavorful.

The texture should be completely smooth, without visible whole spices, except for the Shahi Jeera."
