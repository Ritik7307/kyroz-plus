KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PANEER DHANIA ADRAKI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (70%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
Paneer Cubes (150 g – 180 g)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
This is required for properly roasting the ginger.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil + 1/2 tsp Ghee.

Reheating:
Add 30 ml Water to the pan.
This helps loosen the curry base.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:

Add to the hot oil and ghee:
- 1 tbsp Ginger Juliennes (long-cut ginger)
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Roast the ginger until it becomes lightly golden.

Step 2 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 2 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1 ladle G-202 IVORY BASE

G-202 IVORY BASE gives the dish a royal Yellowish-Brown appearance.

Step 3 - The Coriander Factor:

Add 1 tbsp finely chopped Fresh Coriander directly into the gravy.

This allows the coriander flavour to release properly.

Step 4 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

Step 5 - Paneer:

Add the Paneer Cubes.

Stir gently for 1 minute so that the paneer absorbs the gravy properly.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Thick.
It should be neither too thin nor dry.

The Secret:

10 seconds before switching off the flame, add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- A small amount of Kasoori Methi

Optional:
Sprinkle 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE on top for an extra flavour punch.

Final Flame:
Toss on High Flame for 10 seconds and switch off the flame.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Ceramic Bowl or Karahi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of Fresh Coriander on top
- A large bunch of Ginger Juliennes in the centre
- A light amount of Grated Paneer on the side


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The ginger tastes bitter.

Solution:
Do not burn the ginger.
Only cook it until it becomes light golden.

If the ginger has burnt, change the pan and prepare again.

Problem:
The dish has become too spicy.

Solution:
Add 1 tbsp G-202 IVORY BASE and some Fresh Cream to balance the flavour.


HELPER TIP

"This dish is called Dhaniya Adraki, so the customer should get ginger crunch and fresh coriander aroma in every bite.

Do not be stingy with the ginger and coriander."
