DOCUMENT 1: CHINESE DAILY KITCHEN SETUP SOP
ENGLISH

1. Morning Setup: The Master Station (Complete by 11:00 AM)

Helper should prepare and arrange all containers before service starts.

A. Liquid Bases (Kyroz Master Engines)

Keep these ready:

M-Base (Manchurian Base): 5 Litres (Dark / Earthy flavor)
C-Base (Chilli Base): 5 Litres (Garlicky / Sharp flavor)
S-Paste (Schezwan Paste): 2.5 kg (Red / Oily base)
H-Base (Sweet & Sour Base): 2 Litres (For Honey Chilli / Chopsuey)
M-Chutney (Momo Chutney): 2 Litres
B. Dry Station (Final Touch Section)

Keep these ready:

N-Core Powder: Small airtight container (for Hakka / Rice seasoning)
Cornflour Slurry: Large bowl (Cornflour + Water mixture)

Important Tip

Stir the slurry every 2 hours because cornflour settles at the bottom.
White Sesame Seeds & Honey: Keep ready for Honey Chilli Potato station
C. Vegetable & Protein Preparation

Prepare all ingredients in advance:

Vegetables

Julienne cut vegetables for noodles
Cube cut vegetables for chilli / chopsuey dishes

Fried Ready Items

Manchurian balls
Crispy fried noodles (nests)
Half-fried potato fingers (for double frying)
2. Order Time: 2-Minute Speed SOP

For every order, helper should follow these 4 steps:

Step 1: Identify

Check what dish is ordered:

Dry dish
Gravy dish
Rice
Noodles
Step 2: Select Base

Choose correct master base:

M Base
C Base
S Paste
H Base
Step 3: Toss
High flame wok cooking
Add base + vegetables + protein
Toss quickly
Step 4: Thickening
Add cornflour slurry
Cook until sauce becomes glossy and shiny
3. Daily Kitchen Checklist
Morning Checklist (Before 12:00 PM)

Check:

☐ All 4 master bases ready?
☐ Momo chutney ready?
☐ N-Core powder filled?

Check:

☐ Cornflour slurry discarded? (Fresh slurry must be made daily)

Use fixed ladle measurement system.

Standard Rule

DOCUMENT 2: AMERICAN CHOPSUEY SOP
ENGLISH

1. Crispy Noodle Nest Preparation

American Chopsuey is known for its crispy noodle nest base.

Preparation Steps
Take boiled noodles.
Lightly coat them with cornflour.
Spread the noodles in a round nest shape using a flat strainer or directly in a wok.
Deep fry in very hot oil until golden brown and completely crispy.

Storage Tip

Crispy noodle nests can be prepared in advance.
Store in an airtight container.
2. Gravy Preparation (Per Portion)
Ingredients
Refined Oil: 2 tbsp
Mixed Vegetables (Julienne Cut): 1 cup
Carrot
Capsicum
Onion
Baby Corn
Mushroom
Water: 350 ml
Kyroz Z-105 TANGY COAT Powder: 2 tbsp
Tomato Ketchup: 2 tbsp
Pineapple Pieces (Optional): 2–3 pieces
Cornflour Slurry: 3–4 tbsp
3. Cooking Steps
Step 1: Vegetable Toss
Heat oil in wok.
Add all vegetables.
Stir fry on high flame for 1 minute.
Vegetables should remain crunchy.
Step 2: Sauce Base
Add 350 ml water.
Add:
Z-105 TANGY COAT powder
Tomato ketchup
Mix properly and bring to boil.
Step 3: Thickening
Add cornflour slurry slowly.
Cook until gravy becomes:
Thick
Glossy
Shiny

Important Rule

American Chopsuey gravy should be thicker than Manchurian gravy.
Step 4: Assembly
Place crispy noodle nest on serving plate.
Pour hot gravy over the noodles.
Step 5: Signature Garnish

Optional premium topping:

Half fried egg (Bullseye)
or
Boiled egg

Serve immediately.

4. Troubleshooting

Noodles becoming soft

Reason: Gravy was poured too early.
Fix: Always pour gravy only at serving time.

Gravy too sour

Reason: Too much Z-105 powder.
Fix: Add extra ketchup and a little sugar.

Oil smell in noodles

Reason: Old fried noodles used.
Fix: Use fresh noodles or airtight stored fresh batch.

Color looks orange instead of red

Reason: Less ketchup used.
Fix: Add 1 tbsp extra ketchup.
5. Kitchen Rules
Gravy Consistency Rule
Gravy should flow between noodles and reach plate base.
But it should NOT be watery.
Vegetable Rule
Dish should be loaded with vegetables.
Serving Rule
Always serve in:
Large plate
Shallow bowl

This improves presentation.

DOCUMENT 3: DRY & GRAVY CHILLI SOP
ENGLISH

1. Preparation (Base Setup)

Dish Variants Covered

Chilli Paneer
Chilli Chicken
Chilli Mushroom
Chilli Soya Chaap
Dry Chilli
Gravy Chilli

Required Ingredients

Fried protein (Paneer / Chicken / Mushroom / Soya Chaap)
Onion (cube cut)
Capsicum (cube cut)
Cornflour slurry
Oil
Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Base
2. Cooking Method
Step 1: Vegetable Toss
Heat 1–2 tbsp oil in a wok.
Add cube-cut onion and capsicum.
Stir fry on high flame for 20–30 seconds.
Vegetables should remain crunchy.
Step 2: Choose Style

For Dry Chilli

Add 100 ml Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Base (half ladle)

For Gravy Chilli

Add 250–300 ml Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Base (1 full ladle)

Bring to boil.

Step 3: Thickening
Slowly add cornflour slurry.
Stir continuously.
Sauce should become:
Glossy
Thick
Shiny
Step 4: Final Toss
Add fried protein.
Toss on high flame for 20–30 seconds.

Dry Style Rule

Sauce should only coat the protein.

Gravy Style Rule

Sauce should remain flowing in the bowl.
3. Quick Guide by Variant
Chilli Paneer
Use lightly batter-fried paneer cubes.
Chilli Chicken
Use crispy fried boneless chicken chunks.
Chilli Mushroom
Use batter-fried mushroom halves.
Chilli Soya Chaap
Use fried chaap pieces.
4. Helper Advice

Crunch Rule

Never overcook onion and capsicum.
Chilli dishes must have crunch.

No Delay Rule

Dry chilli should be served immediately.
Otherwise coating becomes soggy.

Extra Spicy Option

Add extra green chilli sauce if customer asks.
5. Troubleshooting

Dish tastes bland

Reason: Base quantity less.
Fix: Add 1 pinch Z-102 powder.

Too spicy

Reason: Too much chilli sauce.
Fix: Add ketchup or butter.

Sauce not sticking

Reason: Cornflour slurry less.
Fix: Add a little more slurry.

Color too dark

Reason: Too much soy sauce in base.
Fix: Add warm water + red chilli sauce.
