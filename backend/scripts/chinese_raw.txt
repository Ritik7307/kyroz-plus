CHINESE DAILY KITCHEN SETUP

This uploaded file is the Chinese Daily Kitchen Setup / Master Station SOP.

ENGLISH VERSION
1. MORNING SETUP — THE “MASTER STATION”

The helper must prepare and arrange the following containers at the station by 11:00 AM.

A. LIQUID BASES — THE KYROZ ENGINES
M-Base (Manchurian): 5 litres — Dark/Earthy
C-Base (Chilli): 5 litres — Garlicky/Sharp
S-Paste (Schezwan): 2.5 kg — Red/Oily
H-Base (Sweet & Sour): 2 litres — for Honey Chilli / Chopsuey
M-Chutney: 2 litres — for Momos
B. DRY STATION — THE FINAL TOUCH
N-Core Powder: Keep in a small airtight container. Used for Hakka/Rice seasoning.
Cornflour Slurry: Keep in a large bowl. Prepare using cornflour + water. Stir every 2 hours.
White Sesame & Honey: Keep at the Honey Chilli Potato station.
C. VEGGIE & PROTEIN PREPARATION
Vegetables: Long-cut for noodles and cube-cut for Chilli/Chopsuey dishes.
Fried Items: Manchurian Balls, Crispy Noodles (Nest), and Half-fried Potato Fingers should be ready for double frying.
2. ORDER TIME — 2-MINUTE SPEED CHECKLIST

When an order arrives, the helper only needs to follow these 4 steps:

Step 1 — Identify

Identify what the order is:

Dry
Gravy
Rice
Noodles
Step 2 — Pick the Base

Select the correct Master Liquid:

M — Manchurian
C — Chilli
S — Schezwan
H — Sweet & Sour
Step 3 — Toss

On high flame, mix the selected base with vegetables and protein in the wok.

Step 4 — Thickening

Add cornflour slurry and cook until the dish develops a shiny appearance.

3. DAILY KITCHEN CHECKLIST

The checklist should be laminated and placed near the kitchen door or above the station.

The helper must complete the morning sign-off.

MORNING CHECKLIST — BEFORE 12:00 PM
 Are all 4 Master Bases — M, C, S, H — and Momo Chutney ready?
 Is the N-Core Powder container full?
 Is all vegetable cutting — Julienne & Cubes — complete?
 Are Manchurian Balls and Fried Noodles ready?
 Are finely chopped Garlic and Ginger ready?
CLOSING CHECKLIST — AFTER 11:00 PM
 Are all remaining Liquid Bases stored in the refrigerator?
 Has the Wok and Gas Station been deep-cleaned?
 Which Kyroz Premix is running low for the next day? Order from Aroma Agro.
 Has the Cornflour Slurry been discarded? A fresh batch must be prepared every day.
4. KYROZ SPEED TIP — “LADLE SYSTEM”

The helper should use the Ladle System (Donga).

1 Full Ladle = Gravy Dish
½ Ladle = Dry Dish

This eliminates the need to measure using soup bowls or small bowls and is intended to double preparation speed.

12. KYROZ+ 5-STAR HOT & SOUR SOUP

ENGLISH VERSION

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Category: Soup

Base Used: Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID



1. INGREDIENTS — PER PORTION / BOWL

| Ingredient                           |         Quantity |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------: |
| **Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID** |           120 ml |
| **Plain Water**                      |           180 ml |
| **Finely Chopped Vegetables**        |           2 tbsp |
| **Vinegar**                          |            ½ tsp |
| **Red Chilli Paste / Green Chilli**  | ½ tsp — optional |
| **Cornflour Slurry**                 |         2–3 tbsp |
| **Spring Onion**                     |      For garnish |

Suggested Vegetables

* Carrot
* Beans
* Cabbage
* Mushroom
* Bamboo shoot / Paneer cubes 

2. COOKING METHOD — 3-MINUTE PROCESS

Step 1 — Sauté the Vegetables

Heat a small amount of oil in the wok.

Add all the chopped vegetables and sauté on **high flame for 20 seconds**.

Do not overcook them. They should remain **slightly crunchy**. 

Step 2 — Add the Liquid

Add:

* **120 ml Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID**
* **180 ml plain water**

Mix properly. 

Step 3 — Add the Sourness

Once the soup comes to a boil, add **½ tsp vinegar**.

This gives the soup its characteristic **sour/tangy flavour**. 

Step 4 — Thicken

Gradually add the **cornflour slurry**.

Hot & Sour Soup should be slightly **thicker than Manchow Soup**, while still looking clear. 

Step 5 — Colour Check

The ideal colour is **clear reddish-brown**.

If a slightly brighter red colour is required, add **1 drop of Red Chilli Sauce**. 

Step 6 — Serve

Transfer the soup to the serving bowl and garnish with **Spring Onion**.

**Do not add fried noodles** to Hot & Sour Soup. 

3. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Soup Is Too Sour

**Reason:** Too much vinegar was added.

**Solution:** Add **½ tsp sugar** and mix well to balance the taste. 

Problem: Soup Is Cloudy

**Reason:** Cornflour was not cooked properly.

**Solution:** Boil the soup on high flame for **30 seconds**, until it becomes **transparent and shiny**. 

Problem: Soup Is Not Spicy Enough

**Reason:** The chilli base has become diluted.

**Solution:** Sprinkle **1 pinch of Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE POWDER** directly into the soup. 

Problem: Flavour Feels Flat

**Reason:** Salt/umami is insufficient.

**Solution:** Add **¼ tsp soya sauce** or **1 pinch MSG**. 

4. KYROZ VARIATION — LEMON CORIANDER SOUP

The same base can be used to prepare **Lemon Coriander Soup**. 

Step 1

Keep the same ratio of **Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID and water**. 

Step 2

**Do not add soya sauce.**

The liquid base will already provide a relatively light colour. 

Step 3

At the finishing stage, add:

* **1 tbsp fresh lemon juice**
* Plenty of **fresh chopped coriander**

This produces a fresh, garlicky soup.

| **सिरका (Vinegar)**                  |            ½ छोटा चम्मच |
| **Red Chilli Paste / Green Chilli**  | ½ छोटा चम्मच — वैकल्पिक |
| **Cornflour Slurry**                 |          2–3 बड़े चम्मच |
| **Spring Onion / हरा प्याज़**        |          Garnish के लिए |

इस्तेमाल की जाने वाली सब्जियाँ

* गाजर
* Beans
* पत्ता गोभी
* Mushroom
* Bamboo shoot / Paneer cubes 

2. बनाने की विधि — 3-MINUTE PROCESS

चरण 1 — सब्जियाँ Sauté करें

Wok में थोड़ा तेल गर्म करें।

सारी कटी हुई सब्जियाँ डालकर **तेज़ आंच पर 20 सेकंड** तक sauté करें।

सब्जियों को ज्यादा नहीं पकाना है। उन्हें **हल्का crunchy** रहना चाहिए। 

चरण 2 — Liquid डालें

डालें:

* **120 मिली Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID**
* **180 मिली सादा पानी**

अच्छी तरह मिलाएँ। 

चरण 3 — खट्टापन डालें

जब Soup में उबाल आने लगे, तब **½ छोटा चम्मच Vinegar** डालें।

इससे Soup में इसका मुख्य **खट्टा/Tangy flavour** आएगा। 

चरण 4 — गाढ़ा करें

धीरे-धीरे **Cornflour Slurry** डालें।

Hot & Sour Soup, Manchow Soup की तुलना में थोड़ी **ज्यादा गाढ़ी**, लेकिन देखने में **clear** होनी चाहिए। 

चरण 5 — Colour Check

सही रंग **Clear Reddish-Brown** होना चाहिए।

अगर थोड़ा ज्यादा लाल रंग चाहिए, तो **1 बूंद Red Chilli Sauce** डाल सकते हैं। 

चरण 6 — Serve करें

Soup को serving bowl में निकालें और ऊपर से **Spring Onion / हरा प्याज़** डालें।

**Hot & Sour Soup में Fried Noodles नहीं डालने हैं।** 

3. समस्या और समाधान

समस्या: Soup बहुत खट्टा है

**कारण:** Vinegar ज्यादा डाल दिया गया।

**समाधान:** **½ छोटा चम्मच Sugar** डालकर अच्छी तरह मिलाएँ और स्वाद को balance करें। 

समस्या: Soup Cloudy / मटमैला है

**कारण:** Cornflour ठीक से नहीं पका।

**समाधान:** Soup को तेज़ आंच पर **30 सेकंड** तक उबालें, जब तक वह **Transparent/Shiny** न दिखाई देने लगे। 

समस्या: Soup में तीखापन कम है

**कारण:** Chilli Base dilute हो गया है।

**समाधान:** Soup के ऊपर **1 चुटकी Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE POWDER** सीधे sprinkle करें। 

समस्या: Flavour फीका/Flat लग रहा है

**कारण:** Salt/Umami कम है।

**समाधान:** **¼ छोटा चम्मच Soya Sauce** या **1 चुटकी MSG** डालें। 

4. KYROZ VARIATION — LEMON CORIANDER SOUP

इसी Base का उपयोग करके **Lemon Coriander Soup** भी बनाया जा सकता है। 

चरण 1

**Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE LIQUID और पानी का वही Ratio** रखें। 

चरण 2

**Soya Sauce बिल्कुल न डालें।**

Liquid Base से ही Soup का रंग अपेक्षाकृत हल्का रहेगा। 

चरण 3

अंत में डालें:

* **1 बड़ा चम्मच Fresh Lemon Juice**
* भरपूर मात्रा में **Fresh कटा हुआ हरा धनिया**

इससे Fresh और Garlicky Lemon Coriander Soup तैयार होगा।

13. KYROZ+ 5-STAR MANCHOW SOUP

ENGLISH VERSION

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Category: Soup

Base Used: Kyroz Z-101 DARK MASTER Master Liquid



1. INGREDIENTS — PER PORTION / BOWL

| Ingredient                                |    Quantity |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------: |
| **Kyroz Z-101 DARK MASTER Master Liquid** |      100 ml |
| **Plain Water**                           |      200 ml |
| **Finely Chopped Vegetables**             |      2 tbsp |
| **Finely Chopped Garlic**                 |       1 tsp |
| **Finely Chopped Ginger**                 |       ½ tsp |
| **Finely Chopped Coriander Stems**        |       1 tsp |
| **Cornflour Slurry**                      |    2–3 tbsp |
| **Fried Noodles**                         | For topping |
| **Spring Onion**                          | For garnish |

Suggested Vegetables

* Carrot
* Cabbage
* Beans
* Capsicum 

2. COOKING METHOD — 3-MINUTE PROCESS

Step 1 — Aromatic Base

Heat a little oil in a wok/pan on **high flame**.

Add:

* Chopped garlic
* Chopped ginger
* Chopped coriander stems

Sauté until the garlic becomes **light brown**. This creates the characteristic burnt-garlic aroma. 

Step 2 — Add Vegetables

Add all the chopped vegetables.

Sauté on high flame for only **10–15 seconds**. 

Step 3 — Add Liquid

Add:

* **100 ml Kyroz Z-101 DARK MASTER Liquid**
* **200 ml plain water**

Mix properly. 

Step 4 — Simmer & Thicken

Once the soup starts boiling, gradually add the **cornflour slurry**.

The Manchow Soup should have a **slightly thick, coating-like consistency**. 

Step 5 — Final Hit

Add:

* **1 pinch black pepper**
* **1 drop vinegar**

This gives the soup a fresh finishing punch. 

Step 6 — Garnish

Transfer the soup to a bowl.

Top generously with:

* **Fried noodles**
* **Spring onion** 

3. 5-STAR TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Soup Tastes Bland

**Reason:** Too much water or insufficient master liquid.

**Solution:** Add **½ tsp Kyroz Z-101 DARK MASTER Powder** directly into the soup and mix. 

Problem: Soup Looks Too Dark

**Reason:** The master liquid contains too much soya.

**Solution:** Add a little **plain water** and slightly increase the **salt** to balance it. 

Problem: Fried Noodles Have Become Soft

**Reason:** The noodles were added to the bowl too early.

**Solution:** Always add the noodles **at serving time**, preferably in front of the customer or serve them separately. 

Problem: Soup Is Missing Aroma

**Reason:** Garlic and coriander stems were not sautéed properly.

**Solution:** Next time, sauté the garlic until it becomes **golden brown**. 

4. KYROZ SPECIAL PRO-TIP

For a more premium finish, add either:

* **½ tsp butter**, or
* **1 drop sesame oil**

while finishing the soup.

This adds extra **glaze and aroma** and gives the soup a more premium, restaurant-style finish.

* कटा हुआ Ginger
* कटी हुई Coriander Stems

चरण 2 — Vegetables डालें

जब Soup में उबाल आने लगे, तो धीरे-धीरे **Cornflour Slurry** डालें।

चरण 5 — Final Hit

* **1 चुटकी Black Pepper**

Soup को bowl में निकालें।

ऊपर से भरपूर मात्रा में डालें:

* **Fried Noodles**
* **Spring Onion / हरा प्याज़** 

3. 5-STAR TROUBLESHOOTING

समस्या: Soup फीका लग रहा है

**कारण:** पानी ज्यादा हो गया या Master Liquid कम है।

**समाधान:** **½ छोटा चम्मच Kyroz Z-101 DARK MASTER Powder** सीधे Soup में डालकर मिलाएँ। 

समस्या: Soup बहुत काला दिखाई दे रहा है

**कारण:** Master Liquid में Soya ज्यादा है।

**समाधान:** थोड़ा **सादा पानी** डालें और स्वाद को balance करने के लिए थोड़ा **नमक** बढ़ाएँ। 

समस्या: Fried Noodles नरम हो गए हैं

**कारण:** Noodles बहुत पहले bowl में डाल दिए गए।

**समाधान:** Noodles हमेशा **serve करते समय** डालें। इन्हें customer के सामने डाल सकते हैं या अलग से serve कर सकते हैं। 

समस्या: Soup में खुशबू नहीं है

**कारण:** Garlic और Coriander Stems को सही तरीके से नहीं भुना गया।

**समाधान:** अगली बार Garlic को **Golden Brown** होने तक sauté करें। 

4. KYROZ SPECIAL PRO-TIP

Spread the noodles into a **round/nest shape** using a flat strainer or in a kadai.

The crispy noodles can be prepared in advance and stored in an **airtight container**.

| Ingredient                    |   Quantity |
| ----------------------------- | ---------: |

* Baby corn

Mix and bring the gravy to a boil.

| **Gravy is too sour**        | Too much Z-105 TANGY COAT was added                               | Add a little more ketchup and a small amount of sugar to balance it                |
| **Noodles smell oily**       | Noodles were fried too long ago                                   | Always use freshly fried noodles or noodles stored in an airtight container        |

Always serve American Chopsuey in a **large plate or shallow bowl** so that the egg remains clearly visible on top.

21. KYROZ CHILLI BASE

ENGLISH VERSION

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Dishes Covered:

* Chilli Paneer — Dry / Gravy
* Chilli Chicken — Dry / Gravy
* Chilli Soya Chaap 

1. MORNING PREPARATION — MASTER LIQUID BASE

Prepare this **once every morning**.

**Batch Size: 5 Litres** 

Ingredients

| Ingredient                       |                Quantity |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------: |
| Refined Oil                      |                  150 ml |
| Chopped Garlic                   |                   150 g |
| Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Premix | 150 g — exactly 1 scoop |
| Water                            |                   4.5 L |
| Red Chilli Sauce                 |                  250 ml |
| Green Chilli Sauce               |                  150 ml |
| Vinegar                          |                  120 ml |
| Dark Soya Sauce                  |                   50 ml |
| Tomato Ketchup                   |                   40 ml |

**Note:** The SOP specifies that the higher quantity of garlic contributes to a Five-Star-style taste. Dark soya sauce is used for a light colour. 

2. METHOD — MASTER LIQUID

Step 1 — Cook the Garlic

Heat the oil in a pot.

Add the chopped garlic and cook it lightly.

**Do not brown the garlic.** 

Step 2 — Add Premix

Add **Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Powder**.

Stir it in the oil for only **5–8 seconds**. 

Step 3 — Add Liquid & Sauces

Immediately add:

* Water
* Red Chilli Sauce
* Green Chilli Sauce
* Soya Sauce
* Vinegar
* Tomato Ketchup

Mix everything properly. 

Step 4 — Boil & Simmer

Bring the mixture to **one boil**.

Then simmer for **3–4 minutes** and store it for service. 

3. SERVICE TIME — COOKING THE DISH

| Dish Type        |         Liquid Base | Vegetables  |
| ---------------- | ------------------: | ----------- |
| **Chilli Dry**   |    100 ml — ½ Ladle | 1 cup cubes |
| **Chilli Gravy** | 300 ml — 1.5 Ladles | 1 cup cubes |

Chilli Dry

Use **100 ml / ½ ladle** of Chilli Master Liquid.

Toss on **high flame** so that the sauce properly sticks to the Paneer/Chicken. 

Chilli Gravy

Use **300 ml / 1.5 ladles** of Chilli Master Liquid.

Add slurry to create a gravy suitable for serving with **rice/noodles**. 

4. STEP-BY-STEP COOKING METHOD

Step 1 — Sauté Vegetables

Heat a little oil in the wok.

Add cubed **onion and capsicum**.

Sauté on **high flame for 20 seconds**.

The vegetables should remain **crunchy**. 

Step 2 — Add Chilli Master Liquid

Add the required amount of Chilli Master Liquid according to whether the order is Dry or Gravy. 

Step 3 — Thicken

Once the liquid starts boiling, add **Cornflour Slurry**.

Cook until the sauce becomes **shiny and thick**. 

Step 4 — Add Protein

Add the fried **Paneer or Chicken**.

Toss everything together on **high flame**. 

Step 5 — Finish

Add:

* Slit green chilli
* Spring onion

Then serve immediately. 

5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Colour Is Too Dark Like Manchurian

**Reason:** Too much soya sauce was used.

**Solution:** Reduce the soya sauce quantity next time. The maximum specified is **50 ml**. 

Problem: Dish Is Too Spicy / Sharp

**Reason:** The white pepper and green chilli quantity is too high.

**Solution:** Add a little **Tomato Ketchup** and **1 spoon of butter** to balance the flavour. 

Problem: Raw Garlic Smell

**Reason:** Garlic was not properly cooked while preparing the morning liquid.

**Solution:** Add a little **fried/burnt garlic** on top while serving. 

Problem: Vegetables Are Soggy

**Reason:** Vegetables were boiled with the liquid for too long.

**Solution:** Add vegetables at the end or sauté them for **only 20 seconds**. 

6. KITCHEN RULES — KYROZ STANDARDS

1. No Extra Salt

The Chilli premix and sauces already contain the required salt.

**Do not add extra salt.** 

2. The Crunch Factor

The key characteristic of a Chilli dish is **crunchy onion**.

Do not overcook the onion. 

3. Extra-Spicy Punch

If a customer asks for **extra spicy**, sprinkle a **small pinch of Kyroz Z-102 CRYSTAL GLAZE Powder** on top.

3. COOKING METHOD — “3-STEP BOIL & TADKA”

Keep the measurements **exact and consistent** so that the level of spiciness remains the same in every batch. 

Step 1 — Base Mix

Take **800 ml water** in a saucepan.

Add **250 g Kyroz Z-107 RUBY CONCENTRATE Powder**.

Whisk properly until there are **no lumps**. 

Step 2 — Boil

Place the mixture on **high flame**.

Once it comes to a boil, reduce the flame and cook for **2 minutes**.

This allows the chilli and garlic powder to fully hydrate and makes the chutney **thicker**. 

Step 3 — Garlic Tadka — Secret Step

Heat **50 ml oil** in a small pan.

Add **2 tbsp finely chopped fresh garlic**.

Cook the garlic only until it becomes **Light Golden**.

**Do not burn it.** 

Pour this hot garlic tadka over the boiling chutney.

Immediately **turn off the gas and cover the chutney**. 

Step 4 — Cooling

Allow the chutney to cool.

Once cooled, it will become **thicker and shinier**.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Chutney Is Thin and Water Is Separating

**Reason:** Too much water was added or the chutney was not boiled enough.

**Solution:** Cook for another **1–2 minutes on high flame**.

After cooling, **cornflour slurry should not be required**. 

Problem: Chutney Is Dark / Blackish Red

**Reason:** Garlic burned during the tadka.

**Solution:** As soon as the garlic becomes **light golden**, transfer the tadka into the chutney. Do not leave the garlic cooking in the pan. 

Problem: Chutney Is Too Spicy

**Reason:** The batch was too small and too much powder was added.

**Solution:** Add **1–2 tbsp Tomato Ketchup** and a little **hot water**, then mix properly to balance the taste. 

Problem: Aroma Is Not Fresh

**Reason:** The fresh garlic tadka was skipped.

**Solution:** Although the powder already contains garlic, the **fresh garlic tadka is mandatory** for the premium aroma.

26. KYROZ MASTER GARLIC OIL

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Category: Finishing Oil / Aroma Enhancer 

1. INGREDIENTS — 500 ML BATCH

| Ingredient   |                               Quantity |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------: |
| Refined Oil  |                                 500 ml |
| Fresh Garlic | 200 g — very finely chopped or crushed |
| Salt         |        1 pinch — only for preservation |



2. COOKING METHOD — “SLOW-GOLDEN” TECHNIQUE

The Garlic Oil should **never be prepared on high flame**. 

Step 1 — Cold Start

Put **500 ml oil** into a pan.

Add the finely chopped/crushed garlic **immediately**.

**Do not add garlic to already-hot oil**, otherwise the garlic may burn on the outside without properly releasing its flavour. 

Step 2 — Low Heat

Keep the gas on **very low flame**.

The garlic will slowly cook in the oil and its bubbles will gradually reduce. 

Step 3 — Check the Colour

As soon as the garlic becomes **Light Golden**, immediately turn off the gas.

**Warning:** Do not allow the garlic to become **Dark Brown**, otherwise the oil will become bitter. 

Step 4 — Cooling

Immediately remove the pan from the gas and allow it to cool.

The residual heat of the pan will allow the garlic to reach its final golden colour. 

Step 5 — Storage

Once cooled, transfer the oil into a **glass bottle or dispenser**.

Keep the fried garlic pieces **inside the oil** rather than straining them out. 

3. HELPER CHECKLIST — WHERE & HOW TO USE

Follow the simple **“1-Spoon Rule.”** 

Hakka Noodles / Fried Rice

Before transferring the dish from the wok to the plate:

Add **1 tablespoon Garlic Oil** and toss **only once**. 

Clear Soups

After transferring the soup into the serving bowl:

Add **½ tablespoon Garlic Oil** on top. 

Dry Starters

For items such as **Crispy Corn** or **Veg Salt & Pepper**, sprinkle Garlic Oil over them at the **finishing stage**. 

4. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Oil Tastes Bitter

**Reason:** Garlic became dark brown or black while cooking.

**Solution:** Next time, turn off the gas as soon as the garlic becomes **Light Golden**. 

Problem: Garlic Aroma Is Missing

**Reason:** The oil was cooked too quickly on **High Flame**.

**Solution:** Always use the **Cold Start** method and cook on low flame for **8–10 minutes**. 

Problem: Oil Has Become Sticky

**Reason:** Old oil was used or moisture remained in the container.

**Solution:** Always use **fresh refined oil** and a completely **dry bottle/container**. 

5. KYROZ MASTER TIP

**Do not use Garlic Oil as Cooking Oil.**

Do not use it for sautéing or cooking vegetables.

It should always be used as a **Finishing Oil at the very end** so that its original flavour does not evaporate and the customer receives the fresh garlic aroma.
