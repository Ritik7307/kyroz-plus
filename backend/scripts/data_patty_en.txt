# KYROZ+ | UNIVERSAL PATTY PREMIX INSTRUCTION SHEET

## 1. METHOD — HOW TO PREPARE

### Veg Patty

* Boil the potatoes and allow them to cool completely.
* Mash the cooled potatoes properly.
* For **1 kg boiled potatoes**, add **60 g C-502 GRILL DUST** (approximately 3 tablespoons).
* Mix thoroughly until the premix is evenly distributed throughout the potato mixture.

### Chicken Patty

* Take **1 kg chicken mince**.
* Add **75 g C-502 GRILL DUST** (approximately 4 tablespoons).
* Mix thoroughly until the seasoning is evenly incorporated into the chicken mince.

### Resting

* After mixing, keep the prepared mixture in the refrigerator for **15 minutes**.
* This allows the roasted gram flour in the premix to absorb excess moisture and helps the patty mixture become firm and easier to shape.

---

## 2. SHAPING — SIZE

* Take approximately **70–80 g** of the mixture.
* Shape it into a ball.
* Flatten the ball evenly.
* Make a patty approximately **3.5 inches in diameter**.
* Keep the thickness uniform for even cooking.

---

## 3. DO'S AND DON'TS — IMPORTANT ADVICE

### Salt Warning

* C-502 GRILL DUST already contains salt.
* **DO NOT ADD EXTRA SALT.**

### Temperature

* Always use completely cooled boiled potatoes for the veg patty.
* Mixing the premix with hot potatoes can release excess moisture and make the patty soft or difficult to handle.

### Breading

* For a crispier patty, roll the shaped patty in breadcrumbs before frying or cooking.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: Patty Mixture Is Too Wet

**Solution:**

* If the mixture is sticky or difficult to shape, add **10–20 g breadcrumbs or dry flour**.
* Mix thoroughly and check the consistency again.

### Problem: Patty Tastes Too Mild

**Solution:**

* Increase C-502 GRILL DUST by approximately **5 g** in the next batch.

### Problem: Patty Is Too Spicy

**Solution:**

* Add a little more boiled potato to the veg mixture.
* Alternatively, add some grated paneer and mix thoroughly.
* Adjust gradually until the flavour becomes balanced.
