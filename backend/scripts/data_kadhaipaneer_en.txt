KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: KADHAI PANEER

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-204 ROASTED RUST

MAIN ITEM:
Fresh Paneer Cubes (150 g – 180 g) + Kadhai Vegetables (Capsicum & Onion)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
Kadhai dishes need a light smoky punch.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Hot Water or Capsicum/Onion Stock.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Authentic Kadhai Tadka:

Heat the oil and ghee.

First add:
- 2 pieces Whole Dry Red Chilli
- 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds

As soon as the cumin starts crackling, add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- 1 tsp Lightly Whisked Curd

Stir quickly for 10–15 seconds.

Step 2 - Veggie Crunch Sauté:

Add:
- Capsicum, cut into squares/cubes
- Onion, cut into squares/cubes

Sauté on High Flame for 30–40 seconds so that the vegetables retain their crunch.
Do not make them completely soft.

Step 3 - Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST

Add 40 ml liquid and allow the gravy to simmer.

Step 4 - Paneer & Secret Spice Blast:

Add the Fresh Paneer Cubes.

Then add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE

K-802 WOK SPICE gives the dish its authentic Kadhai flavour.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should NOT be completely smooth.
It should have a Semi-Dry and Bhuna, grainy/thick texture that properly coats the paneer and vegetables.

Aroma Lock:
Add:
- Hand-crushed Kasoori Methi
- A small amount of Crushed Black Pepper

Final Flame:
Toss on High Flame for 20 seconds until the ghee/oil separates from the sides and gives the dish a beautiful shine.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Traditional Copper Kadhai or Ceramic Handi.

Garnish:
- Long-cut Slit Green Chilli
- Ginger Juliennes
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Light dusting of Grated Paneer on top


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The Kadhai gravy is too watery/liquidy.

Solution:
Cook on High Flame for 30 seconds without a lid.
The gravy will reach the desired Semi-Dry consistency.

Problem:
Capsicum and Onion have become completely soft/soggy.

Solution:
During the vegetable sauté step, cook them quickly on High Flame only.
Do not overcook the vegetables.
