KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN TIKKA
PROCESS: MARINATION & PRE-COOK

USE:
Chicken Tikka Starter, Chicken Lababdar, and Chicken Changezi

TARGET:
Chicken pieces should be completely soft and juicy, with the masala evenly coating every piece.


1. PREPARATION (MISE-EN-PLACE)

Chicken Cut:
Use boneless chicken cubes made from breast or thigh, according to the kitchen's standard.

Piece Size:
Each piece should be approximately 35–40 g, or around 1.5 inches.
Keep all pieces of uniform size so they cook evenly at the same time.

Cleaning:
Wash the chicken and leave it in a strainer for 15–20 minutes.

SOP RULE:
The chicken must contain no excess water.
Excess water can make the masala coating thin.


2. FIRST MARINATION
RESTING TIME: 30 MINUTES

Helper Instruction:
This step helps make the chicken soft from the inside.

Ingredients:
- Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1 tbsp per kg chicken
- A small amount of Lemon Juice

Method:
Mix the chicken pieces thoroughly with the ginger-garlic paste and lemon juice.

Keep the chicken in the chiller for 30 minutes.

IMPORTANT:
Do not add salt at this stage because the seasoning already contains the required salt.


3. SECOND MARINATION
THE FINAL COATING

Helper Instruction:
Maintain the exact masala ratio for consistent results.

Standard Ratio:
1 kg Chicken = 33 g Tikka Masala

Base:
Hung Curd (strained curd)
For 1 kg chicken, use approximately 150 g thick hung curd.

The Mix:
1. Take a bowl and whisk the hung curd with 33 g Tikka Masala until smooth.
2. Add 1 tbsp Mustard Oil.
3. Mix well. Mustard oil improves both colour and flavour.
4. Add the chicken from the first marination.
5. Mix gently so that the chicken pieces do not break.

Resting:
Keep the marinated chicken in the chiller for at least 3–4 hours.


4. PRE-COOKING
THE 70% RULE

Skewer:
Place the chicken pieces on the skewer with a small gap between each piece.
Approximately one-finger spacing should be maintained so that heat reaches all sides.

Tandoor Cooking:
Cook in a Medium-Hot Tandoor for 6–8 minutes.

Result:
The chicken should be cooked on the outside while remaining juicy on the inside.

Storage:
Remove the chicken from the skewers and place it on a wire rack inside a tray.
Once cooled, store it in the chiller.


5. IMPORTANT HELPER POINTS

Weight:
Always measure the Tikka Masala using a weighing scale.
Use exactly 33 g per 1 kg chicken.
Do not estimate the quantity by hand.

Curd Check:
Make sure the curd contains no excess water.
Watery curd can cause the masala to separate from the chicken and fall into the bottom of the tandoor.

Service Finish:
When an order is received:
1. Reheat the chicken pieces in the tandoor for approximately 2 minutes.
2. Apply butter.
3. Add Chaat Masala.
4. Serve as Chicken Tikka or add the pieces to the required gravy.


6. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Chicken Tikka has become very hard or rubbery.

Reason:
The chicken was left in the tandoor for too long, or the pieces were cut too small.

Solution:
Follow the recommended 6–8 minute pre-cooking time and maintain the standard piece size.

Problem:
Masala coating looks weak or faded on the chicken.

Reason:
The chicken may not have been properly dried after washing.

Solution:
Check that excess water was completely removed before the first marination.
Water left on the chicken can dilute the masala coating.
