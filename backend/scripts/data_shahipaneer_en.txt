KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: SHAHI PANEER

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-202 IVORY BASE

MAIN ITEM:
Fresh Paneer Cubes (Approx. 150 g – 180 g)
OR Triangle-Shaped Paneer Cuts


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium-Low.

White bases are cashew/nut-based, so they can burn quickly on high flame.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Milk.
This helps maintain the richness and binding of the white gravy.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Shahi Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

First add:
- 2 pieces Green Cardamom
- 1 small piece Mace (Javitri)

Then add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Stir lightly for only 10 seconds.

IMPORTANT:
The Ginger-Garlic Paste should NOT turn brown.
Only the raw smell should disappear.

Step 2 - Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 40 ml Milk

Allow the gravy to simmer on Low Flame.

Step 3 - Paneer Fusion:

Add the Fresh Paneer Cubes.

Gently coat the paneer with the gravy so that it remains soft and does not break.

Step 4 - Spices & Shahi Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
  For an aromatic Mughlai touch.

- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING
  For flavour balance.

- 1 pinch White Pepper Powder
  If an extra flavour punch is required.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli.
The premium off-white colour of the dish must be maintained.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely Silky, Smooth, Creamy and Coating.

The Royal Touch:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Honey OR Sugar

Shahi Paneer should have a mild sweetness.

Add:
- A pinch of Hand-Crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Cook on Low-Medium Flame for 20 seconds until the ghee starts to lightly shine on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium White Ceramic Bowl or Royal Handi.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream in a beautiful round swirl
- A little Saffron Milk OR a pinch of Cardamom Powder
- Lightly Grated Paneer
- Saffron Strands OR Almond Flakes for a premium appearance


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy is sticking to the bottom of the pan or starting to brown.

Solution:
Immediately reduce the flame to Low.

Add 2 tbsp Cold Milk and stir quickly.

This will help restore the colour and texture.

Problem:
The dish has become too sweet.

Solution:
Add:
- 1/2 Ladle extra G-202 IVORY BASE
- A pinch of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

Cook for 10 seconds to balance the sweetness.


HELPER TIP

"Shahi Paneer is a premium and delicate dish.

The creamy colour and soft royal character of G-202 IVORY BASE are its true identity.

Do not rush while stirring the pan.

The paneer should be finished beautifully with cream before sending it to the counter."
