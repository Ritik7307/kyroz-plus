KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN CHANGEZI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (50%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (30%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
70% Pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken Pieces OR Roasted Chicken


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.

Base:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 50 ml Milk or Water to the pan.
For authentic Chicken Changezi, milk is used to add richness.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, finely chopped Green Chilli and Ginger Juliennes to the hot ghee.

Step 2 - The Tangy Factor:
Add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd and 1/2 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING.
Roast for approximately 20 seconds.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1 large ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 1/2 ladle G-202 IVORY BASE

Step 4 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken pieces.
The smoky flavour of Tandoori Chicken pairs well with Changezi gravy.

Step 5 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-802 WOK SPICE


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Thick and Oily.
Ghee should be visible floating on the surface.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Fresh Cream and crushed Kasoori Methi.

Final Touch:
Add 1 drop Kewra Water if available OR a small pinch of Sugar.
This helps balance the spiciness of the masalas.

Final Flame:
Cook on high flame until the Ghee starts separating from the sides.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on a Flat Ceramic Plate or in a Round Handi.

Garnish:
- Plenty of finely chopped Fresh Coriander
- Ginger Juliennes
- Long-cut Green Chilli
- Pour 1 tbsp Desi Ghee over the top before serving.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Gravy is too White or Pale

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp G-205 ROYAL ROGAN and a little Kashmiri Chilli.
Adjust until the gravy becomes Deep Reddish-Orange.

Problem: Gravy has Curdled after adding Curd

Solution:
Keep the flame low while adding curd.
Always whisk the curd properly before adding it to the gravy.


HELPER TIP

Chicken Changezi is a Spicy and Tangy dish.

The texture should not be completely smooth.
It should have a slightly Roasted and Grainy texture.

The shine of Ghee is one of the key characteristics of this dish.
