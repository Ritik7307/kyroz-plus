KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MALAI KOFTA (IVORY)

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-202 IVORY BASE

MAIN ITEM:
Premium Fried Stuffed Paneer & Khoya Koftas
(3–4 pieces per portion)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Milk.
This helps maintain the richness and smooth texture of the white gravy.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Shahi Royal Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

First add:
- 2 Green Cardamoms
- 1 small piece of Mace (Javitri)

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Stir for only 10 seconds.

IMPORTANT:
The colour of the paste should not change.
Only the raw smell should disappear.

Step 2 - Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 40 ml Milk

Allow the gravy to simmer on Low Flame.

Step 3 - Spices & Shahi Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH — for Mughlai depth
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING — for flavour balance

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli.
The premium off-white colour of the dish must be maintained.

Step 4 - The Kofta Rule (CRITICAL):

NEVER cook the koftas inside the gravy.

The koftas are very soft and may break apart and dissolve into the gravy if stirred in the pan.

Keep the koftas separately and place them directly in the service bowl/plate.


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely silky, velvety and thick enough to provide a smooth coating.

The Royal Touch:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Sugar OR Honey

This provides a mild sweetness.

Add:
- A pinch of hand-crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Cook on Low Flame for 20 seconds until a small amount of ghee starts floating on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Use a Premium Deep Ceramic Bowl or Handi.

Assembly:
Place the pre-fried and warm koftas in the service bowl.

Pour the hot G-202 IVORY BASE gravy over the koftas and cover them completely.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream in a round swirl
- A pinch of Cardamom Powder on top
- Fried Almond Flakes
- A little Grated Khoya or Paneer


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The kofta breaks or dissolves as soon as it is placed on the plate.

Solution:
Never boil or cook the kofta together with the gravy.

Prepare the gravy separately.
Place the kofta in the serving bowl and pour the hot gravy over it.

Problem:
The gravy has become too thick and the kofta is not absorbing it properly.

Solution:
Add 2 tbsp Hot Milk to the pan and loosen the gravy slightly.
The texture will become silky again.
