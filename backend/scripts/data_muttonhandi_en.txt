KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MUTTON HANDI
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (60%)
G-204 ROASTED RUST (20%)
G-201 SUNSET BASE (20%)

MAIN ITEM:
4–5 Pieces Mutton (Mix Cut/Nalli) – 80% Pre-cooked


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.
A Handi dish should always cook slowly over low heat.

Base:
1.5 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil

Reheating:
Add 50 ml Mutton Stock (Yakhni) to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, 2 Whole Red Chillies, 1 Bay Leaf and 2 Green Cardamoms to the hot ghee.

Step 2 - The Handi Base:
Add 1 tbsp finely chopped Onion and Tomato.
Sauté until lightly brown.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:
Add:
- 2.5 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN – for the main body
- 1 ladle G-204 ROASTED RUST – for texture and whole-spice character
- 1/2 ladle G-201 SUNSET BASE – for smoothness and richness

Step 4 - Meat:
Add the pre-cooked Mutton pieces.
Mix thoroughly with the gravy for approximately 2 minutes.

Step 5 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp Coriander Powder
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Medium-Thick and coating.
When lifted with a spoon, the gravy should cling to the meat.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd and 1 tsp Fresh Cream.
This gives the Handi its rich and royal texture.

The Dum Touch:
If possible, cover the pan and cook on Dum for approximately 2 minutes.
This allows the ghee to rise properly to the surface.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in an Earthen Handi (Best) or Deep Ceramic Handi.

Garnish:
- Ginger Juliennes
- Fresh Coriander
- 1 tbsp Desi Ghee floating on top
- A light sprinkle of Kasoori Methi


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Gravy looks too thin, like a curry.

Solution:
Continue cooking until the gravy starts releasing oil around the edges of the Handi.

Problem:
Mutton is not soft.

Solution:
Add a little hot stock, cover the pan and cook on low flame for 3–4 minutes.


HELPER TIP

The meat, gravy and ghee should be properly blended together.

This dish is the perfect combination of Rustic and Royal."
