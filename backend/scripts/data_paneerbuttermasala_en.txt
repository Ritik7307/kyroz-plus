KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PANEER BUTTER MASALA

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-201 SUNSET BASE

MAIN ITEM:
Fresh Paneer Cubes (Approx. 150 g – 180 g)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base Fat:
1.5 tbsp Amul Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.
The oil helps prevent the butter from burning.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
30 ml Hot Water or Milk.
This helps maintain a smooth texture and proper binding.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base (Tadka):

Add to the hot butter and oil:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
- Finely Chopped Green Chilli

Sauté for 10–15 seconds so that the raw smell disappears and the fresh aroma is released.

Step 2 - Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 30 ml Liquid

Allow the gravy to reach a light simmer.

Step 3 - Paneer Fusion:

Add the Fresh Paneer Cubes.

Helper Tip:
Do not stir the paneer too much, otherwise the cubes may break.

Gently coat the paneer with the gravy.

Step 4 - Spices & Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Thick, Silky and Coating in texture.

The Finishing Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- Hand-crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Toss quickly on High Flame for only 20 seconds until the butter separates from the gravy and starts to shine.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium Round Handi or Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream in a smooth swirl
- One large Butter Cube in the centre
- Finely Chopped Fresh Coriander
- A light sprinkling of Grated Paneer on top for a premium look


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Paneer feels tight or chewy.

Solution:
Do not add paneer directly to the pan after taking it out of the refrigerator.

Always dip the paneer in warm water and keep it there before using.


Problem:
The gravy tastes slightly too spicy or hot.

Solution:
Add a little extra Butter or 1/2 tsp Honey and mix properly.

This will make the flavour smoother.
