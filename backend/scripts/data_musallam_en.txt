KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MURG MUSALLAM
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (40%)
G-202 IVORY BASE (30%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (30%)

MAIN ITEM:
1/2 Tandoori Chicken (70% Pre-cooked)
OR
4–5 Large Tikka Pieces + 50 g Pre-cooked Keema


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium to High.
This is required to properly combine the keema and gravy.

Base:
1 tbsp Butter + 1 tsp Desi Ghee

Reheating:
Add 50 ml Chicken Stock or Water to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Keema Base:
Add 50 g Pre-cooked Keema and 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste to the hot ghee and butter.
Roast for approximately 1 minute.

Step 2 - The Triple Gravy Mix:

Add:
- 1.5 ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
  For the reddish base and tanginess.

- 1 ladle G-202 IVORY BASE
  For royal richness.

- 1 ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
  For spice and thickness.

Step 3 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A pinch of Cardamom Powder

Step 4 - The Chicken:
Add the pre-cooked Tandoori Chicken (half chicken or pieces) to the gravy.
Coat the chicken thoroughly with the gravy.

Step 5 - Dum:
Cover the pan and cook on low flame for 3–4 minutes so that the chicken becomes completely hot and soft from the inside.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Extra Thick and Rich.
The keema should be properly incorporated into the gravy.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Fresh Cream and crushed Kasoori Methi.

The Saffron Touch:
If available, pour a small amount of Saffron Milk over the top.

Final Flame:
Toss on high flame for only 10 seconds until the butter starts appearing on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve on a Large Oval Plate or Sizzler Plate.

Layering:
First spread a bed of Rich Keema Gravy on the plate.
Place the Tandoori Chicken on top.

Garnish:
- Boiled Egg: Place two halves of one boiled egg on the side.
- Silver Foil (Edible Silver Leaf): Use on the chicken for premium serving.
- Fried Dry Fruits: Sprinkle Cashews and Raisins on top.
- Fresh Coriander
- Ginger Juliennes


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Gravy is too thin or runny.

Solution:
Increase the amount of keema slightly and dry the gravy on high flame.
Musallam gravy should not be flowing.

Problem:
Chicken piece is cold from the inside.

Solution:
Before adding the chicken to the gravy, heat it for approximately 1 minute in the microwave or tandoor.


HELPER TIP

"Murg Musallam is a Presentation dish.

The plate should contain less gravy and the chicken-keema should have more visual dominance.

Eggs and dry fruits are an important part of its presentation.
Do not forget them."
