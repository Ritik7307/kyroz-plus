KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: NAVRATAN KORMA

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY BASE:
100% G-202 IVORY BASE

MAIN ITEM — THE 9 GEMS:
Mix Blanched Vegetables:
- Carrot
- Beans
- Green Peas
- Cauliflower

Plus:
- Paneer Cubes
- Makhana
- Pineapple Chunks
- Fried Cashews
- Raisins (Kishmish)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at Low to Medium.

Base Fat:
1 tbsp Desi Ghee + 1/2 tsp Oil.

Reheating / Base Liquid:
40 ml Milk.
This helps maintain a creamy consistency and rich binding.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - The Shahi Royal Tadka:

Heat the ghee and oil.

Add:
- 2 Green Cardamoms
- 1 small piece of Mace (Javitri)

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Stir lightly for 10 seconds.

IMPORTANT:
The colour of the paste should not change.
Only the raw smell should disappear.

Step 2 - Gems & Base Integration:

Add:
- 2 Standard Ladles G-202 IVORY BASE
- 40 ml Milk

Allow the gravy to simmer on Low Flame.

Now add:
- Boiled Mixed Vegetables
- Paneer Cubes
- Fried Makhana
- Raisins
- Fried Cashews

Stir gently so that the vegetables do not break.

Step 3 - Spices & Mild Flavor Punch:

Add:
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/4 tsp K-806 ZESTFUL ZING

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT add Kashmiri Chilli.
The original premium off-white colour must be maintained.


3. FINISHING — AROMA, FRUITS & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be completely silky, smooth and rich, with a proper coating texture.

Sweet Fruit Touch:
Add:
- Pineapple Chunks
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Sugar OR Honey

This provides the required mild sweetness.

Add:
- A pinch of hand-crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Cook on Low Flame for 20 seconds until a small amount of ghee starts floating on top.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Premium White Ceramic Bowl or Royal Handi.

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream in a round swirl
- Fried Cashews
- Raisins
- Fresh Pomegranate Seeds
- Pineapple Slices

Arrange the garnish properly so that the colourful "Navratan" look is clearly visible.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy becomes slightly sour or appears curdled after adding pineapple.

Solution:
Always use canned or slightly sweet pineapple chunks.

Raw or highly acidic fruits can cause the white gravy to curdle.

If curdling occurs:
Add 2 tbsp Cold Milk and a little Fresh Cream.
Mix properly until the gravy becomes smooth again.

Problem:
The vegetable colour looks dull or pale.

Solution:
During garnishing, highlight the dish with Pomegranate Seeds and Fresh Green Peas.

This will bring back the colourful multi-colour "Navratan" appearance.
