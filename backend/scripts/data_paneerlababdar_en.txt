KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PANEER LABABDAR
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

PRIMARY GRAVY:
G-201 SUNSET BASE (70%)

SECONDARY GRAVY:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (30%)


1. PREPARATION — THE START

Flame:
Keep the flame at Medium.

Base:
1/2 tbsp Butter + 1/2 tsp Oil.
The oil helps prevent the butter from burning.

Reheating:
Add 20–30 ml Water to the pan so that the gravy does not stick.


2. THE PROCESS — STEP-BY-STEP

Step 1:
Heat the Butter and Oil in the pan.
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger Juliennes and a small amount of Fresh Coriander.

Step 2:
Add:
- 2 large ladles G-201 SUNSET BASE
- 1 ladle G-205 ROYAL ROGAN

Step 3:
Add 30–40 ml Water and boil for 1 minute on High Flame.

Step 4:
Add 150 g Paneer Cubes.

Step 5 — Spices:
Add:
- A pinch of Kashmiri Chilli — for colour
- A small amount of K-801 ROYAL PUNCH


3. FINISHING — THE AROMA

Consistency:
The gravy should be Semi-Thick.
It should neither be too thin nor completely dry.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Fresh Cream
- 1/2 tsp Crushed Kasoori Methi

Final Flame:
Toss on High Flame for only 10 seconds so that the butter releases properly.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Bowl:
Serve in a Deep Ceramic Bowl or Handi.

Garnish:
- One line of Fresh Cream
- A little Grated Paneer
- One Fresh Coriander Leaf in the centre


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The gravy has become too sweet.

Solution:
Increase G-205 ROYAL ROGAN by 1/2 tbsp and check the salt/chilli balance.

Problem:
The gravy colour looks dull.

Solution:
Prepare a separate tadka using:
- 1/2 tsp Butter
- 1/4 tsp Kashmiri Chilli

Add this tadka to improve the colour.


HELPER TIP

"Always remember, Lababdar means 'Creamy and Chunky'.

The gravy should coat the paneer properly.
It should not run or become watery."
