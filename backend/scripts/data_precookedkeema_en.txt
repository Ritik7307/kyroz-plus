KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: PRE-COOKED KEEMA
PROCESS: BASE PREPARATION

USE:
For adding richness to gravy dishes and for stuffing.

TARGET:
Keema should remain loose and separated, without lumps, and should be completely roasted.


1. PREPARATION (MISE-EN-PLACE)

Quality:
Use Chicken or Mutton Keema.
Fine machine-minced keema is preferred for base preparation.

Cleaning:
Place the keema in a fine strainer and lightly wash it with cold water.

Draining:
Keep the keema in the strainer for 15–20 minutes so that all blood and excess water drain out.
If the keema remains wet, it will form lumps while roasting.


2. COOKING PROCESS

Helper Instruction:
Keep stirring the keema continuously so that it does not stick together in one place.

Step 1 - Tadka:
Heat 1 tbsp Oil and 1 tbsp Ghee in a large Kadhai or Lagan.
Add:
- 2–3 Bay Leaves
- 1 Black Cardamom

Step 2 - Aroma:
Add 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste.
Roast until the raw smell disappears.

Step 3 - The Sauté (Bhunai):
Add the keema and start roasting on High Flame.

HELPER TIP:
Continuously mash and separate the keema with a spoon so that it remains loose and does not form lumps.

Step 4 - Basic Spices:
When the water from the keema starts drying and the colour begins to change, add:
- 1/2 tsp Salt
  Use only a small amount because the gravy will also contain seasoning.
- 1/2 tsp Turmeric
- 1/2 tsp Cumin Powder

Step 5 - Final Cooking:
Add a little water (1/2 cup).
Cover the pan and cook on Low Flame for 10–12 minutes until the keema becomes soft and starts releasing oil.


3. COOLING & STORAGE

Cooling:
Spread the keema on a tray and allow it to cool.
Do not place hot keema directly into the refrigerator.

Storage:
Once cooled, store it in a Stainless Steel container inside the Chiller at 0°C to 4°C.

Shelf Life:
1–2 days.
Keema spoils quickly, so preparing a fresh batch is recommended.


4. IMPORTANT HELPER POINTS

Lumps:
If the keema forms lumps while roasting, use a Potato Masher or the back of a Ladle to press and separate the lumps.

Fat Content:
If the keema contains a high amount of fat, reduce the amount of oil used.

NO RED CHILLI:
Do not add Red Chilli during pre-cooking.
This allows the keema to be used in both White and Red gravies.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Keema has an unpleasant smell.

Solution:
This means the roasting was not done properly.
Add a little Ginger and some extra Ghee, then roast again.

Problem:
Keema is too hard.

Solution:
Sprinkle a little water over the keema and cook on Low Flame for another 5 minutes.


6. SERVICE USE

For Lababdar:
Add 2 tbsp of this pre-cooked keema to 1 portion of gravy.

For Musallam:
Use directly for stuffing inside the chicken or for thickening the gravy.
