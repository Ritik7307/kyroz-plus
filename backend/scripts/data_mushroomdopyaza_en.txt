KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: MUSHROOM DO PYAZA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-204 ROASTED RUST (60%)
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
Button Mushrooms (Sliced/Quartered) + Onion Cubes (Diced)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame at High.
This helps keep the mushrooms and onions crunchy.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Butter.

Reheating:
Add 20–30 ml Water to the pan.
Use only to loosen the gravy.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Onion & Mushroom Sauté:

Heat the oil and butter in the pan.
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste

Add the large Onion Cubes (Onion Petals) and Mushrooms.

Sauté quickly on High Flame for 1–2 minutes until the mushrooms release a little water and the onions become translucent/light pink.

Step 2 - The Masala Punch:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE
- A small amount of finely chopped Green Chilli

Toss well.

Step 3 - Gravy Mixing:

Add:
- 2 large ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST — for texture
- 1.5 ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN — for depth

Step 4 - Spices:

Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/4 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- A small amount of K-806 ZESTFUL ZING


3. FINISHING — AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The dish should be Semi-Dry.
The gravy should properly coat the mushrooms and onions.

The Secret:
Add:
- 1 tbsp Whisked Curd or Fresh Cream

This gives the dish a rich, royal glaze.

Flame:
Cook on High Flame for 10–15 seconds.
Bhunao until the oil starts separating from the sides.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Round Karahi or Ceramic Bowl.

Garnish:
- Plenty of Fresh Coriander
- Ginger Juliennes
- 1–2 Fried Onion Rings (Optional, for visual appeal)


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
The mushrooms have become too soft and are releasing too much water.

Solution:
Always sauté mushrooms on High Flame.
On Low Flame, mushrooms release more water and become chewy.

Problem:
The gravy has become too spicy.

Solution:
Add 1/2 tsp White Gravy or a little Butter to reduce the spiciness.


HELPER TIP

"Do Pyaza means onion twice!

One type of onion is mixed into the gravy, while the second is the large onion pieces that the customer should clearly see on the plate.

Do not overcook the onion chunks.
Keep them slightly crunchy."
