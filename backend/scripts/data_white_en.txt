# KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

## PRODUCT: CAFE-STYLE WHITE SAUCE PASTA

---

## 1. MEASUREMENT

For consistent taste and quality, follow the recommended quantities below.

| Item                         |                         Quantity — 1 Portion |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------: |
| Boiled Pasta (Penne/Fusilli) |                                    180–200 g |
| Full-Cream Milk              |                             200 ml (1 glass) |
| C-505 ALFREDO CORE           |                  45 g (approximately 3 tbsp) |
| Butter — Optional            |                                 10 g (1 tsp) |
| Vegetables                   | Sweet Corn, Capsicum, Mushroom — as required |

---

## 2. COOKING STEPS

### Step 1 — Sauté Vegetables

Heat a small amount of butter or oil in a pan. Add the vegetables and lightly sauté for approximately **1 minute**.

### Step 2 — Add Milk & Premix

Add **200 ml cold milk** to the pan.

Immediately add **45 g C-505 ALFREDO CORE** and mix thoroughly.

### Step 3 — Whisking

Continuously stir or whisk the mixture to prevent lumps.

Keep the flame at **medium heat**.

### Step 4 — Thickening

Continue cooking until the sauce becomes smooth, thick, and glossy.

Add the **boiled pasta** once the sauce reaches the required consistency.

### Step 5 — Finishing

Cook the pasta with the sauce for approximately **1 minute**, allowing the sauce to coat the pasta properly.

Serve hot.

---

## 3. IMPORTANT HELPER ADVICE

### No Extra Salt

* C-505 ALFREDO CORE already contains salt.
* **Do not add additional salt** unless specifically required by the standard recipe.

### Cold Start

* Always dissolve C-505 ALFREDO CORE in **cold or room-temperature milk**.
* Adding the powder directly to hot milk can cause lumps.

### Texture

* If the pasta becomes too dry after cooling, add approximately **2 tablespoons of hot milk** while serving and mix gently to restore the creamy texture.

### Freshness

* Cook the pasta to approximately **80% doneness (al dente)**.
* The remaining cooking should be completed in the sauce so the pasta absorbs the flavour.

---

## 4. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem             | Possible Reason                                                       | Solution                                                                                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sauce has lumps     | Powder was added to hot milk or the mixture was not stirred properly. | Dissolve the powder in cold milk first and stir continuously while cooking.             |
| Sauce is too thin   | Too much milk or insufficient premix was used.                        | Add approximately 1 teaspoon of premix and cook for an additional 1 minute.             |
| Flavour is too mild | Too much water was used instead of milk.                              | Always use full-cream milk or add a small amount of butter.                             |
| Pasta is sticking   | Pasta was not cooled after boiling.                                   | Rinse the pasta with cold water immediately after boiling and lightly coat it with oil. |

---

## 5. PRESENTATION TIP

Before serving, lightly sprinkle:

* **C-509 FIRE DUST**, or
* Chilli flakes

onto top of the pasta for a more premium presentation.
