# KYROZ+ | HERB & GARLIC SPREAD MIX

## SOP: Usage Instructions for Helper

### 1. Garlic Bread — Butter Spread

**Ratio:**
200 g Soft Butter (Amul or Local) + 40 g C-504 HERB INFUSION

**Method:**

1. Keep the butter at room temperature until it becomes soft.
2. Do not melt the butter.
3. Add 40 g of C-504 HERB INFUSION to 200 g of soft butter.
4. Mix thoroughly until the premix is completely blended with the butter.
5. Apply a thin, even layer of the prepared garlic butter on the bread.
6. Toast the bread in an oven or on a tawa until properly cooked.

**Result:**
A rich Herb & Garlic Butter Spread will be ready for garlic bread.

---

### 2. Sandwich / Wraps — Creamy Spread

**Ratio:**
300 g Mayonnaise + 30 g C-504 HERB INFUSION

**Method:**

1. Take 300 g mayonnaise in a clean bowl.
2. Add 30 g of C-504 HERB INFUSION.
3. Mix thoroughly until the premix is evenly blended with the mayonnaise.
4. Allow the mixture to rest for 10 minutes before use.

**Result:**
A premium Herb & Garlic Mayo Spread will be ready for sandwiches and wraps.

---

### 3. Preparation

**Butter Mix**

* Butter must always be soft, not melted.
* Melted butter can cause the spice mix to settle at the bottom.
* Bring the butter to room temperature before mixing.

**Mixing**

* Mix the premix thoroughly until it becomes completely uniform with the butter or mayonnaise.
* Make sure there are no unmixed portions of the premix.

---

### 4. How to Use

**Garlic Bread**

* Apply a light and even layer of the spread on both sides of the bread where required.
* Toast in an oven or on a tawa.

**Sandwich**

* Apply the spread as a base layer on the bread slice before adding the other ingredients.

**Wraps**

* Apply an even layer of the spread on the wrap before adding the filling.

---

### 5. Important Chef-Less Tips

**Salt Warning**

* The premix already contains salt.
* Butter may also contain salt.
* Always follow the recommended ratio.
* Do not add extra salt unless required by the standard recipe.

**Freshness**

* Always keep the premix container tightly closed when not in use.
* This helps retain the garlic aroma and freshness.

---

### 6. Troubleshooting

**Problem: Bread is Burning**

* Check the spread ratio and the quantity applied.
* Too much premix with insufficient butter may cause the spread to burn faster.
* Increase the butter slightly if required.

**Problem: Flavour is Too Mild**

* Check whether the recommended premix ratio was followed.
* If a stronger garlic flavour is required, a small amount of fresh garlic paste may be added according to the kitchen's standard recipe.
