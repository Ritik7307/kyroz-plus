KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: CHICKEN / MUTTON RARA
PORTION SIZE: 300 g

A4 KITCHEN DISPLAY FORMAT

GRAVY MIX:
G-205 ROYAL ROGAN (60%)
G-204 ROASTED RUST (40%)

MAIN ITEM:
2–3 Pieces Chicken/Mutton (80% Pre-cooked)
+
60 g Keema (80% Pre-cooked with Masala)


1. INITIAL SETUP

Flame:
Keep the flame High. High heat is essential for proper bhunai.

Base:
1 tbsp Oil + 1 tsp Desi Ghee.
Ghee enhances the flavour of the meat.

Reheating:
Add 40–50 ml Meat Stock (boiled mutton stock) or plain water to the pan.


2. THE COOKING PROCESS

Step 1 - Aroma Base:
Add 1/2 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste, finely chopped Green Chilli and Ginger Juliennes to the hot oil and ghee.

Step 2 - The Keema Punch:
First, add 60 g pre-cooked Keema.
Roast on high flame for approximately 1 minute until the oil starts releasing from the keema.

Step 3 - Meat & Gravy Mixing:
Add the pre-cooked Chicken/Mutton pieces.

Then add:
- 2 large ladles G-205 ROYAL ROGAN
- 1.5 ladles G-204 ROASTED RUST

G-204 ROASTED RUST provides the "Khada Masala" texture that is a key characteristic of Rara.

Step 4 - Spices:
Add:
- 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Chilli
- 1/2 tsp K-801 ROYAL PUNCH
- 1/2 tsp K-802 WOK SPICE

Step 5 - The Bhunai - CRUCIAL:
Cook on medium flame for 3–4 minutes until the meat and keema properly bind together and the gravy becomes thick.


3. FINISHING - AROMA & TEXTURE

Consistency:
The gravy should be Thick and Grainy.
There should be no free-flowing water in the dish.

The Secret:
Add 1 tbsp Whisked Curd for balance.
Finish with 1 tsp Desi Ghee on top.

Final Touch:
Add plenty of Kasoori Methi and finely chopped Fresh Coriander.
Toss on high flame for approximately 20 seconds.


4. GARNISH & PLATING

Plating:
Serve in a Copper Handi or Earthen Handi.

Garnish:
- Grated Boiled Egg — an authentic characteristic of Rara
- Ginger Juliennes
- Fresh Coriander
- One long Slit Green Chilli


5. TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem: Keema and Meat look separated / Gravy is releasing water.

Solution:
Roast on high flame until the gravy completely coats and binds with the meat.

Problem: Dish looks too oily.

Solution:
Remove the excess oil with a ladle before serving.
Alternatively, add 1 tsp White Gravy and mix properly.


HELPER TIP

"Rara means 'Bhuna Hua' — a properly roasted preparation.
Do not rush this dish.

The Keema and Gravy should look uniform and properly combined.

If preparing Mutton Rara, the meat pieces must be completely soft and tender."
