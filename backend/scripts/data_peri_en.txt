# KYROZ+ | PERI-PERI SEASONING INSTRUCTIONS

## SOP: How to Use — For Helper

### 1. French Fries & Nuggets

**Action**

* Immediately after deep-frying, place the fries or nuggets in a bowl while they are still hot and lightly oily.

**Recommended Ratio**

* For **200 g fries**, add approximately **10–15 g C-509 FIRE DUST**.

**Method**

1. Sprinkle C-509 FIRE DUST evenly over the hot fries or nuggets.
2. Toss the bowl thoroughly so the seasoning coats all pieces evenly.
3. Serve immediately for the best flavour and coating.

---

### 2. Paneer & Chicken Grills

**Action**

* Coat the paneer or chicken with C-509 FIRE DUST before grilling.
* Ensure the seasoning is distributed evenly over the surface.
* Grill according to the kitchen's standard cooking procedure.

**Result**

* A spicy Peri-Peri flavour will be added to the paneer or chicken.

---

# A4 FORMAT SOP

## THE "SPICE & SHAKE" GUIDE

### 1. HOW TO USE

**Toss It Hot**

* Always apply C-509 FIRE DUST to hot fried items.
* Seasoning may not adhere properly to cold fries and may settle at the bottom of the bowl.
* Apply the seasoning immediately after frying.

**Dry Storage**

* C-509 FIRE DUST contains citric acid and sugar.
* These ingredients may absorb moisture and cause the seasoning to become sticky or form lumps.
* Always store the seasoning container in a clean and dry place.
* Keep the container tightly closed when not in use.

---

### 2. HELPER ADVICE

**No Extra Salt**

* C-509 FIRE DUST already contains salt.
* Do not add additional salt to the fries during or after frying unless specifically required by the standard recipe.

**Uniform Coating**

* After adding C-509 FIRE DUST, close the container or bowl and shake/toss thoroughly.
* Tossing provides more uniform coating than mixing with a spoon.

---

### 3. TROUBLESHOOTING

**Problem: Too Spicy**

* Reduce the amount of C-509 FIRE DUST slightly during the next preparation.
* Serve with Kyroz Mayo Dip if a milder eating experience is required.

**Problem: Seasoning Is Not Sticking**

* Apply the seasoning immediately after removing the fries from the fryer.
* The fries should still be hot and lightly oily.
* If the fries have become too dry, lightly spray them with oil and then add the seasoning.

---
