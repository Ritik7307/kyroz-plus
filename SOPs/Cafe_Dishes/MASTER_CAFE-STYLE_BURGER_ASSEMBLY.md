==================================================
1. THE 6-LAYER ASSEMBLY
BURGER ASSEMBLY SYSTEM
==================================================

Assemble the burger from bottom to top strictly in the following sequence:

BOTTOM BUN:
• Apply C-503 VELVET GLAZE as the base sauce layer.

LAYER 02 — BOTTOM VEGETABLES:
• Lightly shredded Lettuce or Cabbage.

LAYER 03 — CHEESE (OPTIONAL):
• 1 Cheese Slice, only when ordered.

LAYER 04 — THE HERO:
• Hot and crispy Patty prepared using C-502 GRILL DUST.

LAYER 05 — TOPPINGS:
• 2 Tomato Slices
• 2 Cucumber/Onion Slices

TOP BUN:
• Apply C-503 VELVET GLAZE on the inside surface of the top bun.

IMPORTANT:
• Maintain the same assembly sequence for every burger.
• Do not randomly change the layer order.


==================================================
2. COOKING & ASSEMBLY STEPS
BANANE KA TARIKA
==================================================

1. BUN TOASTING:

• Cut the burger bun horizontally into two halves.
• Apply a light layer of butter to the inner side.
• Toast on a tawa or griddle for approximately 15–20 seconds.
• The bun should remain soft and lightly toasted.
• Do NOT burn or over-toast the bun.

2. BASE SAUCE:

• Apply 1 teaspoon C-503 VELVET GLAZE evenly on the inside of the
  Bottom Bun.

3. VEGETABLE PLACEMENT:

• Add a small layer of finely shredded Lettuce or Cabbage over the sauce.
• This creates a barrier between the hot patty and the bottom bun and
  helps reduce sogginess.

4. PATTY & CHEESE:

• Place the freshly cooked, hot and crispy patty prepared using
  C-502 GRILL DUST.
• For a Cheese Burger, immediately place 1 Cheese Slice over the hot
  patty so that it melts slightly.

5. TOP TOPPINGS:

• Place the tomato and onion/cucumber slices evenly over the patty.

6. TOP SAUCE & CLOSE:

• Apply 1 teaspoon C-503 VELVET GLAZE to the inside of the Top Bun.
• Place the Top Bun over the assembled burger.
• Press very lightly.
• Wrap immediately for service.

4. HELPER SPECIAL ADVICE
CHEF-LESS TIPS
==================================================

THE CRUNCH RULE:

• Fry the patty only when the order is received.
• Do not keep fried patties sitting for long periods.
• Pre-fried patties lose their crispness and can negatively affect the
  burger texture.

SAUCE BALANCE:

• Spread the sauce evenly across the bun surface.
• Do not use excessive sauce.
• Maintain the approved 1-teaspoon portion for consistent taste.

VEGETABLE FRESHNESS:

• Prepare tomato, onion and other vegetables in advance using approved
  food-safe preparation procedures.
• Keep prepared vegetables refrigerated.
• Drain excess moisture from tomatoes before assembly.
• Fresh, chilled and crunchy vegetables provide better contrast with the
  hot patty.


==================================================
5. TROUBLESHOOTING
PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
==================================================

PROBLEM:
BURGER IS SLIPPING / FALLING APART

REASON:
• Excess sauce.
• Tomato contains excessive moisture.
• Layers are not aligned properly.

SOLUTION:
• Drain excess tomato moisture.
• Maintain the standard 1-teaspoon sauce portion.
• Centre each layer properly before closing the burger.


PROBLEM:
BOTTOM BUN IS SOGGY

REASON:
• Hot patty was placed directly on the bun.
• Excess moisture from vegetables or sauce.

SOLUTION:
• Always place a Lettuce/Cabbage layer between the bun and patty.
• Control sauce quantity.
• Drain excess moisture from vegetables.


PROBLEM:
BUN IS DRY AND HARD

REASON:
• Bun is old or stale.
• Bun was over-toasted.

SOLUTION:
• Use fresh buns.
• Apply a light amount of butter.
• Toast only until lightly warm and golden.
• Do not over-toast.
