2. ASSEMBLY STEPS
PIZZA BANANE KA TARIKA
==================================================

1. BASE DOCKING / PRICKING:

• Take the 10-inch pizza base prepared using C-501 DOUGH MASTER.
• If the base is not pre-baked, prick the entire surface with a fork.
• Make small, evenly distributed holes across the base.
• This prevents the centre of the pizza from puffing excessively during
  baking.


2. SAUCE LAYERING:

• Place 50 G C-506 MARINARA CORE sauce in the centre of the pizza base.
• Spread the sauce in a circular motion from the centre towards the edges.
• Leave approximately 1/2 inch of the outer edge uncovered for the crust.


3. FIRST CHEESE LAYER
THE BASE LOCK:

• Take approximately 30% of the total cheese quantity.
• Sprinkle it lightly over the sauce.
• This first cheese layer helps hold the toppings in place during baking.


4. TOPPINGS PLACEMENT:

• Spread Onion, Capsicum, Sweet Corn and Mushroom evenly across the pizza.
• Do not create large piles of toppings in one area.
• Maintain even distribution from centre to edge.


5. FINAL CHEESE LAYER:

• Add the remaining 70% of the diced cheese over the toppings.
• Distribute evenly so that the toppings are lightly covered.


==================================================
3. BAKING STANDARDS
OVEN SETTINGS
==================================================

CRITICAL RULE:

• Preheat the oven for at least 15 minutes before baking pizza.
• Never place the pizza into a cold or insufficiently preheated oven.

COMMERCIAL DECK OVEN:

• Top Temperature: 280°C
• Bottom Temperature: 260°C

CONVEYOR PIZZA OVEN:

• Temperature: 250°C
• Conveyor Time/Speed Setting: Approximately 5–5.5 minutes

STANDARD BAKING TIME:

• Approximately 5–6 minutes.

FINAL CHECK:

Pizza is ready when:

• Cheese is completely melted.
• Light golden-brown spots appear on the cheese.
• Crust is evenly golden brown.
• Base is properly cooked and firm enough to hold the toppings.


IMPORTANT:

Actual baking time may vary depending on oven type, dough thickness,
topping load and oven recovery. Use the visual doneness check along with
the approved equipment setting.


==================================================
4. HELPER SPECIAL ADVICE
CHEF-LESS TIPS
==================================================

NO SOGGY BASE:

• Excess moisture from vegetables can make the pizza base soggy.
• Drain and properly dry vegetables before use.
• Frozen corn and washed vegetables should not be used while excessively
  wet.


THE GOLDEN EDGE:

• After removing the pizza from the oven, lightly brush the crust edges
  with Butter or Olive Oil.
• This gives the crust a glossy and premium appearance.


CUTTING RULE:

• Cut the pizza immediately after removing it from the oven.
• Use a Pizza Cutter.
• Cut into 4 or 6 equal slices according to the serving standard.
• Delayed cutting can cause the cheese to cool and become difficult to
  cut cleanly.


==================================================
5. TROUBLESHOOTING
PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
==================================================

PROBLEM:
CHEESE IS BURNT BUT BASE IS UNDERCOOKED

REASON:
• Top heat is too high compared with bottom heat.
• Pizza may be positioned too close to the top heating element.

SOLUTION:
• Reduce the top temperature by approximately 10–20°C.
• Increase bottom heat only within the approved oven specification.
• Recheck the pizza position inside the oven.


PROBLEM:
PIZZA PUFFED UP IN THE CENTRE

REASON:
• Base was not properly docked.
• Too much air remained trapped under the dough.

SOLUTION:
• Prick/dock the base evenly before sauce is added.
• Ensure the docking covers the centre area properly.


PROBLEM:
CHEESE IS MELTED BUT HAS NO CHEESE STRETCH

REASON:
• Oven temperature was too low.
• Pizza was removed before the cheese reached the required melt stage.
• Cheese blend or storage condition may also affect stretch.

SOLUTION:
• Maintain the approved oven temperature.
• Bake until the cheese is fully melted with light golden-brown spots.
• Use properly stored, approved Mozzarella/Cheddar cheese blend.


PROBLEM:
PIZZA BASE IS SOGGY

REASON:
• Vegetables contained excess water.
• Too much sauce or topping moisture was used.

SOLUTION:
• Drain and dry vegetables before assembly.
• Maintain the standard 50 G sauce quantity.
• Do not overload the pizza with wet toppings.

6. PRESENTATION & SERVING
==================================================

• Place the finished pizza in an approved pizza box or on a wooden
  serving platter.

• Cut into 4 or 6 equal slices immediately after baking.

• Serve with 1 small portion/pouch of C-509 FIRE DUST on the side so the
  customer can add additional seasoning according to preference.

FINAL PRESENTATION:

[ ] Crust is golden brown.

[ ] Cheese is fully melted with light brown spots.

[ ] Toppings are evenly distributed.

[ ] Base is properly cooked and not soggy.

[ ] Crust edges have a light premium glaze.

[ ] Pizza is evenly cut.

[ ] C-509 FIRE DUST is provided as required.
