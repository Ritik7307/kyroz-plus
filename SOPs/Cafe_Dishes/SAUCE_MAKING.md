KYROZ+ | BURGER SAUCE & DIP MAKING

SOP: SAUCE MAKING
MIXING RATIOS FOR RESTAURANT


==================================================
1. CLASSIC BURGER SAUCE
HAR BURGER KI JAAN
==================================================

RATIO:

• Mayonnaise: 200 G
• Ketchup: 100 G
• C-503 VELVET GLAZE: 30 G

RESULT:

A smooth, creamy classic orange burger sauce with a familiar fast-food
style flavour profile.


==================================================
2. TANDOORI BURGER SAUCE
NORTH INDIAN SPECIAL
==================================================

RATIO:

• Mayonnaise: 300 G
• C-503 VELVET GLAZE: 50 G
• Red Chilli Powder: 10 G

IMPORTANT:

• Do NOT add ketchup.
• The finished sauce should have a rich tandoori colour and flavour,
  suitable for Tandoori Paneer Burger and similar products.


==================================================
3. CHEESY-GARLIC DIP
FOR GARLIC BREAD
==================================================

RATIO:

• Mayonnaise: 250 G
• C-503 VELVET GLAZE: 30 G
• Liquid Cheese: 50 G

RESULT:

Creamy, cheesy and garlic-forward dip suitable for Garlic Bread and
similar snacks.


==================================================
4. PREPARATION METHOD
KAISE BANAYEIN
==================================================

1. Take a clean food-grade plastic or glass mixing bowl.

2. Measure the required quantity of Mayonnaise and Ketchup according to
   the selected sauce recipe.

3. Add the required quantity of C-503 VELVET GLAZE.

4. For Tandoori Burger Sauce, add the specified Red Chilli Powder.

5. For Cheesy-Garlic Dip, add the specified Liquid Cheese.

6. Mix thoroughly using a clean whisk or spoon.

7. Continue mixing until the sauce becomes completely smooth and no
   visible lumps remain.

8. Cover the prepared sauce and refrigerate for approximately 20 minutes
   before service.

WHY REST?

The resting period allows the seasoning to hydrate and the flavour and
colour to develop properly.


==================================================
5. STORAGE & HYGIENE
==================================================

• After preparation, always keep the sauce refrigerated.

• Store the sauce in a clean, covered, food-grade container.

• Do NOT repeatedly insert dirty or used spoons into the container.

• Always use a clean and dry serving spoon.

• Avoid cross-contamination between different sauces.

• Prepare sauce in controlled batches according to expected service
  demand to maintain freshness.


==================================================
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
==================================================

PROBLEM: SAUCE TASTES TOO SALTY

SOLUTION:

• Add a small quantity of Mayonnaise.
• Mix thoroughly and re-check the taste.


PROBLEM: SAUCE IS TOO THIN

CAUSE:

• Mayonnaise may have low viscosity or excess liquid ingredients were
  used.

SOLUTION:

• Use approved Veg Extra Thick Mayonnaise.
• Maintain the standard recipe ratio.
• Do not add unnecessary liquid.


PROBLEM: SAUCE COLOUR IS TOO LIGHT

SOLUTION:

• First verify that the correct quantity of C-503 VELVET GLAZE was used.
• For the next batch, adjust the premix only within the approved recipe
  specification.
• For Classic Burger Sauce, Ketchup may be adjusted only according to
  the approved recipe standard.


==================================================
7. FINAL QUALITY CHECK
==================================================

[ ] Sauce is smooth and lump-free.

[ ] Correct colour is achieved.

[ ] Sauce has the required thickness.

[ ] Taste is balanced.

[ ] Sauce has rested for approximately 20 minutes.

[ ] Sauce is stored covered and refrigerated.

[ ] Clean serving spoon is being used.

[ ] No cross-contamination is visible.

• Classic Burger Sauce में Ketchup की quantity को भी केवल approved recipe
