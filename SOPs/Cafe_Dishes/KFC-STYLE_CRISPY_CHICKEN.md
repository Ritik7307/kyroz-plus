KYROZ+ | STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

PRODUCT: KFC-STYLE CRISPY CHICKEN
PRODUCT CODE: C-510 ZING MASTER

==================================================
1. MARINATION STANDARD
24-HOUR PROCESS
==================================================

CHICKEN CUTS:

• Popcorn: 10–12 G per piece
• Strips: 40 G per piece
• Wings
• Leg Pieces

Total Chicken Batch: 1 KG

Chicken must be fresh, clean and properly cut.

MARINADE:

• C-510 ZING MASTER: 40 G per 1 KG Chicken

MARINATION TIME:

• 24 Hours — Compulsory
• Store inside the refrigerator/chiller zone.

MARINATION METHOD:

1. Take 1 KG fresh chicken pieces.
2. Add 40 G C-510 ZING MASTER.
3. Mix thoroughly so that the seasoning coats every piece evenly.
4. Transfer to an airtight food-grade container.
5. Keep refrigerated for 24 hours.

This allows the seasoning to penetrate the chicken properly.

BULK STOCK OPTION:

• For monthly stock preparation, chicken may be seasoned in bulk.
• Divide the marinated chicken into small service-size portions.
• Pack each portion in airtight freezer-safe pouches.
• Freeze and use portions after controlled thawing under refrigeration.
• Never refreeze chicken after it has been completely thawed.

IMPORTANT:
Follow approved food-safety and freezer-storage standards for frozen
chicken. Do not keep frozen stock indefinitely.


==================================================
2. DRY COATING STATION SETUP
THE CRUNCH SYSTEM
==================================================

Prepare two dedicated containers at the coating station:

TUB 1 — DRY MIX

• Maida / Refined Flour

TUB 2 — CHILLED WATER

• Completely chilled/ice-cold water


==================================================
3. STEP-BY-STEP COATING METHOD
DOUBLE DIPPING
==================================================

STEP 1 — FIRST COATING

• Remove one 24-hour marinated chicken piece from refrigeration.
• Place it into Tub 1 containing dry Maida.
• Lightly roll and coat the piece with Maida.
• Do NOT press the chicken aggressively into the flour.


STEP 2 — WATER DIP

• Lift the coated piece.
• Dip it into Tub 2 containing chilled water.
• Keep it submerged for only approximately 2–3 seconds.


STEP 3 — SECOND COATING
THE FLAKING STEP

• Remove the piece from the water.
• Immediately place it back into Tub 1.
• Gently roll the piece through the Maida approximately 10–12 times.
• Use a light hand to create irregular flour flakes.
• Do NOT compress the coating.


STEP 4 — SHAKE

• Hold the coated piece carefully.
• Shake gently to remove excess loose flour.
• The surface should show visible irregular flakes.


STEP 5 — FRY

• Transfer the coated chicken immediately into the preheated oil.


==================================================
4. FRYING TEMPERATURE & TIME CHART
==================================================

CRITICAL RULE:

Oil must be properly preheated before chicken is added.

Do NOT place coated chicken into cold or insufficiently heated oil.
Low oil temperature can cause excessive oil absorption and a soft coating.

VARIANT          OIL TEMP.       APPROX. TIME       CHECK
--------------------------------------------------------------
Popcorn          180°C           3–4 Minutes        Golden brown
Strips           180°C           4–5 Minutes        Crunchy coating
Wings            170°C           6–7 Minutes        Fully cooked near bone
Leg Piece        160°C           10–12 Minutes      Deeply cooked


IMPORTANT:

Cooking time is only a guideline. Actual cooking depends on piece size,
oil recovery, fryer load and equipment.

Chicken must reach the required safe internal cooking temperature before
serving.


==================================================
5. HELPER SPECIAL ADVICE
CHEF-LESS TIPS
==================================================

NO EXTRA SALT:

• C-510 ZING MASTER contains the standardized seasoning.
• Do not add additional salt to the Maida or water unless specifically
  instructed by the approved recipe.

ICE-COLD WATER:

• Use properly chilled water for the dipping stage.
• Maintain the required temperature so the coating structure remains stable.

AVOID OVERCROWDING:

• Do not add too many chicken pieces to the fryer at once.
• Overloading can sharply reduce oil temperature.
• Low oil temperature results in excessive oil absorption and reduced
  crispiness.

BATCH CONTROL:

• Maintain consistent piece sizes.
• Fry similar-sized pieces together whenever possible.


==================================================
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
==================================================

PROBLEM:
CRUNCH IS TOO HARD / ROCK-LIKE

REASON:
• Chicken was pressed too aggressively into the Maida during coating.
• Coating became excessively compact.

SOLUTION:
• Keep the hand light.
• Roll the chicken gently.
• Do not press or compress the flour coating.


PROBLEM:
COATING IS FALLING OFF

REASON:
• Water was not sufficiently chilled.
• Coating was not properly formed.
• Excess loose flour was not shaken off before frying.

SOLUTION:
• Use properly chilled water.
• Follow the first-coat → water-dip → second-coat sequence.
• Gently shake off excess flour before frying.
• Fry immediately after coating.


PROBLEM:
LEG PIECE IS RAW / UNDERCOOKED INSIDE

REASON:
• Oil temperature was too high.
• Outside cooked faster than the centre.
• Piece size may have been too large.

SOLUTION:
• Maintain controlled frying temperature.
• Cook the leg piece slowly and verify internal doneness before serving.
• Never rely only on outside colour to determine doneness.


PROBLEM:
CHICKEN IS OILY / COATING IS SOFT

REASON:
• Oil temperature dropped because of overcrowding.
• Chicken was added before oil reached the required temperature.

SOLUTION:
• Preheat oil properly.
• Fry in smaller batches.
• Allow the fryer to recover to the required temperature between batches.


==================================================
7. PRESENTATION
==================================================

1. Remove fried chicken from the oil using a suitable strainer.

2. Allow it to drain for approximately 1 minute.

3. Do not stack freshly fried pieces tightly while they are steaming, as
   trapped steam can soften the coating.

4. Transfer to the approved serving bucket or plate.

5. Before serving, lightly sprinkle C-509 FIRE DUST over the chicken for
   an additional spicy finishing punch.

IMPORTANT:

• Apply seasoning evenly.
• Do not over-season the finished product.


==================================================
8. FINAL QUALITY CHECK
==================================================

[ ] Chicken is fully cooked.

[ ] Coating is crisp and visibly flaky.

[ ] Coating is not excessively hard or compact.

[ ] Chicken is not excessively oily.

[ ] Outside colour is evenly golden brown.

[ ] Piece size follows the standard.

[ ] No raw or undercooked centre.

[ ] C-509 FIRE DUST is applied as per serving standard.

[ ] Product is served immediately after finishing.

PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
==================================================

PROBLEM:
CRUNCH बहुत HARD / पत्थर जैसा है

REASON:

• Coating करते समय chicken को Maida में बहुत जोर से press किया गया।
• Flour coating बहुत ज्यादा compact हो गई।

SOLUTION:

• हाथ हमेशा हल्का रखें।
• Chicken को gently roll करें।
• Coating को press या compress न करें।


PROBLEM:
COATING उतर रही है

REASON:

• Water पर्याप्त chilled नहीं था।
• Coating properly form नहीं हुई।
• Fry करने से पहले extra Maida shake नहीं किया गया।

SOLUTION:

• Properly chilled water इस्तेमाल करें।
• First Coat → Water Dip → Second Coat sequence follow करें।
• Fry करने से पहले extra Maida हल्के हाथ से shake करें।
• Coating के तुरंत बाद chicken fry करें।


PROBLEM:
LEG PIECE अंदर से RAW / KACHA है

REASON:

• Oil temperature बहुत ज्यादा था।
• बाहर जल्दी cook हो गया लेकिन centre कच्चा रह गया।
• Piece का size बहुत बड़ा हो सकता है।

SOLUTION:

• Controlled frying temperature maintain करें।
• Leg piece को धीरे-धीरे पकाएं और serve करने से पहले internal doneness
  verify करें।
• केवल बाहर का colour देखकर chicken के cooked होने का निर्णय न लें।


PROBLEM:
CHICKEN OILY / COATING SOFT है

REASON:

• Overcrowding के कारण oil temperature कम हो गया।
• Oil required temperature तक पहुंचने से पहले chicken डाल दिया गया।

SOLUTION:

• Oil को properly preheat करें।
• छोटे batches में fry करें।
• हर batch के बीच fryer को required temperature तक recover होने दें।
