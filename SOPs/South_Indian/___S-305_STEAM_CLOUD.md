# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## WATER QUALITY

* Always use **fresh RO water** for batter preparation.

## IDLI TRAYS / MOULDS

* Lightly grease the idli moulds with oil before filling.
* Ensure all mould cavities are evenly coated to prevent sticking.

## CRITICAL RULE — NO ADDITIONAL SALT

**DO NOT ADD SALT while preparing the batter.**

* **100% of the required salt is already included in S-305 STEAM CLOUD.**
* Adding additional salt will disturb the standardized product formulation.

---

# 2. MIXING RATIO

|            **200 Grams** |         **250 ml** |     **8–10 Idlis** |

**Critical:** Batter consistency is essential for achieving soft and spongy rice idlis.

---

# 3. MIXING & STEAMING STEPS

## 1. MIXING

* Transfer the required quantity of **S-305 STEAM CLOUD** into a large, clean vessel.
* Slowly add RO water while continuously mixing.
* The batter should be **thick and smooth**.
* The consistency must be noticeably thicker than dosa batter.

## 2. RESTING PERIOD — MUST

* Cover the prepared batter.
* Allow it to rest for **15 minutes**.

### Reason

The rice flour and poha powder need sufficient time to absorb water. Proper hydration is essential for achieving a soft and spongy idli texture.

## 3. FILLING THE MOULDS

* Lightly oil the idli moulds.
* Fill each mould with the prepared batter.
* **Do not fill the moulds completely to the top**, as the idli will expand during steaming.

## 4. STEAMING

* Bring water to a boil in the steamer.
* Once sufficient steam is generated, carefully place the filled idli stand inside the steamer.

## 5. STEAMING TIME

* Steam at **medium-high heat for 10–12 minutes**.
* Do not exceed the standardized cooking time unnecessarily.

## 6. COOLING & DEMOULDING

* After switching off the gas, do not remove the idli stand immediately.
* Allow it to rest for approximately **2 minutes**.
* Remove the stand after the 2-minute cooling period.
* Dip the spoon in water before removing each idli to help release it cleanly from the mould.

---

# 4. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                                | Cause                                                             | Solution                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Idli is hard**                     | Insufficient water or batter was not rested properly.             | Ensure the batter rests for **15 minutes** and maintain the recommended soft/thick consistency.        |
| **Idli has collapsed / become flat** | Excess water was added to the batter.                             | Reduce the water quantity by approximately **50–100 ml** in the next batch and check consistency.      |
| **Idli is sticking to the mould**    | Mould was not properly greased or idli was removed while too hot. | Lightly oil the moulds before filling and allow the stand to cool for **2 minutes** before demoulding. |
| **Idli is not properly white**       | Water quality was poor or idli was over-steamed.                  | Always use **fresh RO water** and do not steam beyond the recommended **12-minute maximum**.           |

---

# 5. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## FRESHNESS

* Prepare only the quantity of idlis expected to be consumed within approximately **1–2 hours**.
* If required, refrigerated/cold idlis may be reheated using the restaurant's approved steaming procedure before serving.

## THE TOOTHPICK TEST

To check whether the idli is cooked:

* Insert a clean toothpick or knife into the centre of the idli.
* If it comes out **clean without wet batter sticking to it**, the idli is ready.

## STORAGE OF S-305 STEAM CLOUD

* Keep the S-305 STEAM CLOUD packet tightly sealed.
* After opening, transfer the remaining powder to an **airtight container**.
* Protect the powder from moisture to preserve the performance of the raising/leavening system.

# 3. MIXING & STEAMING STEPS

## Idli बनाने की विधि

### 1. MIXING

* Required quantity में **S-305 STEAM CLOUD** को एक बड़े और साफ बर्तन में निकालें।
* धीरे-धीरे RO water डालते हुए लगातार mix करें।
* Batter **thick और smooth** होना चाहिए।
* इसकी consistency dosa batter से स्पष्ट रूप से ज्यादा गाढ़ी होनी चाहिए।

### 2. RESTING PERIOD — MUST

* तैयार batter को ढककर **15 मिनट** के लिए rest दें।

### क्यों?

Rice flour और poha powder को पानी absorb करने के लिए समय चाहिए। Proper hydration से idli soft और spongy बनती है।

### 3. MOULDS FILL करना

* Idli moulds पर हल्का oil लगाएं।
* तैयार batter को moulds में भरें।
* **Mould को पूरा ऊपर तक नहीं भरना है**, क्योंकि steaming के दौरान idli फूलती है।

### 4. STEAMING

* Steamer में पानी को अच्छी तरह उबालें।
* जब पर्याप्त steam बनने लगे, तब filled idli stand को सावधानी से steamer के अंदर रखें।

### 5. STEAMING TIME

* **Medium-high heat पर 10–12 मिनट** steam करें।
* Standard cooking time से अनावश्यक रूप से ज्यादा steam नहीं करना है।

### 6. COOLING & DEMOULDING

* Gas बंद करने के तुरंत बाद idli stand को बाहर नहीं निकालना है।
* लगभग **2 मिनट** तक अंदर ही rest करने दें।
* 2 मिनट बाद stand बाहर निकालें।
* Idli निकालने से पहले spoon को पानी में dip करें, फिर mould से idli को निकालें ताकि वह आसानी से release हो जाए।

**Category:** South Indian

---

# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## BATTER SOURCE

* Use **Kyroz S-305 STEAM CLOUD** for preparing the Uttapam batter.

## VEGETABLE TOPPINGS

Prepare the following toppings separately in a clean bowl:

* Finely chopped **Onion**
* Finely chopped **Tomato**
* Finely chopped **Green Chilli**
* Finely chopped **Fresh Coriander**

Mix the toppings evenly and keep them ready for service.

## CRITICAL RULE — NO ADDITIONAL SALT

**DO NOT ADD SALT to the batter or vegetable toppings.**

* **100% of the required salt is already included in S-305 STEAM CLOUD.**
* No additional salt should be added during preparation or cooking.

---

# 2. MIXING RATIO

## MEASUREMENT CHART

Uttapam batter should be:

* Slightly softer than Idli batter
* Slightly thicker than Dosa batter

| S-305 STEAM CLOUD Powder |           RO Water | Required Consistency |
| -----------------------: | -----------------: | -------------------- |
|                 **1 KG** | **1.4–1.5 Litres** | Thick but Pourable   |
|            **500 Grams** |     **700–750 ml** | Semi-thick           |

---

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## THE "PIZZA" TECHNIQUE

### 1. MIXING & RESTING

* Mix the required quantity of **S-305 STEAM CLOUD** with RO water.
* Cover and allow the batter to rest for **15 minutes**.
* Do not skip the resting period.

### 2. TAWA TEMPERATURE

* Heat the tawa to **medium temperature**.
* The tawa should be slightly cooler than the temperature used for regular dosa.

### 3. POURING

* Lightly grease the tawa with oil.
* Pour one standard ladle of batter into the centre.
* Do **not** spread it as thin as a dosa.
* Maintain a moderately thick Uttapam layer.

### 4. TOPPING

* Immediately spread the prepared mixed-vegetable toppings over the batter.
* Lightly press the toppings into the batter so that they adhere properly.

### 5. OILING

* Apply a small amount of oil or butter around the edges and lightly over the toppings.

### 6. FLIPPING

* Once the bottom surface becomes **golden brown**, carefully flip the Uttapam.
* Cook the topping side for approximately **1 minute**.
* Allow the onion and tomato to lightly roast without burning.

### 7. FINAL TOUCH

* Flip once more if required.
* Serve immediately while hot.

---

# 4. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                               | Cause                                                                                             | Solution                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Uttapam is raw inside**           | Batter was too thick or cooking heat was too high.                                                | Add a small amount of water to adjust the batter and cook on lower heat for longer.       |
| **Toppings are falling off**        | Toppings were not pressed into the batter or the batter surface dried before toppings were added. | Add toppings immediately after pouring the batter and gently press them into the surface. |
| **Uttapam is not spongy**           | Required resting period was skipped.                                                              | Always allow the batter to rest for **15 minutes** before cooking.                        |
| **Uttapam is sticking to the tawa** | Tawa temperature is too low or insufficient oil was used.                                         | Check the tawa temperature and apply an appropriate amount of oil or butter.              |

---

# 5. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## COLOUR CHECK

* A proper **golden-brown colour** is an important visual quality indicator.
* An excessively pale Uttapam may indicate insufficient cooking.

## UNIFORMITY

* Use the same fixed-size bowl or ladle for every Uttapam.
* This maintains consistent size, thickness, and portion control.

## COMBO IDEA

* For North Indian customer preferences, serve **Red Kara Chutney** along with **Coconut Chutney** as the standard accompaniment.
