# 1. PREPARATION

## WATER QUALITY

* Always use **fresh RO water** for batter preparation.

## OIL

* Always use **fresh refined oil** for frying.

## CRITICAL RULE — NO ADDITIONAL SALT

**DO NOT ADD SALT while preparing the batter.**

* **100% of the required salt is already included in S-304 CRUNCH CORE.**
* No additional salt should be added.

---

# 2. MIXING RATIO

## MEASUREMENT CHART

Medu Vada batter must be **thicker and stiffer than dosa or idli batter** so that the vada retains its shape during frying.

| S-304 CRUNCH CORE Powder |       RO Water | Required Consistency              |
| -----------------------: | -------------: | --------------------------------- |
|                 **1 KG** | **800–900 ml** | Thick / stiff batter              |
|            **500 Grams** | **400–450 ml** | Maintain standardized consistency |

**Important:** Water quantity must be measured carefully. Do not make the batter excessively thin.

---

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## 1. MIXING

* Add the required quantity of water to **S-304 CRUNCH CORE**.
* Mix thoroughly to create a **thick and stiff batter**.

## 2. RESTING PERIOD — MUST

* Cover the batter and allow it to rest for **20 minutes**.
* This allows the urad dal flour component to absorb water and properly hydrate.

## 3. WHISKING / BEATING

* Immediately before frying, beat/whisk the batter thoroughly for **2–3 minutes in one direction**.
* Proper beating incorporates air into the batter and helps produce a lighter, spongy interior.

## 4. SHAPING

* Wet your hands with clean water.
* Take a portion of batter.
* Shape it into a round vada and make a hole through the centre.
* Carefully release it into the hot oil.

**Machine Option:**
A **Medu Vada Maker machine** may be used for standardized shaping and faster production.

## 5. FRYING

* Fry on **medium heat**.
* Cook until the vada develops an even **golden-brown exterior**.
* Ensure the centre is fully cooked before removing.

---

# 4. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                              | Cause                                             | Solution                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vada is absorbing too much oil** | Batter is too thin or oil temperature is too low. | Add a small amount of **S-304 CRUNCH CORE** to thicken the batter and bring the oil to the correct frying temperature. |
| **Vada is hard**                   | Batter was not beaten properly.                   | Beat the batter thoroughly for **2–3 minutes** to incorporate air.                                                     |
| **Vada is not holding its shape**  | Batter is too soft/thin.                          | Refrigerate the batter for **10–15 minutes** or add a small quantity of S-304 CRUNCH CORE and mix again.               |
| **Vada is raw inside**             | Vada was fried at excessively high heat.          | Maintain **medium heat** and allow the vada to cook gradually so the centre cooks properly.                            |

---

# 5. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## CRISPNESS TIP

* For improved crispness, a small amount of **hot oil may be incorporated into the batter** during preparation.
* Use only a controlled quantity and ensure the oil is handled safely.

## BATTER FLOAT TEST

* Take a small amount of prepared batter and place it into a bowl of water.
* If the batter **floats**, the beating/aeration is generally sufficient.

## SERVICE

* Serve Medu Vada **hot and fresh**.
* Recommended accompaniments:

  * **Sambar**
  * **Coconut Chutney**
