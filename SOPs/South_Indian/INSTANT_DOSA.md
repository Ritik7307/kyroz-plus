# 1. DOSA BATTER PREPARATION

## S-301 COASTAL CRUST TO WATER RATIO

* **1 KG S-301 COASTAL CRUST:** 1.5–1.6 Litres RO Water

## MIXING METHOD

* Take the required quantity of **S-301 COASTAL CRUST** in a large, clean mixing vessel.
* Slowly add RO water while continuously whisking.
* Mix thoroughly until a smooth batter is formed.

## ZERO-LUMPS RULE

* The batter must be completely smooth.
* **No lumps should remain in the batter.**

## RESTING PERIOD

* Cover the prepared batter and allow it to rest for **15–20 minutes**.
* This resting period allows the semolina and rice flour components to properly absorb the water.

## FINAL CONSISTENCY CHECK

After 20 minutes:

* Check the batter consistency.
* If the batter has become too thick, add approximately **50 ml water** and mix again.
* Final consistency should be **pourable** — neither excessively thin nor excessively thick.

---

# 2. COOKING STEPS

## 1. TAWA TEMPERATURE

* Heat the dosa tawa properly.
* To check the temperature, sprinkle a small amount of water on the surface.
* If the water evaporates immediately with a **"shhh" sound**, the tawa is ready.

## 2. SURFACE CLEANING & TEMPERATURE CONTROL

* Before preparing each dosa, wipe the tawa with a **clean damp cloth**.
* This helps control the surface temperature and prepares the tawa for even spreading.

## 3. BATTER SPREADING

* Pour one standard ladle of batter into the centre of the tawa.
* Using light pressure, spread the batter in a circular motion from the centre outward.
* Maintain an even thickness throughout the dosa.

## 4. OILING

* Once the dosa begins to dry slightly:

  * Add approximately **1 teaspoon oil or butter** around the edges.
  * Add a small amount over the centre as required.

## 5. FLASHING / FINAL COOKING

* Cook on **medium heat**.
* Continue cooking until the dosa naturally starts releasing from the edges.
* The bottom should develop an even **golden-brown colour**.
* Remove and serve immediately.

---

# 3. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                               | Cause                                                       | Solution                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dosa is sticking to the tawa**    | Tawa temperature is too high.                               | Sprinkle a small amount of water on the tawa to bring the surface temperature down, then wipe clean before spreading. |
| **Dosa is not spreading properly**  | Batter is too thick.                                        | Add **2–3 tablespoons RO water**, mix thoroughly, and check the consistency again.                                    |
| **Dosa is not crispy**              | Tawa is too cool or dosa was removed too early.             | Maintain medium heat and allow the dosa to cook slightly longer before removing.                                      |
| **Dosa is tearing from the centre** | Batter is old or the required resting period was not given. | Allow fresh batter to rest for **15 minutes** and ensure proper mixing during preparation.                            |
| **Dosa is not developing colour**   | Heat distribution across the tawa is uneven.                | Adjust the tawa position and flame so that heat is distributed evenly across the cooking surface.                     |

---

# 4. HELPER & CHEF-LESS KITCHEN ADVICE

## FIRST IN, FIRST OUT — FIFO

* Always use the **oldest prepared batter first**.
* Follow FIFO strictly for prepared batter batches.

## PORTION CONTROL

* Keep a **fixed-size ladle** for dosa preparation.
* Use the same ladle size for every order to maintain consistent dosa size and portion control.

## CLEAN TAWA, BETTER DOSA

* After every **5–10 dosas**, lightly sprinkle salt water on the tawa and clean the surface.
* This helps maintain a consistent cooking surface.

## NO CORNSTARCH RULE

* S-301 COASTAL CRUST does not contain cornstarch.
* Proper tawa temperature control is essential for preventing sticking and achieving consistent dosa release.

## HYGIENE

* Always use a **clean and dry ladle/spoon** when taking batter from the storage container.
* Keep the batter container covered whenever it is not being used.

---

# 5. STORAGE INSTRUCTIONS

## S-301 COASTAL CRUST — DRY PREMIX

* Store in a **cool, dry place**.
* After opening the packet, transfer the remaining premix to an **airtight container**.
* Protect the powder from moisture.

## PREPARED BATTER

* If batter remains after service, store it in the **refrigerator**.
* Before using refrigerated batter again, mix it thoroughly to restore an even consistency.
* Follow the restaurant's approved food-safety holding period for prepared batter.

# 1. DOSA BATTER PREPARATION

## घोल बनाने का तरीका

### S-301 COASTAL CRUST TO WATER RATIO

* **1 KG S-301 COASTAL CRUST:** 1.5–1.6 Litres RO Water

### MIXING METHOD

* एक बड़े और साफ mixing vessel में required quantity में **S-301 COASTAL CRUST** लें।
* धीरे-धीरे RO water डालते हुए लगातार whisk/fentni से मिलाएं।
* अच्छी तरह mix करें जब तक एक smooth batter तैयार न हो जाए।

### ZERO-LUMPS RULE

* Batter बिल्कुल smooth होना चाहिए।
* **Batter में एक भी गांठ/lump नहीं रहना चाहिए।**

### RESTING PERIOD

* तैयार batter को ढककर **15–20 मिनट** के लिए rest करने दें।
* यह resting period जरूरी है ताकि batter में मौजूद semolina और rice flour पानी को अच्छी तरह absorb कर सकें।

### FINAL CONSISTENCY CHECK

20 मिनट बाद:

* Batter की consistency check करें।
* अगर batter ज्यादा गाढ़ा हो गया हो तो लगभग **50 ml पानी** डालकर दोबारा mix करें।
* Final consistency **pourable** होनी चाहिए — न बहुत पतली और न बहुत गाढ़ी।

# 2. COOKING STEPS

## Dosa बनाने की विधि

### 1. TAWA TEMPERATURE

* Dosa tawa को अच्छी तरह गर्म करें।
* Temperature check करने के लिए tawa पर थोड़ा पानी छिड़कें।
* अगर पानी तुरंत सूखकर **"shhh" sound** करे, तो tawa तैयार है।

### 2. SURFACE CLEANING & TEMPERATURE CONTROL

* हर dosa बनाने से पहले tawa को **साफ गीले कपड़े** से हल्के हाथ से पोंछें।
* इससे surface temperature control होता है और अगला dosa evenly spread होता है।

### 3. BATTER SPREADING

* Tawa के बीच में एक standard ladle batter डालें।
* हल्के हाथ से centre से बाहर की तरफ गोल-गोल घुमाते हुए batter फैलाएं।
* पूरे dosa की thickness लगभग समान रखें।

### 4. OILING

जब dosa हल्का सूखने लगे:

* किनारों पर लगभग **1 teaspoon oil या butter** डालें।
* जरूरत के अनुसार centre पर भी थोड़ी मात्रा डालें।

### 5. FLASHING / FINAL COOKING

* Dosa को **medium heat** पर पकाएं।
* तब तक पकाएं जब तक dosa अपने आप किनारों से tawa छोड़ने न लगे।
* नीचे की तरफ **golden-brown colour** आ जाना चाहिए।
* तुरंत निकालकर serve करें।

# 4. HELPER & CHEF-LESS KITCHEN ADVICE

## FIRST IN, FIRST OUT — FIFO

* हमेशा **सबसे पहले तैयार किया गया batter पहले इस्तेमाल करें**।
* Prepared batter के लिए FIFO system strictly follow करें।

## PORTION CONTROL

* Dosa बनाने के लिए एक **fixed-size ladle** रखें।
* हर order में उसी ladle का उपयोग करें ताकि सभी customers को consistent size और portion का dosa मिले।

## CLEAN TAWA, BETTER DOSA

* हर **5–10 dosas** के बाद tawa पर हल्का नमक वाला पानी छिड़ककर surface साफ करें।
* इससे cooking surface की consistency बनाए रखने में मदद मिलती है।

## NO CORNSTARCH RULE

* **S-301 COASTAL CRUST में cornstarch नहीं है।**
* Dosa को tawa पर चिपकने से रोकने और proper release के लिए सही temperature control सबसे महत्वपूर्ण है।

## HYGIENE

* Batter निकालने के लिए हमेशा **साफ और सूखे ladle/spoon** का इस्तेमाल करें।
* Batter container को इस्तेमाल न होने पर हमेशा ढककर रखें।
