# 1. PREPARATION

## WATER QUALITY

* Always use **cold RO water** for preparing the chutney.
* Cold water helps maintain a fresh coconut flavour and appearance.

## CRITICAL RULE — NO ADDITIONAL SALT

**DO NOT ADD SALT while preparing the chutney or during tadka preparation.**

* **100% of the required salt is already included in S-307 KERALA KERNEL.**
* No additional salt should be added during preparation or finishing.

---

# 2. MIXING RATIO

## MEASUREMENT CHART

| Approx. Chutney Quantity | S-307 KERALA KERNEL |   Cold RO Water |
| -----------------------: | ------------------: | --------------: |
|       **Approx. 3.5 KG** |            **1 KG** |  **2.5 Litres** |
|       **Approx. 1.7 KG** |       **500 Grams** | **1.25 Litres** |
|    **Approx. 350 Grams** |       **100 Grams** |      **250 ml** |

**Important:** Always follow the standardized measurement ratio for consistent taste and texture.

---

# 3. MIXING STEPS

## 1. BLENDING

* Measure the required quantity of **S-307 KERALA KERNEL** according to the chart.
* Transfer the powder into a clean mixing vessel.

## 2. ADDING WATER

* Slowly add **cold RO water** in portions.
* Continuously mix using a whisk or clean spoon.
* Ensure the powder hydrates evenly without dry pockets or lumps.

## 3. RESTING PERIOD — MUST

* After mixing, cover the chutney and allow it to rest for **10–15 minutes**.

### Reason

The desiccated coconut component absorbs water and hydrates during resting. This improves the texture and gives the chutney a fresh coconut-like appearance and mouthfeel.

## 4. CONSISTENCY CHECK

After 15 minutes:

* Check the chutney consistency.
* If the chutney is too thick, gradually add a small amount of cold RO water.
* Mix thoroughly and adjust to the required serving consistency.

---

# 4. FRESH TADKA

## THE KYROZ SIGNATURE

Fresh tadka should be prepared after the chutney has completed its resting period.

### TADKA PREPARATION

* Heat **1–2 teaspoons oil** in a small pan.
* Add:

  * **Mustard Seeds (Rai)**
  * **1–2 Whole Red Chillies**
  * **8–10 Fresh Curry Leaves**
* Allow the mustard seeds to splutter properly.
* Pour the fresh tadka over the prepared chutney.
* Lightly mix so that the tempering is distributed without disturbing the chutney's texture.

---

# 5. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                     | Cause                                                 | Solution                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Chutney is too thin**   | Excess water was added.                               | Add a small quantity of **S-307 KERALA KERNEL powder**, mix well, and allow it to rest for **5 minutes**. |
| **Flavour is weak**       | Excess water diluted the standardized formulation.    | Follow the measurement chart strictly in the next batch.                                                  |
| **Colour is yellow/dark** | Hot water was used or the chutney was left uncovered. | Always use **cold RO water** and refrigerate the prepared chutney in a covered container.                 |
| **Texture is dry**        | The required resting time was not given.              | Allow the chutney to rest for at least **10 minutes** before final consistency adjustment and service.    |

---

# 6. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## SMALL BATCHES

* Do not prepare unnecessarily large quantities at one time.
* Prepare **2–3 smaller batches throughout the day** to maintain freshness and quality.

## COLD STORAGE

* After preparation, always store the chutney in the **refrigerator**.
* Coconut-based chutney can spoil quickly if left unrefrigerated.

## SERVICE

* During plating/service, ensure that a small amount of the fresh tadka is visible and distributed across each serving.

---

# 3. MIXING STEPS

## Chutney बनाने की विधि

### 1. BLENDING

* Measurement chart के अनुसार required quantity में **S-307 KERALA KERNEL** लें।
* Powder को एक साफ mixing vessel में डालें।

### 2. WATER ADD करना

* **Cold RO Water** को धीरे-धीरे portions में डालें।
* Whisk या साफ spoon से लगातार mix करते रहें।
* Powder पूरी तरह hydrate होना चाहिए और कोई dry portion या lumps नहीं रहने चाहिए।

### 3. RESTING PERIOD — MUST

* Mixing के बाद chutney को ढककर **10–15 मिनट** के लिए rest दें।

### क्यों?

Desiccated coconut वाला हिस्सा पानी को absorb करके hydrate होता है। इससे chutney की texture बेहतर होती है और इसका appearance और mouthfeel fresh coconut जैसा बनता है।

### 4. CONSISTENCY CHECK

15 मिनट बाद:

* Chutney की consistency check करें।
* अगर chutney बहुत गाढ़ी है तो थोड़ी मात्रा में cold RO water धीरे-धीरे डालें।
* अच्छी तरह mix करके required serving consistency तक adjust करें।

# 4. FRESH TADKA

## THE KYROZ SIGNATURE

Chutney का resting period पूरा होने के बाद fresh tadka लगाना जरूरी है।

### TADKA PREPARATION

* एक छोटे pan में **1–2 teaspoon oil** गर्म करें।
* इसमें डालें:

  * **Rai / Mustard Seeds**
  * **1–2 Sabut Laal Mirch**
  * **8–10 Fresh Curry Leaves**
* Rai को अच्छी तरह चटकने दें।
* तैयार fresh tadka को chutney के ऊपर डालें।
* हल्के हाथ से mix करें ताकि tadka evenly distribute हो जाए और chutney की texture खराब न हो।
