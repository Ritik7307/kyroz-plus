# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## FRESH VEGETABLE PREPARATION

* Cut **Bottle Gourd (Lauki)**, **Drumstick (Sahjan)**, and **Onion (Pyaaz)** into large, uniform pieces.
* Keep the vegetable pieces reasonably consistent in size for even cooking.

## BOILING RULE

* Boil the vegetables separately in a clean vessel using **RO water**.
* Cook until the vegetables are tender but still retain their shape.

## CRITICAL RULE — NO ADDITIONAL SALT

**DO NOT ADD SALT while boiling the vegetables.**

* All required salt is already included in **S-308 LENTIL LAVA**.
* Do not add additional salt during vegetable preparation.

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## 1. WATER HEATING

* Transfer the required quantity of RO water into a large cooking vessel.
* Heat the water until it is **warm/hot**, but avoid unnecessary vigorous boiling at this stage.

## 2. S-308 LENTIL LAVA BLENDING

* Slowly add **S-308 LENTIL LAVA** to the warm water.
* Whisk continuously until the powder is fully dispersed.
* Ensure there are **no visible lumps**.

## 3. VEGETABLE ADDITION

* Add the previously boiled fresh vegetables to the prepared sambhar base.
* Mix gently to distribute the vegetables evenly.

## 4. SIMMERING

* Simmer the sambhar on **medium heat for 10–12 minutes**.
* Stir periodically to prevent settling and ensure the lentil powder and spices are evenly integrated.

## 5. CONSISTENCY CHECK

* If the sambhar becomes too thick, add a small quantity of **boiling/hot RO water** and mix thoroughly.
* **Never add cold water directly to hot sambhar.**

---

# 4. FRESH TADKA

## MANDATORY STEP

Fresh tadka must be added after the sambhar has completed simmering.

### TADKA PREPARATION

* Heat **2 teaspoons oil or ghee** in a small pan.
* Add:

  * **Mustard Seeds (Rai)**
  * **2–3 Whole Red Chillies**
  * **10–12 Fresh Curry Leaves**
* Allow the mustard seeds to splutter.
* Pour the hot tadka into the prepared sambhar.
* Immediately cover the vessel with a lid to help retain the aroma.

---

# 5. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                                        | Cause                                                                       | Solution                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sambhar is too salty**                     | Too little water was used or salt was accidentally added to the vegetables. | Add a controlled quantity of **boiling RO water** and, if required, a small amount of **jaggery** to balance the flavour. |
| **Sambhar tastes too weak / under-seasoned** | Excess water was added.                                                     | Simmer for an additional **5–7 minutes** to reduce the liquid and concentrate the flavour.                                |
| **Lumps are present**                        | S-308 LENTIL LAVA was added to cold water or was not whisked properly.      | Use a suitable hand blender to smooth the mixture, or strain the sambhar if required.                                     |
| **Taste is bland / not developed**           | Insufficient simmering time.                                                | Simmer for at least **10 minutes** and allow the lentil and spice profile to integrate properly.                          |

---

# 6. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## PORTIONING

* Maintain a consistent **vegetable-to-sambhar ratio** for every customer.
* Vegetables should be distributed evenly rather than settling at the bottom of the vessel.

## HYGIENE

* Keep sambhar **covered whenever it is not being actively served or prepared**.
* Use clean ladles and utensils during service.

## NO EXTRA SALT

* Do not keep an open salt container on the kitchen counter.
* This reduces the risk of accidental additional salt being added during service.

# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## तैयारी

### FRESH VEGETABLE PREPARATION

* **लौकी (Lauki)**, **सहजन/Drumstick**, और **प्याज** को बड़े और लगभग समान आकार के टुकड़ों में काटें।
* सभी vegetables का size लगभग समान रखें ताकि वे evenly cook हों।

### BOILING RULE

* Vegetables को एक अलग साफ vessel में **RO Water** के साथ boil करें।
* Vegetables इतने soft होने चाहिए कि आसानी से पक जाएं लेकिन उनका shape पूरी तरह खत्म न हो।

### CRITICAL RULE — NAMAK बिल्कुल नहीं डालना है

**Vegetables boil करते समय NAMAK/SALT बिल्कुल नहीं डालना है।**

* Required salt पहले से **S-308 LENTIL LAVA** में मौजूद है।
* Vegetable preparation के दौरान कोई additional salt नहीं डालना है।

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## Sambhar बनाने की विधि

### 1. WATER HEATING

* Required quantity में RO water को एक बड़े cooking vessel में डालें।
* पानी को **हल्का गर्म/गरम** करें।
* इस stage पर जरूरत से ज्यादा तेज उबाल न दें।

### 2. S-308 LENTIL LAVA BLENDING

* Warm water में धीरे-धीरे **S-308 LENTIL LAVA** डालें।
* साथ-साथ whisk से लगातार mix करते रहें।
* Powder पूरी तरह पानी में disperse होना चाहिए।
* **कोई visible lumps नहीं रहनी चाहिए।**

### 3. VEGETABLE ADDITION

* पहले से boiled fresh vegetables को तैयार sambhar base में डालें।
* हल्के हाथ से mix करें ताकि vegetables evenly distribute हो जाएं।

### 4. SIMMERING

* Sambhar को **medium heat पर 10–12 मिनट** तक simmer करें।
* बीच-बीच में हल्के हाथ से चलाते रहें ताकि powder नीचे settle न हो और lentil तथा spices अच्छी तरह integrate हो जाएं।

### 5. CONSISTENCY CHECK

* अगर sambhar बहुत गाढ़ा हो जाए तो थोड़ी मात्रा में **उबलता/गरम RO water** डालकर mix करें।
* **Hot sambhar में cold water सीधे नहीं डालना है।**

# 4. FRESH TADKA

## MANDATORY STEP

Sambhar का simmering पूरा होने के बाद fresh tadka लगाना जरूरी है।

### TADKA PREPARATION

* एक छोटे pan में **2 teaspoon oil या ghee** गर्म करें।
* इसमें डालें:

  * **Rai / Mustard Seeds**
  * **2–3 Sabut Laal Mirch**
  * **10–12 Fresh Curry Leaves**
* Rai को अच्छी तरह चटकने दें।
* तैयार hot tadka को sambhar में डालें।
* तुरंत vessel को ढक्कन से cover कर दें ताकि tadka की aroma अंदर retain रहे।
