# KYROZ+ SOUTH INDIAN DAILY OPERATIONAL CHART

## 1. MORNING SETUP & PRE-PREP

### 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM

As soon as the helper enters the kitchen, the following three activities must be completed first.

---

## A. BATTER HYDRATION

### Dosa & Idli

* Prepare the batter according to the respective SOP.
* Allow the prepared batter to rest for **20 minutes** before service or further preparation.

### Chutneys

* Prepare **Coconut Chutney** and **Red Kara Chutney** according to their respective SOPs.
* Keep prepared chutneys refrigerated until required for service.

### Medu Vada

* Prepare the Medu Vada batter according to the approved SOP.
* Whisk/beat the batter properly.
* Keep the prepared batter refrigerated.
* **Cold batter helps the Medu Vada maintain better shape during preparation.**

---

# B. VEGETABLE CUTTING

## STANDARD SIZES

In a chef-less kitchen, vegetable preparation should be completed in one organized batch instead of repeated cutting throughout the day.

### Sambhar

* Cut **Bottle Gourd (Lauki)**, **Drumstick (Sahjan)**, and **Onion** into large pieces of approximately **2 inches**.
* Boil the vegetables according to the Sambhar SOP.
* Cool appropriately and store under refrigeration in clean, covered containers.

### Aloo Masala

* Boil the potatoes.
* Peel and lightly mash them.
* Keep them ready for Aloo Masala preparation.

### Toppings — Uttapam / Rava Dosa

Prepare one **Mix-Veg Topping Bowl** containing:

* Finely chopped Onion
* Finely chopped Tomato
* Finely chopped Green Chilli
* Finely chopped Fresh Coriander

Keep covered and refrigerated until required.

---

# C. THE TADKA STATION

Maintain one dedicated tadka station containing the following items:

* **Rai / Mustard Seeds**
* **Whole Red Chillies**
* **Fresh Curry Leaves**
* **Cooking Oil / Ghee**
* **Split Urad Dal**

All essential tadka ingredients must be replenished before service.

---

# 3. STANDARD DAILY SCHEDULE

### 10:30 AM — QUALITY CHECK

Perform:

* Batter **Salt Check**
* Batter **Consistency Check**
* Chutney **Salt Check**
* Chutney **Consistency Check**

**Important:** No additional salt should be added where the respective premix SOP specifies that salt is already included in the premix.

### 11:30 AM — PRE-LUNCH PREPARATION

Before the lunch rush:

* Prepare the first fresh batch of **Sambhar**.
* Prepare the first fresh batch of **Aloo Masala**.
* Ensure all service stations are fully stocked.

### 4:00 PM — EVENING SNACK PREPARATION

* Prepare fresh **Medu Vada batter** as required.
* Refill dosa/idli batter stations.
* Check chutney and topping quantities.
* Replenish tadka ingredients.

### 9:30 PM — CLOSING PROCEDURE

At closing:

1. Transfer remaining approved batter to refrigeration promptly.
2. Perform **deep cleaning of the dosa tawa and surrounding station**.
3. Clean and sanitize work surfaces and utensils.
4. Check premix inventory for the following day's operation.
5. Record low-stock items for replenishment.

# 4. OWNER'S QUALITY AUDIT

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN CHECKLIST

Kitchen performance can be audited through four simple checks.

### 1. THE SOUND TEST — TAWA HEAT

* Sprinkle a small amount of water on the tawa.
* Listen for the characteristic **"shhh"** sound.
* Use this as a quick indication that the tawa has reached the required cooking temperature.

### 2. THE VISUAL TEST — PRODUCT QUALITY

Check:

* Sambhar should have a consistent, appetizing colour.
* Coconut Chutney should retain its expected light/white appearance.
* Red Kara Chutney should retain its expected red colour.
* Dosa, Rava Dosa and Uttapam should have their specified colour and texture.

### 3. THE SMELL TEST — FRESHNESS

* Fresh **curry leaves and mustard tadka** should produce a noticeable aroma during service.
* Unusual sour, stale, burnt or off-odours should be investigated immediately.

### 4. WASTE CHECK — PORTION CONTROL

At the end of the day, check:

* Quantity of unused batter
* Quantity of unused chutney
* Vegetable waste
* Other discarded food

Excessive waste indicates that production quantities or portion control need adjustment.

---

# 5. SPECIAL CHEF-LESS HACK

## THE MEASUREMENT CUP SYSTEM

Helpers should **never rely on अंदाज़ / guesswork** for water measurement.

Keep standardized measuring cups/jugs in the kitchen:

* **250 ml**
* **500 ml**
* **1 Litre**

## STANDARD RULE

**1 Premix Packet = Standardized Quantity of Water**

The exact water quantity must always be taken from the **respective product SOP**.

### Example

If a particular SOP specifies:

**1 KG Premix = 2.5 Litres Water**

then use:

**2 × 1 Litre + 2 × 250 ml = 2.5 Litres**

This ensures the same water-to-premix ratio every time.

---

# DAILY GOLDEN RULES

1. **Measure — Don't Guess.**
2. **Follow the individual product SOP.**
3. **Do not add extra salt when salt is already included in the premix.**
4. **Keep prepared food covered and refrigerated when required.**
5. **Use fixed ladle sizes for portion control.**
6. **Keep every station stocked before rush hours.**
7. **Record waste and low-stock items every day.**
8. **Clean as you work and deep-clean at closing.**
