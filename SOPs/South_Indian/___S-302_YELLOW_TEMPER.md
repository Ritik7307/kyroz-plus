# KYROZ+ OPERATIONAL MANUAL

## ALOO MASALA (STUFFING)

**Document Code:** KY/SOP/ALU-03
**Category:** South Indian

---

# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## POTATO PREPARATION

* Boil **1 KG potatoes**, peel them, and keep them ready for preparation.
* Ensure the potatoes are properly cooked but not excessively watery.

## MASHING

* Lightly break/mash the boiled potatoes by hand.
* Do **not** turn the potatoes into a completely smooth paste.

### CRITICAL RULE

* Small potato pieces should remain in the mixture.
* This provides the required texture when the dosa is eaten.

## NO-SALT RULE

**DO NOT ADD SALT while boiling the potatoes.**

* All required salt is already included in **Kyroz S-302 YELLOW TEMPER**.
* No additional salt should be added during preparation.

---

# 2. QUANTITY CHART

## MEASUREMENT

Always follow the standardized ratio to maintain consistent taste and texture.

| Boiled Potatoes | S-302 YELLOW TEMPER |   RO Water |         Cooking Oil |
| --------------: | ------------------: | ---------: | ------------------: |
|        **1 KG** |       **120 Grams** | **150 ml** | **2–3 Tablespoons** |
|   **500 Grams** |        **60 Grams** |  **75 ml** | **1.5 Tablespoons** |
|   **250 Grams** |        **30 Grams** |  **40 ml** |    **1 Tablespoon** |

---

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## 1. HEAT OIL

* Heat the required quantity of cooking oil in a clean kadai according to the measurement chart.

## 2. SAUTÉ S-302 YELLOW TEMPER

* Add **S-302 YELLOW TEMPER** to the hot oil.
* Stir for only **30–40 seconds**.

### Important

* Do not burn the seasoning.
* The mustard seeds should begin to splutter and the dal should become lightly golden brown.

## 3. ADD WATER

* Immediately add the required quantity of RO water.
* Once water is added, the dehydrated onion and curry leaves will hydrate and the mixture will develop into a thick masala base.

## 4. ADD POTATOES

* Add the lightly mashed boiled potatoes.
* Mix thoroughly so that the seasoning and masala coat the potatoes evenly.

## 5. FINISHING

* Cook on **low heat for approximately 2 minutes**.
* Switch off the heat after the mixture reaches the required semi-solid stuffing consistency.

**Optional:**
If fresh coriander is available, finely chop and add a small quantity before finishing.

---

# 4. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

| Issue                                       | Cause                                                              | Solution                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Masala is too dry**                       | Insufficient water remained in the mixture.                        | Add **2–3 teaspoons warm RO water** and adjust to the required soft consistency.                                    |
| **Masala tastes too salty**                 | Potato quantity was insufficient for the amount of seasoning used. | Add **1–2 additional boiled potatoes**, lightly mash them, and mix thoroughly.                                      |
| **Masala has burnt**                        | Oil was excessively hot or the seasoning was cooked for too long.  | In the next batch, add the seasoning as soon as the oil is hot and add water promptly after the 30–40 second sauté. |
| **Masala does not spread properly on dosa** | Stuffing is too firm/thick.                                        | Add a small amount of water and mash/mix until the stuffing becomes **semi-solid and easily spreadable**.           |

---

# 5. HELPER ADVICE

## CHEF-LESS KITCHEN

## FRESHNESS

* Prepare only the quantity of Aloo Masala expected to be consumed within approximately **2–3 hours**.
* Older potato masala may lose its desired taste and texture.

## STORAGE

* If any masala remains, transfer it to a clean **airtight food-grade container** and refrigerate promptly.
* Follow the restaurant's approved food-safety holding period.

## CONSISTENCY

* The stuffing should be **soft and semi-solid**.
* It must spread easily over the dosa without tearing or damaging the dosa surface.

---

# KEY OPERATIONAL STANDARDS

* **Product:** S-302 YELLOW TEMPER
* **Additional Salt:** **NONE**
* **1 KG Potatoes:** 120 G S-302 + 150 ml RO Water + 2–3 tbsp Oil
* **500 G Potatoes:** 60 G S-302 + 75 ml RO Water + 1.5 tbsp Oil
* **250 G Potatoes:** 30 G S-302 + 40 ml RO Water + 1 tbsp Oil
* **Seasoning Sauté Time:** 30–40 seconds
* **Final Cooking:** Approximately 2 minutes on low heat
* **Texture:** Semi-solid and easily spreadable
* **Potato Texture:** Lightly mashed with small pieces remaining

# 1. PRE-COOKING PREPARATION

## तैयारी

### POTATO PREPARATION

* **1 KG आलू** को अच्छी तरह boil करके peel कर लें और preparation के लिए ready रखें।
* आलू पूरी तरह cooked होने चाहिए लेकिन उनमें जरूरत से ज्यादा पानी नहीं होना चाहिए।

### MASHING

* उबले हुए आलू को हाथ से हल्के-हल्के तोड़कर mash करें।
* आलू को पूरी तरह smooth paste में convert नहीं करना है।

### CRITICAL RULE

* आलू के छोटे-छोटे pieces mixture में रहने चाहिए।
* इससे dosa खाते समय proper potato texture मिलता है।

### NO-SALT RULE

**आलू boil करते समय NAMAK/SALT बिल्कुल नहीं डालना है।**

* Required salt पहले से **Kyroz S-302 YELLOW TEMPER** में शामिल है।
* Preparation के दौरान कोई additional salt नहीं डालना है।

# 3. COOKING STEPS

## Aloo Masala बनाने की विधि

### 1. HEAT OIL

* Measurement chart के अनुसार required quantity में cooking oil को साफ kadai में गर्म करें।

### 2. SAUTÉ S-302 YELLOW TEMPER

* गरम oil में **S-302 YELLOW TEMPER** डालें।
* केवल **30–40 seconds** तक हल्के हाथ से चलाएं।

### IMPORTANT

* Seasoning को जलने नहीं देना है।
* Rai चटकना शुरू होनी चाहिए और dal हल्की golden brown होनी चाहिए।

### 3. ADD WATER

* तुरंत required quantity में RO water डालें।
* पानी डालने के बाद dehydrated onion और curry leaves hydrate होकर एक thick masala base बनाएंगे।

### 4. ADD POTATOES

* हल्के mashed boiled potatoes को kadai में डालें।
* अच्छी तरह mix करें ताकि seasoning और masala पूरे आलू पर evenly coat हो जाए।

### 5. FINISHING

* **Low heat पर लगभग 2 मिनट** तक पकाएं।
* Required semi-solid stuffing consistency आने के बाद gas बंद कर दें।

**Optional:**
अगर kitchen में fresh coriander उपलब्ध है तो थोड़ा बारीक कटा हुआ हरा धनिया ऊपर से डाल सकते हैं।
