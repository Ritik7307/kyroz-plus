KYROZ+ OPERATIONAL MANUAL
SOP: INDO-ARABIC WHITE MANDI
MASTER METHOD

Batch Size: 1 KG Chicken/Mutton + 1 KG Rice
Kit: B-404 A (Meat Marination) + B-404 B (White Rice Mix)
Category: Indo-Arabic


1. PREPARATION

MEAT PREPARATION

• Clean 1 KG Chicken/Mutton properly and make deep cuts on the meat.
• Cuts should be deep enough for the marinade to penetrate properly.

RICE PREPARATION

• Soak 1 KG Long Grain Basmati Rice for 30 minutes.
• If using Sella Rice, soak it for 1–1.5 hours.
• After soaking, drain the rice properly and keep it ready.

FRESH INGREDIENTS

• Ginger-Garlic Paste: 40 G
• Fresh Lemon: 2
• Green Chillies: 5–6
• Onion: 2, finely chopped
• Cooking Oil: As required


2. MEAT MARINATION
B-404 A

1. In a clean mixing bowl, add:
   • B-404 A
   • 40 G Ginger-Garlic Paste
   • Juice of 2 Fresh Lemons
   • 30 ML Cooking Oil

2. Mix all ingredients properly to make a smooth marinade.

3. Rub the marinade evenly over the prepared meat.

4. Make sure the marinade reaches inside the deep cuts.

5. Cover and marinate under refrigeration:

   • Chicken: Minimum 1 Hour
   • Mutton: Minimum 2 Hours


3. STEAM & STOCK
MEAT COOKING METHOD

1. Boil approximately 2–3 Litres of water in a large cooking vessel.

2. Place a suitable steamer/channi over the vessel.

3. Place the marinated meat inside the steamer.

4. Cover properly and cook on low heat.

5. Steam until the meat becomes tender:

   • Chicken: Approximately 30–45 Minutes
   • Mutton: Approximately 1–1.5 Hours

6. Check the meat periodically during cooking.

7. CRITICAL STOCK RULE:

   Do NOT discard the liquid/juices collected from the meat during
   steaming.

   This liquid is the primary STOCK for cooking the Mandi Rice.

8. If the collected stock is insufficient, use hot RO water to complete
   the required rice-cooking quantity.


4. RICE COOKING
B-404 B

1. Heat 150 ML cooking oil in a separate large vessel.

2. Add finely chopped onions.

3. Cook the onions only until soft and lightly pink.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT brown or burn the onions.

4. Add B-404 B WHITE RICE MIX.

5. Lightly sauté for only 10–15 seconds.

6. Immediately add the measured meat STOCK.

WATER/STOCK RATIO:

• For 1 KG Rice, use approximately 2 Litres total water/stock.
• Measure the available meat stock first.
• Complete the remaining quantity with hot RO water if required.

7. Once the stock begins to boil, add:

   • 5–6 Split Green Chillies
   • Soaked and drained Rice

8. Cook until most of the liquid is absorbed.

9. Once the liquid is almost completely absorbed, reduce the heat.

10. Cover and give approximately 15 Minutes Dum.

11. After dum, gently fluff the rice.

IMPORTANT:
Do not aggressively mix the rice as this can break the grains.


5. FINISHING & PRESENTATION
ROYAL LOOK

MEAT FRY

1. Transfer the steamed and tender meat to a suitable pan.

2. Add a small quantity of oil.

3. Fry for approximately 2 Minutes.

4. The outside should become lightly crispy with the desired orange finish.

IMPORTANT:
Do not over-fry the meat, otherwise the inside may become dry.


SERVING

1. Place the prepared White Mandi Rice on the serving platter.

2. Arrange the Orange Meat on top of the rice.

3. Maintain a clean and premium presentation.


DHUNGAR / SMOKE FINISH

1. Place a small piece of burning charcoal in a heat-safe container.

2. Add a small quantity of ghee over the charcoal.

3. Carefully place the smoking setup in the serving dish.

4. Cover the dish for approximately 5 Minutes.

5. Carefully remove the cover and serve immediately.

IMPORTANT:
Keep burning charcoal safely contained and away from direct customer
contact. Follow proper ventilation and fire-safety practices.


SIDES & GARNISH

Serve with:

• Red Chutney / Sahawek
• Fresh Kachumbar Salad
• Roasted Almonds
• Raisins
• Barista / Crispy Fried Onion
• Dried Lemon / Black Lemon aroma as per approved presentation standard


6. TROUBLESHOOTING
HELPER TIPS

PROBLEM: RICE COLOUR IS YELLOW OR DULL

CAUSE:
• Onion was browned too much.
• Stock contains excessive colour or impurities.

SOLUTION:
• Cook onions only until soft/light pink.
• Strain the stock before using.
• If required, adjust the final cooking quantity with clear hot RO water.


PROBLEM: MEAT IS HARD

CAUSE:
• Meat was not steamed for sufficient time.
• Meat tenderness was not checked.

SOLUTION:
• Give additional steaming time until the meat becomes properly tender.
• Follow separate cooking times for Chicken and Mutton.


PROBLEM: RICE SEASONING IS NOT BALANCED

CHECK:
• Taste the cooking liquid/stock before adding rice.
• The cooking liquid should have a sufficiently seasoned profile because
  the rice will absorb and dilute the flavour.

IMPORTANT:
• Do not automatically add extra salt.
• If additional salt is required according to the approved B-404 product
  specification, adjust only in the approved quantity.


7. MANDI DISH PRESENTATION
QUALITY CHECKLIST

[ ] RICE:
    Are the rice grains separate and maintaining the required white colour?

[ ] MEAT:
    Is the meat lightly crispy outside and juicy/tender inside?

[ ] AROMA:
    Is the desired charcoal smoke and dried lemon aroma present?

[ ] SIDES:
    Is the Red Chutney/Sahawek fresh and appropriately spicy?

[ ] GARNISH:
    Are roasted almonds, raisins and barista evenly presented?

[ ] PRESENTATION:
    Is the Orange Meat cleanly and neatly arranged over the White Rice?
