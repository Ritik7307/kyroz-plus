# KYROZ PLUS MASTER SOP

## VEG TANDOOR SERIES

### Batch Standard: 10 Portions

**Applicable Codes:**

* **T-601 CLASSIC CHAR** — Tandoori Paneer / Chaap
* **T-602 WHITE VELVET** — Malai Paneer / Chaap
* **T-603 VERDANT RUB** — Hariyali Paneer / Chaap
* **T-602-A ACHARI TWIST** — Instant Variant via White Velvet Base

---

# STAGE 1: MORNING SHIFT — BULK LIQUID PASTE PREPARATION

Kitchen staff must prepare the master liquid paste at the beginning of the morning shift according to the standard weights below and store it in the chilled storage area.

The liquid base ratio remains the **same for T-601, T-602, and T-603**, while the respective premix determines the final flavour profile.

## STANDARD BULK RATIO

### FOR T-601, T-602 & T-603 — INDIVIDUALLY

* **Target Premix Powder:** 1 Packet — 120 grams
* **Thick Hung Curd:** 300 grams
* **Fresh Cream:** 150 grams
* **Mustard Oil:** 50 ml

## PREPARATION METHOD

### 1. Prepare the Liquid Base

In a clean stainless-steel bowl, add:

* 300 grams beaten/hung curd
* 150 grams fresh cream
* 50 ml mustard oil

Whisk thoroughly until the mixture is completely blended.

### 2. Add the Premix

* Add **1 packet / 120 grams** of the relevant premix:

  * T-601 CLASSIC CHAR, or
  * T-602 WHITE VELVET, or
  * T-603 VERDANT RUB
* Mix thoroughly to create a **smooth, lump-free paste**.

### 3. Chilled Storage

* Store each prepared paste in a separate, properly covered container.
* Keep at **2–4°C** in the chilled storage/counter.

### 4. Yield Standard

* One complete batch of paste is standardized for **10 portions**.
* Approximately **60 grams of paste per portion** should be used during live order execution.

---

# STAGE 2: BASE ITEM PRE-PREP & CUTTING STANDARD

## 1. Paneer Cutting

* Use exactly **150 grams raw paneer per portion**.
* Cut into **6 equal square pieces**.
* Each piece should weigh approximately **25 grams**.

## 2. Soya Chaap Preparation

* Thaw frozen chaap first in normal water.
* Fry the chaap lightly in oil **with the stick still inserted** until the outer layer begins to open slightly.
* After frying, remove the stick.
* Cut the chaap into approximately **1.5-inch round pieces**.
* **Critical Step:** Gently press the pieces by hand to remove excess internal water and oil.

## 3. Mushroom Preparation

* Use **150 grams button mushrooms per portion**.
* Trim the bottom stems.
* Blanch in hot water for **2 minutes**.
* Drain and dry thoroughly before use.

## 4. Veg Momos Preparation

* Use **6 pieces of momos per portion**.
* Deep-fry the momos.
* Allow them to cool to **room temperature** before live order coating.

---

# STAGE 3: LIVE ORDER EXECUTION FLOW

## PER PORTION

### 1. KOT Execution

When the KOT arrives:

* Add **150 grams of the selected base item** — Paneer / Chaap / applicable vegetable item — to a mixing bowl.
* Add the required diced onions and capsicum.

### 2. ACHARI CUSTOMIZATION — T-602-A

For an **Achari order**:

* First take **60 grams of T-602 WHITE VELVET base paste** into the mixing bowl.
* Add **15 ml smooth mango pickle paste** kept separately.
* Mix thoroughly at the time of order to create the instant Achari variant.

For all other orders, directly use the respective prepared paste from the morning batch.

### 3. Coating

* Add **60 grams of the prepared paste** to the base item.
* Gently massage/coat the pieces.
* Handle carefully to prevent paneer, chaap, or momos from breaking.

### 4. Skewering & Roasting

* Thread the coated items onto square skewers along with the required vegetable combination.
* Place in a **220–240°C tandoor**.
* Roast for approximately **6–8 minutes**.
* Remove once midway and brush with melted butter.
* Return for the final roast.
* Remove when the edges develop **light charred spots** without over-burning the coating.

---

# STAGE 4: TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

## CRITICAL ERRORS

### PROBLEM 1: Masala is sliding off the skewer and the tikka comes out dry

**Root Cause:**

* Excess moisture remained in the curd because the curd was not properly drained/thickened.
* Proper hung/chakka curd was not used.

**Solution:**

* During the live order, add **½ teaspoon roasted gram flour (bhuna besan)** to the mixing bowl.
* Mix immediately to thicken the coating.

---

### PROBLEM 2: Achari Tikka splits immediately after entering the tandoor

**Root Cause:**

* The pickle paste contains excessive oil/vinegar, causing the mixture to separate under high heat.

**Solution:**

* Always **fine-blend the pickle paste into a smooth emulsion** before mixing it with the curd/White Velvet base.

---

# STAGE 5: FINAL PRESENTATION & PORTIONING

## PLATING STANDARD

* A full plate must contain **exactly 6 pieces**.
* For Soya Chaap:

  * Remove the chaap from the skewer.
  * Cut it diagonally on the cutting board in a **1:2 diagonal proportion/style**.
  * This creates a fuller and more attractive plate presentation.

---

# THE FINISHER BOWL

Immediately after removing the items from the tandoor, transfer them into a mixing bowl.

## T-601 CLASSIC CHAR & T-603 VERDANT RUB

Add:

* **1 tablespoon Melted Butter**
* **½ teaspoon Chaat Masala**
* **1 teaspoon Lemon Juice**

Gently toss to coat.

## T-602 WHITE VELVET & T-602-A ACHARI TWIST

Add:

* **1 tablespoon Fresh Cream**
* **1 tablespoon Melted Butter**

Gently shake/toss.

**Important:**
Do **not** add chaat masala to the T-602-A Achari variant.

---

# PLATE LAYOUT

Use a **rectangular black or copper plate**.

### Center

* Arrange the pieces neatly in a clean stacked line.

### Sides

Add:

* **30 grams Mint Lachha Onion**
* **1 Fresh Lemon Wedge**

### Chutney

Serve **30 ml chilled green mint chutney** separately.

### Momos — COMPULSORY DIP

For Momos, serve **20 ml spicy momo chutney separately**.

### T-601, T-602 और T-603 — प्रत्येक के लिए

* **Target Premix Powder:** 1 Packet — 120 ग्राम

* प्रत्येक तैयार paste को अलग-अलग properly covered container में रखें।

### 4. Yield Standard

* एक complete batch paste **10 portions** के लिए standardized है।
* Live order पर लगभग **60 ग्राम paste प्रति portion** इस्तेमाल करना है।

# STAGE 2: BASE ITEM PRE-PREP & CUTTING STANDARD

## 1. Paneer Cutting

* प्रत्येक portion के लिए exactly **150 ग्राम raw paneer** लें।
* इसे **6 बराबर square pieces** में काटें।
* प्रत्येक piece लगभग **25 ग्राम** का होना चाहिए।

## 2. Soya Chaap Preparation

* Frozen chaap को पहले normal पानी में thaw करें।
* Stick लगे हुए chaap को oil में हल्का fry करें, जब तक उसकी outer layer थोड़ी खुलने न लगे।
* Frying के बाद stick निकाल दें।
* Chaap को लगभग **1.5 inch के गोल pieces** में काटें।
* **Critical Step:** Pieces को हाथ से हल्के से दबाकर उनके अंदर का excess पानी और तेल निकाल दें।

## 3. Mushroom Preparation

* प्रत्येक portion के लिए **150 ग्राम button mushrooms** लें।
* Bottom stems काट दें।
* गर्म पानी में **2 मिनट blanch** करें।
* अच्छी तरह drain करके dry कर लें।

## 4. Veg Momos Preparation

* प्रत्येक portion के लिए **6 pieces momos** लें।
* Momos को deep-fry करें।
* Live order में इस्तेमाल करने से पहले उन्हें **room temperature तक ठंडा** होने दें।

# STAGE 3: LIVE ORDER EXECUTION FLOW

## PER PORTION

### 1. KOT Execution

KOT आते ही:

* Mixing bowl में **150 ग्राम selected base item** — Paneer / Chaap / applicable vegetable item — डालें।
* Required diced onions और capsicum डालें।

### 2. ACHARI CUSTOMIZATION — T-602-A

अगर order **Achari** है:

* सबसे पहले mixing bowl में **60 ग्राम T-602 WHITE VELVET base paste** लें।
* इसमें अलग रखे हुए **15 ml smooth mango pickle paste** को डालें।
* Order के समय दोनों को अच्छी तरह mix करके instant Achari variant तैयार करें।

बाकी सभी normal orders के लिए सुबह तैयार किया गया संबंधित paste सीधे इस्तेमाल करें।

### 3. Coating

* Base item में **60 ग्राम तैयार paste** डालें।
* हल्के हाथ से massage करके सभी pieces को अच्छी तरह coat करें।
* Paneer, chaap या momos टूटने नहीं चाहिए।

### 4. Skewering & Roasting

* Coated items को required vegetable combination के साथ square seekh पर पिरोएं।
* **220–240°C tandoor** में रखें।
* लगभग **6–8 मिनट** roast करें।
* बीच में एक बार निकालकर melted butter brush करें।
* Final roast के लिए दोबारा tandoor में रखें।
* Edges पर **हल्के charred spots** आते ही निकाल लें। Coating को ज्यादा burn नहीं करना है।

# STAGE 4: TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

## CRITICAL ERRORS

## PROBLEM 1: Masala seekh से बह रहा है और tikka सूखा निकल रहा है

**Root Cause:**

* Dahi का excess पानी ठीक से नहीं निकाला गया था।
* Proper hung/chakka dahi का इस्तेमाल नहीं हुआ।

**Solution:**

* Live order के mixing bowl में **½ teaspoon roasted besan (bhuna besan)** डालें।
* तुरंत mix करके coating को thick करें।

---

## PROBLEM 2: Achari Tikka tandoor में जाते ही split हो रहा है

**Root Cause:**

* Achar paste में oil/vinegar content ज्यादा है, जिसके कारण high heat पर mixture अलग हो रहा है।

**Solution:**

* Achar paste को हमेशा पहले **fine-blend करके smooth emulsion** बनाएं।
* इसके बाद ही उसे curd/White Velvet base के साथ mix करें।
