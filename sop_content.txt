## T-605 SILK INFUSION SERIES

### Batch Standard: 1 KG Raw Chicken

**Applicable Code:**

* **T-605 SILK INFUSION** — Afghani Chicken / Chicken Malai Tikka
* **Yield Standard:** 1 KG Raw Chicken Batch Scale — Kyroz 1 KG Packet Concept

---

## STAGE 1: INGREDIENT MEASUREMENT FOR 1 KG BATCH

Kitchen staff must strictly use a weighing scale and measure all ingredients according to the quantities below:

* **Target Premix:** 1 Packet T-605 SILK INFUSION
* **Raw Chicken:** 1 KG — Thigh Boneless or Bone-in Pieces
* **Thick Hung Curd (Chakka Dahi):** 80 grams
* **Fresh Cream:** 40 grams — Amul or equivalent
* **Melted Butter:** 20 grams
* **Ginger-Garlic Paste:** 20 grams — Fresh
* **Lemon Juice:** 5–8 grams

---

## STAGE 2: PRE-PREP & WASHING STANDARD

### 1. Raw Chicken Preparation

**For Chicken Malai Tikka:**

* Raw chicken must strictly consist of **30–35 gram thigh boneless pieces**.
* Thigh meat should be used because it remains juicy and tender in creamy preparations.

**For Afghani Chicken — Bone-in:**

* Use either **exactly 4 standard chicken pieces** or standard joint pieces.
* Make deep cuts/slits on the pieces so that the white marination can penetrate properly into the meat.

### 2. Washing Standard

* Wash the chicken thoroughly.
* Place the washed chicken in a **colander/jali for 15 minutes** so that all excess water drains completely.
* If the chicken remains wet, the white marination will become thin and will not coat the meat properly.

---

## STAGE 3: LIQUID MARINATION & HOLDING TIME

### 1. Prepare the Liquid Base

In a clean stainless-steel bowl, add:

* 80 grams thick hung curd
* 40 grams fresh cream
* 20 grams melted butter
* 20 grams ginger-garlic paste
* 5–8 grams lemon juice

Whisk thoroughly until the mixture is properly blended.

### 2. Add T-605 Premix

* Add **1 complete packet of T-605 SILK INFUSION powder** to the liquid mixture.
* Whisk properly to create a **smooth, rich, creamy white paste without lumps**.

### 3. Marinate the Chicken

* Add **1 KG chicken pieces** to the prepared paste.
* Gently massage and coat all pieces evenly with the marination.

### 4. Holding / Marination Time

* Cover the container with a lid or cling wrap.
* Store in a **deep refrigerator/chiller at 2–4°C**.
* Marinate for a **minimum of 12 hours or overnight**.
* Overnight marination allows the creamy malai flavour to penetrate deeper into the meat.

---

# STAGE 4: TWO ROASTING METHODS

## OPTION A: THE EXPRESS METHOD

### 70–80% PRE-COOK / COMMERCIAL RESTAURANT STYLE

**Target Use:**
Rush hours and fast service, allowing malai items to be served within approximately **5 minutes without excessive colour development**.

### 1. Morning Preparation Shift

* Remove the marinated chicken pieces from the chiller.
* Place the pieces on seekh.
* Roast in a **180°C tandoor for 8–10 minutes**.
* Do not allow the white marination to become dark.
* Remove the chicken once it is approximately **75% cooked**.

### 2. Holding Stage

* Remove the pre-cooked pieces from the seekh.
* Place them in a clean stainless-steel kitchen tray.
* Cover the tray and hold/store according to the restaurant's approved food-safety procedure.

### 3. Live Order Fire — 5-Minute Flash

When the KOT arrives:

* Apply the remaining T-605 liquid paste from the morning preparation to the pre-cooked pieces.
* Add a small touch of fresh cream.
* Place the pieces back on the seekh.
* Flash-fire in the tandoor for **3–4 minutes**.
* Brush with melted butter.
* Serve piping hot while maintaining the **white creamy glaze without excessive browning**.

---

## OPTION B: THE LIVE METHOD

### DIRECT COOK FROM RAW STOCK

**Target Use:**
Normal operating hours when the customer can wait for freshly cooked premium white meat.

### 1. Live Order Execution

* When the KOT arrives, remove the raw marinated pieces from the chiller.
* Place the pieces directly on the seekh.

### 2. Chicken Tikka Roasting

* Roast at **220°C in a medium-high heat tandoor for 6–7 minutes**.

### 3. Bone-in Afghani Chicken

* Roast at **180°C in a medium-low heat tandoor for approximately 15 minutes**.
* Cook slowly and evenly so the cream and butter do not burn while the chicken cooks thoroughly inside.

# STAGE 5: TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX

## SILK INFUSION — CRITICAL ERRORS

### PROBLEM 1: Afghani Chicken becomes yellow or excessively black

**Root Cause:**

* Tandoor temperature was too high, or
* Chicken was placed directly over excessively hot charcoal/flame.
* This can cause the cream and butter to burn.

**Solution:**

* At finishing/service, add **1 tablespoon fresh cold cream** in the finisher bowl and gently mix/coat the chicken to restore the creamy white appearance.

---

### PROBLEM 2: White masala slides off the tikka immediately inside the tandoor and the seekh comes out dry

**Root Cause:**

* Excess moisture in the cream or curd, or
* The marination became too thin during coating.

**Solution:**

* For a live order, add **½ teaspoon cashew powder or cornflour** to the bowl.
* Mix immediately until the paste becomes sufficiently thick for proper coating.

### Batch Standard: 1 Whole Chicken / 800–900 GM

**Applicable Code:**

* **T-607 ARABIAN SMOKE** — Al Faham Chicken
* **Yield Standard:** 1 Portion Whole Chicken Scale — Kyroz 1 Packet Concept

# STAGE 1: INGREDIENT MEASUREMENT FOR 1 PORTION

Kitchen staff must strictly use a weighing scale and measure all ingredients according to the quantities below:

* **Target Premix:** 1 Packet T-607 ARABIAN SMOKE
* **Raw Whole Chicken:** 1 Piece — With Skin or Without Skin, strictly **800–900 grams**
* **Thick Hung Curd (Chakka Dahi):** 125 grams
* **Fresh Lemon Juice:** 10 ml — approximately the juice of 2 whole lemons
* **Ginger-Garlic Paste (GG Paste):** 25 grams
* **Olive Oil:** 30 ml

---

# STAGE 2: AL FAHAM SPECIAL CUTTING & PRE-PREP STANDARD

The complete appearance and tender texture of Al Faham Chicken depend heavily on the cutting and preparation. Kitchen helpers must strictly follow the steps below.

## 1. THE BUTTERFLY CUT

* Clean the whole chicken properly.
* Cut the chicken open through the center from the **breastbone side** and flatten it completely.
* The chicken should remain connected through the **backbone**, creating one single flat piece.

## 2. MEAT HAMMERING / TAPPING

* Place the flattened chicken on a clean cutting board.
* Using a meat mallet/wooden hammer or the back of a rolling pin, gently tap the chicken.
* Pay special attention to the **breast area and leg joints**.
* This helps loosen/break the joints and ensures the chicken becomes evenly flat.

## 3. DEEP GASHES / CUTS

* Make deep cuts across the **legs, thighs, and breast**.
* These cuts allow heat and marinade to penetrate deeper toward the bone during grilling.

---

# STAGE 3: LIQUID PASTE MARINATION & FREEZER HOLDING

## 1. Prepare the Liquid Base

In a clean mixing bowl, add:

* 125 grams thick hung curd
* 25 grams ginger-garlic paste
* 10 ml fresh lemon juice
* 30 ml olive oil

Whisk thoroughly until all ingredients are properly blended.

## 2. Add T-607 Premix

* Add **1 complete packet of T-607 ARABIAN SMOKE powder** to the liquid mixture.
* Mix thoroughly to create a **smooth Arabian marinade paste**.

## 3. Apply the Marinade

* Place the butterfly-cut flattened chicken on a clean tray.
* Pour the prepared marinade over the chicken.
* Using your fingers, properly fill the marinade inside all the deep cuts.
* Ensure the entire chicken is evenly coated.

## 4. Freezer Holding

* Place the marinated chicken on a tray.
* Cover the tray tightly with plastic wrap.
* Place it in the freezer for a **minimum of 2 hours**.
* This allows the marinade to set firmly and helps soften the meat fibres.

---

# STAGE 4: TWO GRILLING METHODS

## OPTION A: THE EXPRESS METHOD

### 70–80% PRE-COOK / COMMERCIAL RESTAURANT STYLE

**Target Use:**
Rush hours and fast service, allowing an Al Faham order to reach the table in approximately **5 minutes**.

## 1. Morning Preparation Shift

* Remove the set/marinated chicken from the freezer.
* Lock the chicken securely inside an **Al Faham grill net/jali**.
* Place it on a charcoal grill/sigri over **low charcoal heat**.
* Pre-roast both sides for approximately **7–8 minutes**.
* The chicken should reach approximately **70% cooked**.

## 2. Holding Stage

* Remove the pre-cooked chicken from the grill net.
* Place it in a clean kitchen tray.
* Cover and hold/store according to the restaurant's approved food-safety procedure.

## 3. Live Order Fire — 5-Minute Flash

When the KOT arrives:

* Lightly brush the pre-cooked chicken with the remaining T-607 liquid paste.
* Add a light brush of extra olive oil.
* Lock the chicken inside the grill net again.
* Re-flash over a high-flame grill or open coal heat above the tandoor for **4–5 minutes**.
* Continue until the outer surface/skin becomes **crispy, smoky, and properly grilled**.

---

# OPTION B: THE LIVE METHOD

### DIRECT COOK FROM RAW STOCK

**Target Use:**
Slow hours or open-kitchen counters where customers can watch the chicken being freshly grilled.

## 1. Live Order Execution

* When the KOT arrives, remove the chilled marinated chicken from the freezer/chiller.
* Place the chicken directly inside the **Al Faham grill net**.

## 2. Roasting Time

* Grill over **low-medium charcoal heat**.
* Turn the chicken approximately every **3 minutes**.
* Continue slow grilling for a total of **20–22 minutes**.
* Brush with olive oil at intervals during grilling to prevent the chicken from becoming dry.

## AL FAHAM — CRITICAL ERRORS

### PROBLEM 1: Chicken is completely black/burnt outside but raw inside near the joints

**Root Cause:**

* Charcoal grill temperature was too high, or
* Breast/joint hammering was not performed properly, leaving thick areas around the joints.

**Solution:**

* Remove the chicken from the grill net.
* Make deeper cuts around the affected joints.
* Place the chicken over low charcoal embers or use the approved oven/microwave re-flash method for approximately **1 minute**.

---

### PROBLEM 2: Al Faham meat is dry, rubbery, and not soft while chewing

**Root Cause:**

* Chicken weight exceeded **900 grams**, or
* Regular olive-oil brushing was not performed during grilling.

**Solution:**

* Before serving, brush the pieces with extra **melted butter or olive oil**.
* Finish with a light squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
